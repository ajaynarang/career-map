## Class 9-10: Building the Foundation

This is when preparation really begins — not for exams specifically, but for the mindset and skills that will carry your child through.

### For all paths:
- **Master NCERT PCM thoroughly** — this is the foundation everything builds on
- **Start basic coding:** Python is the best starting language. Free resources: [freeCodeCamp](https://freecodecamp.org), [CS50 by Harvard](https://cs50.harvard.edu)
- **Maintain 85%+ in school** — boards matter for USA, UK, Germany, and Canada admissions
- **Explore interests:** Let your child join a robotics club, try competitive programming, or build a small project

### If targeting India (JEE path):
- Start **foundation courses** at coaching (Allen, FIITJEE, or PW). These cover Class 11 concepts early.
- Begin with **Olympiad preparation** (NSEP, RMO) — this builds the problem-solving muscle needed for JEE Advanced

### If targeting USA:
- Start building a **reading habit** — critical for SAT English and college essays
- Explore **extracurriculars with depth** — colleges want "spikes," not a list of 20 clubs
- Consider starting **SAT prep** in summer after Class 10

### If targeting Germany:
- **START GERMAN NOW.** This is the biggest bottleneck. Enroll at [Goethe-Institut](https://www.goethe.de/ins/in/en/index.html) or Max Mueller Bhavan. Goal: reach A2 by end of Class 10.
- German learning takes 1.5-2 years to reach B2 (the level most universities require)

## Class 11: The Critical Year

This is the most important year. The habits and knowledge built here determine everything.

### For India (JEE):
- **Join serious coaching.** Daily 5-6 hours of study (including coaching hours).
- Cover the **entire Class 11 syllabus** by March. Do NOT leave gaps — Class 12 builds on Class 11.
- Start solving **PYQs (Previous Year Questions)** topic-wise from January.
- Take coaching tests seriously — they simulate exam pressure.

### For USA:
- **Take the SAT** for the first time (aim 1400+). You can retake unlimited times — colleges see only best score.
- Build your **"spike" project** — one deep, impressive activity (research paper, app, community project, olympiad medal)
- Take **AP exams** in May if available (AP Calculus BC, AP Physics C are most relevant)
- Start **college list research** — understand the difference between Early Decision, Early Action, Regular Decision

### For Germany:
- **Reach German B1.** Take Goethe B1 exam.
- Keep 12th marks strong — German universities care about board percentage (85%+ recommended)

### For UK:
- Research **UCAS process** — UK applications are submitted October of Class 12
- Prepare for **personal statement** writing — UK cares about academic passion, not extracurriculars
- Consider taking **STEP/MAT/PAT** if targeting Cambridge/Oxford engineering

### For Canada & Australia:
- **Focus on grades** — both countries weight academic performance heavily
- Take **IELTS** if your English isn't your first language (target 7.0+)

## Class 12: Execution

Everything comes together. This is a sprint, but pace yourself.

### Month-by-month (India JEE path):
- **July-September:** Complete Class 12 syllabus at coaching. Revise Class 11.
- **October-December:** Full-length mock tests every week. Fix weak topics.
- **January:** JEE Main Session 1. Treat as serious practice.
- **February-March:** Board exams. Score 75%+ (mandatory for JEE eligibility).
- **April:** JEE Main Session 2. Go all-in for best score.
- **May:** JEE Advanced. Two papers, one day, everything on the line.
- **June-July:** JoSAA counselling. Fill choices carefully. Consult seniors.

### Parallel backup exams:
- **BITSAT** (May) — register by January at [bitsadmission.com](https://www.bitsadmission.com)
- **VITEEE** (April) — register by December at [vit.ac.in](https://vit.ac.in)
- **SRMJEEE** (April) — register at [srmist.edu.in](https://www.srmist.edu.in)

### For USA applicants:
- **August:** Common Application opens. Start essays.
- **October:** Final SAT if needed. Submit Early Decision/Early Action by November 1.
- **January:** Regular Decision deadline for most colleges.
- **March-April:** Decisions arrive. Compare financial aid packages carefully.

### For Germany applicants:
- **Score 85%+ in boards.** This is critical.
- **Take TestAS** (April/October) — good scores help compensate for lower grades.
- **Apply through [uni-assist](https://www.uni-assist.de)** — deadline July 15 for winter semester.
- German B2 should be complete by now.

## Smart Strategy: What the Best Students Do

1. **Don't put all eggs in one basket.** Apply to India (JEE) + at least one abroad option. The effort overlap is 70%+.
2. **Germany is the hidden gem:** Nearly free tuition, 18-month post-study work visa, excellent quality. But you MUST start German early.
3. **USA financial aid is real.** Stanford, MIT, and others meet 100% demonstrated need for international students. Don't self-reject based on fees.
4. **Coding is the #1 career multiplier.** Regardless of which college or branch your child gets, coding skills = better placements. Start now.
5. **Mental health first.** The competitive pressure is real. Make sure your child has hobbies, exercise, and downtime. A burned-out student performs worse than a balanced one.
