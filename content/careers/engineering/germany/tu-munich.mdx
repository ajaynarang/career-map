## Why TU Munich?

TU Munich (TUM) is Germany's #1 technical university and arguably the best engineering school in continental Europe. It combines German engineering excellence with an increasingly international outlook — TUM has more English-taught programs than almost any other German TU, making it the most accessible top German university for Indian students.

The location is a massive advantage: Munich is home to BMW, Siemens, Allianz, and the European headquarters of Google, Microsoft, and Amazon. Internship and job opportunities are abundant. Munich is also one of Europe's safest and most beautiful cities.

## How to Get In

TUM's admissions process for international students involves several steps:

1. **APS Certificate:** Indian students must get their academic credentials verified by the APS (Akademische Pruefstelle) at the German Embassy in New Delhi. This takes 2-4 months — start early
2. **Uni-assist application:** Submit your application through uni-assist.de or directly through TUM's portal (some programs use TUM's own system)
3. **Language proof:** TestDaF 4x4 or DSH-2 for German-taught programs. Some Bachelor's programs now offered in English require IELTS 6.5+
4. **Studienkolleg:** If your Indian 12th results don't meet direct admission criteria, you'll need to complete a 1-year Studienkolleg in Germany first

**Application deadlines:**
- Winter semester (October): Apply by May 31 (international) via uni-assist; July 15 for TUM's own portal
- Summer semester (April): Apply by January 15

**Reality check:** TUM is the most competitive German university. Strong grades (90%+ in 12th boards) and genuine German language ability are essential.

## Curriculum & Structure

Bachelor's degree is typically 3 years (6 semesters), though most students take 7-8 semesters in practice.

- **German-taught programs dominate** at the Bachelor's level — Maschinenwesen, Elektrotechnik, and Bauingenieurwesen are all in German
- **Informatik (CS) at TUM** is world-class — the department is one of the largest in Europe
- **Mandatory internships** (Pflichtpraktikum) are built into the curriculum — typically 8-12 weeks at companies like BMW, Siemens, or Bosch
- **Thesis at a company** — many students write their Bachelor's thesis at a company, getting paid and making industry connections
- **TUM has three campuses:** Munich city center (main), Garching (engineering/sciences — modern, suburban), and Weihenstephan (life sciences)

## Campus Life

TUM doesn't have a traditional "campus" in the Indian or American sense. Most engineering classes are at the **Garching campus**, 20 minutes north of Munich city center by U-Bahn (metro).

- **Indian community is growing** — Munich has a significant Indian student population. Indian Student Association Munich is active
- **Munich is expensive for Germany** — rent is €600-900/month for a shared apartment (WG). This is the main cost of studying here
- **Student life revolves around the city** — beer gardens, English Garden park, Alps day trips on weekends
- **Mensa (cafeteria)** — subsidized meals for €3-5. Most students eat here during the week
- **Public transport is excellent** — the Semesterticket gives unlimited public transport in Munich
- **Bavarian culture** — Oktoberfest, Christmas markets, hiking in the Alps. Munich is a uniquely livable city
- **Safety is excellent** — Munich is one of the safest cities in Europe
