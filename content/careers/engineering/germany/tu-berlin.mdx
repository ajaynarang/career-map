## Why TU Berlin?

TU Berlin gives you a top German engineering degree in one of Europe's most exciting cities. Berlin is the startup capital of Europe — home to over 500 tech startups, plus Tesla's European Gigafactory. For students who want engineering rigor combined with a vibrant, multicultural city life, TU Berlin is ideal.

Berlin is also the most affordable major capital in Western Europe. While Munich rents strain student budgets, Berlin offers shared apartments for €400-600/month, and the semester ticket covers ALL public transport in the entire city.

## How to Get In

TU Berlin's admissions follow the standard German process:

1. **APS Certificate** from the German Embassy
2. **Language proof** — German B2/C1 for most programs
3. **Uni-assist application** for most programs
4. Some programs have NC (restricted admission) with a waiting period

TU Berlin is generally more accessible than TUM or RWTH — the city's large capacity means more seats are available.

**Deadlines:**
- Winter semester: July 15 via uni-assist
- Summer semester: January 15

## Curriculum & Structure

Bachelor's: 3 years (6 semesters) officially, typically 7-8 semesters in practice.

- **Strong interdisciplinary focus** — TU Berlin encourages combining engineering with humanities, economics, or design
- **Innovation labs and makerspaces** — accessible to undergraduates
- **Startup ecosystem integration** — many students launch companies through the university's startup incubator
- **Research partnerships** with Fraunhofer, Helmholtz, and Leibniz institutes, all nearby in Berlin
- **Tesla partnership** — the Berlin Gigafactory creates internship and thesis opportunities for TU Berlin students

## Campus Life

TU Berlin's main campus is in Charlottenburg, a central and well-connected district of Berlin.

- **Berlin is incredible for young people** — nightlife, art, music, food, culture. It's the most cosmopolitan city in Germany
- **Indian community is large** — Berlin has 20,000+ Indians. Indian restaurants, grocery stores, and cultural events are everywhere
- **Very affordable for a capital city** — rent is €400-600/month in shared flats. Groceries and eating out are cheap
- **Multicultural** — Berlin is Germany's most diverse city. Your child will feel less like a foreigner here
- **The semester ticket is gold** — covers S-Bahn, U-Bahn, buses, and trams across all of Berlin. No transport costs
- **Social life is excellent** — but can be distracting. Berlin's attractions can pull students away from studies if they're not disciplined
