## Why TU Darmstadt?

TU Darmstadt is one of Germany's original technical universities (founded 1877) and the first in the world to establish an Electrical Engineering department. It's a TU9 member (Germany's top 9 technical universities) with particular strengths in Electrical Engineering, Computer Science, and Materials Science.

The location is strategic: Darmstadt sits in the Rhine-Main metropolitan area, close to Frankfurt (30 minutes by S-Bahn). This gives students access to the financial capital of Europe while living in an affordable, mid-sized city. Hesse state charges **no tuition** to international students — making TU Darmstadt significantly cheaper than universities in Baden-Württemberg.

## How to Get In

Standard German admissions process. TU Darmstadt is in Hesse, which means **zero tuition fees** for international students.

1. **APS Certificate** from the German Embassy
2. **German B2/C1** — Bachelor's programs are taught in German
3. Apply via TU Darmstadt's application portal
4. Some programs require an aptitude assessment (Eignungsfeststellungsverfahren)

**Deadlines:** Winter semester: July 15 | Summer semester: January 15

## Curriculum & Structure

Bachelor's: 3 years (6 semesters), typically 7-8 in practice.

- **Strong EE tradition** — as the birthplace of formal Electrical Engineering education, TU Darmstadt's EE department is world-class
- **Computational Engineering** — a unique interdisciplinary program combining CS, math, and engineering simulation
- **Industry thesis projects** — strong connections to Merck (headquartered in Darmstadt), Deutsche Telekom, and the European Space Agency (ESOC is in Darmstadt)
- **Research focus** — TU Darmstadt punches above its weight in research output, especially in cybersecurity, AI, and materials science
- **Flexible structure** — students have considerable freedom in choosing electives and specializations

## Campus Life

Darmstadt is a small city (160,000 people) with a strong scientific identity. The European Space Operations Centre (ESOC) and several Fraunhofer institutes are here.

- **Very affordable** — rent is €350-450/month for a shared flat. One of the cheapest university cities in western Germany
- **Close to Frankfurt** — 30 minutes by S-Bahn. Access to Frankfurt's international airport, cultural scene, and job market
- **Indian community** — moderate size, with Indian Student Association and cultural events
- **Quiet and focused** — Darmstadt is not a party city. It's ideal for students who want to focus on academics
- **Science city** — ESA, GSI (heavy ion research), Fraunhofer institutes — the scientific infrastructure is remarkable for a small city
- **Semester ticket covers the region** — travel to Frankfurt, Wiesbaden, and Mainz included
