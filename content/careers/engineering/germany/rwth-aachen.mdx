## Why RWTH Aachen?

RWTH Aachen is the "IIT Bombay of Germany" — it's the most prestigious engineering university in the country for traditional engineering disciplines like Mechanical Engineering, Electrical Engineering, and Materials Science. German industry respects an RWTH degree above almost anything else.

What makes RWTH special for Indian students: Aachen is a small, affordable city where your entire cost of attendance (including living) is about ₹10-11L/year. That's cheaper than many private Indian engineering colleges, for a globally top-40 engineering degree.

## How to Get In

RWTH's admissions are relatively straightforward compared to US universities:

1. **APS Certificate** from the German Embassy in New Delhi
2. **German language proof** — TestDaF 4x4 or DSH-2 (almost all Bachelor's programs are in German)
3. **Academic credentials** — strong 12th board results
4. **Studienkolleg** — may be required depending on your 12th board and subjects

**Key difference from TUM:** RWTH is slightly more accessible in terms of grades, but the German language requirement is strict — nearly all Bachelor's programs are exclusively in German.

**Deadlines:**
- Winter semester (October): July 15 via uni-assist
- Summer semester (April): January 15

## Curriculum & Structure

Bachelor's degree: 3 years (6 semesters), with most students taking 7-8 semesters.

- **Maschinenbau (Mechanical Engineering) is RWTH's crown jewel** — one of the largest and most respected ME programs in Europe
- **Strong lab and practical component** — German engineering education is hands-on. Expect significant lab work
- **Praxissemester (practical semester)** — mandatory industry internship in many programs
- **Tight integration with industry** — Aachen is surrounded by research institutions (Fraunhofer, DLR) and companies that actively recruit RWTH students
- **Formula Student team** — RWTH's student racing team is one of the best in the world

## Campus Life

Aachen is a small city (250,000 people) near the Dutch and Belgian borders. It's quintessentially German — medieval architecture, cobblestone streets, and a strong student culture.

- **Very affordable** — rent is €350-500/month for a shared flat (WG). Significantly cheaper than Munich or Berlin
- **Student city** — RWTH students make up a significant portion of Aachen's population. The city revolves around the university
- **Indian community exists** — smaller than Munich but active. Indian Student Association Aachen organizes regular events
- **Mensa is excellent** — RWTH has some of the best university canteens in Germany
- **Border location** — Netherlands is 20 minutes away. Belgium and Luxembourg are short trips. Weekend trips to Amsterdam, Brussels, or Paris are easy and cheap
- **Quiet social life** — Aachen is not Berlin. It's a focused, studious environment. If your child wants big-city excitement, look elsewhere
