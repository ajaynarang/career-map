## Why U of Alberta?

The University of Alberta is one of Canada's top research universities and a dominant force in energy, petroleum, mining, and AI engineering. Alberta is Canada's energy capital — the province produces more oil and gas than many countries — and U of A's engineering faculty has deep partnerships with the energy industry.

But U of A is not just about oil. The university is home to the **Alberta Machine Intelligence Institute (Amii)**, one of Canada's three national AI centers. U of A's reinforcement learning research group (where DeepMind's AlphaGo breakthroughs originated with Professor Richard Sutton) is world-class.

For Indian families, U of A offers the **best value in Canadian engineering**: lower tuition than Toronto/Waterloo/UBC, significantly lower living costs (Edmonton is very affordable), and excellent career outcomes especially in energy and tech.

## How to Get In

U of A Engineering is more accessible than the top Ontario/BC schools, with **30-40%** acceptance rates for international students.

**Application process:**
- Apply directly through U of A's online portal (Apply Alberta)
- Deadline: **March 1** for Fall admission (earlier is better for scholarship consideration)
- IELTS 6.5+ or TOEFL 90+
- 85%+ in Indian boards
- No supplementary essays or interviews required — admission is primarily grades-based

**This is ideal for strong students who prefer a straightforward, grades-based process** without the essay-writing demands of US or Waterloo applications.

## Curriculum & Structure

4-year BScEng or 5-year co-op option.

- **Year 1:** Common first-year engineering — math, physics, chemistry, computing, engineering design, and communication
- **Year 2 onward:** Specialize in your chosen discipline
- **Co-op option** — U of A offers an optional co-op program that extends the degree to 5 years with 3-4 paid work terms. Energy companies pay well — co-op salaries of CAD 5,000-8,000/month are common
- **Petroleum Engineering** — U of A has one of the strongest petroleum engineering programs globally, with direct industry partnerships and excellent job placement
- **Software Engineering** — growing rapidly with AI/ML focus through Amii connections
- **Research opportunities** — U of A is a top-5 Canadian research university. Undergraduate research assistantships are available from Year 2
- **Capstone projects** — industry-sponsored design projects in Year 4
- **Engineering Safety Programs** — strong emphasis on workplace safety, reflecting Alberta's energy industry culture

## Campus Life

U of A's main campus (North Campus) is along the North Saskatchewan River valley in Edmonton — Canada's northernmost major city.

- **Edmonton is very affordable** — rent is CAD 500-800/month, significantly cheaper than Toronto or Vancouver. This is a major financial advantage
- **Indian community is large** — Edmonton has a substantial Indian population. Indian grocery stores, restaurants, and cultural events (Diwali, Garba) are well-established
- **Cold winters — seriously cold** — Edmonton winters reach -30°C to -40°C. The campus has a pedway system (enclosed walkways) connecting buildings, so you rarely need to go outside in winter
- **Beautiful summers** — Edmonton has some of Canada's longest summer days (18+ hours of daylight in June). Summers are warm and gorgeous
- **West Edmonton Mall** — the largest mall in North America is in Edmonton. It has a waterpark, amusement park, and ice rink inside
- **Northern Lights** — visible from Edmonton on clear winter nights. A unique experience
- **Active campus life** — 450+ student clubs, including Indian Students' Association. Engineering Week ("E-Week") is a major annual tradition
- **Safe city** — Edmonton is generally safe, especially around the university area
