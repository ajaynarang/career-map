## Why Waterloo?

Waterloo is legendary for one thing: **co-op**. The University of Waterloo's co-operative education program is the largest in the world, and it is mandatory for all engineering students. Over 6 paid work terms alternating with study terms, your child will gain 2 full years of professional experience — at companies like Google, Apple, Tesla, and Meta — before graduating.

Waterloo engineers are recruited at rates that rival MIT and Stanford for tech roles. Silicon Valley companies send recruiters specifically to Waterloo. The result: Waterloo engineering graduates have among the highest starting salaries in Canada, and many receive offers from US tech companies with salaries in USD.

For Indian families focused on career outcomes and return on investment, Waterloo is arguably the best choice in Canada.

## How to Get In

Waterloo Engineering is the most selective engineering program in Canada, with **15-25%** acceptance rates. Software Engineering and Computer Engineering are even more competitive (under 10%).

**Application process:**
- Apply through **OUAC** (Ontario Universities' Application Centre)
- Complete the **AIF (Admission Information Form)** — a detailed supplementary application. This is critical and heavily weighted. Describe engineering projects, competitions, leadership, and explain why Waterloo
- Some programs require a **video interview**
- Deadline: **February 1** for Fall admission
- 90%+ in Indian boards (mid-90s for Software/Computer Engineering)

**The AIF is your child's chance to stand out.** Waterloo specifically looks for students who have built things — Arduino projects, coding competitions, science fairs, hackathons. Academic grades alone are not enough for the top programs.

## Curriculum & Structure

5-year BASc with mandatory co-op (alternating study and work terms).

- **Structure:** 4A/4B study terms alternated with 6 co-op work terms over 5 years. By graduation, your child has 2 full years of paid work experience
- **Co-op earnings:** Average CAD 12,000-20,000 per 4-month work term. Over 6 terms, students earn CAD 70,000-120,000+ total — this significantly offsets tuition costs
- **Software Engineering** — Waterloo's flagship program, jointly offered by Engineering and CS. Produces graduates recruited by Google, Meta, and top US tech companies. Starting salaries often exceed CAD 100,000 (or USD 120,000+ for US placements)
- **Mechatronics Engineering** — a unique program combining mechanical, electrical, and software engineering. Produces versatile graduates for robotics and automation
- **Nanotechnology Engineering** — unique to Waterloo, combining chemistry, physics, and engineering at the nanoscale
- **Systems Design Engineering** — a distinctive human-centered engineering program focused on design thinking
- **Capstone design projects** — major team-based industry projects in final year

## Campus Life

Waterloo is a mid-sized city (130,000) about 100 km west of Toronto. It's not glamorous, but it's focused.

- **The campus is all about engineering and tech** — Waterloo is sometimes called "Canada's Silicon Valley." The surrounding Waterloo Region is home to dozens of tech startups and scale-ups
- **Indian community is large and growing** — Waterloo has a significant Indian student population. Indian grocery stores, restaurants, and a Gurudwara are easily accessible
- **Cost of living is reasonable** — much cheaper than Toronto or Vancouver. Rent: CAD 700-1,000/month. Groceries and transportation are affordable
- **Cold winters** — Waterloo gets genuine Canadian winters (-15°C to -25°C). Budget for a good winter jacket, boots, and layers
- **Student culture is engineering-focused** — parties and social life exist, but Waterloo's culture is more academic/entrepreneurial than party-oriented
- **Toronto is 1 hour away** — GO Transit connects to Toronto for weekends and events
- **Velocity startup incubator** — Waterloo's ecosystem has produced companies like Kik, Thalmic Labs, and numerous AI startups. Many students start companies during or after their degrees
