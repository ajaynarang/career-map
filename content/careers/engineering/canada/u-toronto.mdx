## Why U of T?

The University of Toronto's Faculty of Applied Science & Engineering (known as "Skule") is Canada's top-ranked engineering school and consistently places in the global top 25. Located in downtown Toronto — Canada's largest city and economic capital — U of T engineers have unmatched access to internships at major tech companies, banks, and startups.

U of T is also a research powerhouse. The university is where Geoffrey Hinton pioneered deep learning (leading to modern AI), and the engineering faculty continues to push boundaries in AI, robotics, biomedical engineering, and sustainable energy. For Indian families, U of T offers a globally recognized degree, strong placements, and Toronto's massive, welcoming Indian community.

## How to Get In

U of T Engineering is competitive, with acceptance rates of **20-30%** for international students.

**Application process:**
- Apply through the **Ontario Universities' Application Centre (OUAC)** — international applicants use OUAC 105
- Complete U of T's **Online Student Profile** (supplementary application with short essays about why engineering, extracurriculars, and personal experiences)
- Deadline: **January 15** for Fall admission
- IELTS 6.5+ or TOEFL 100+
- 90%+ in CBSE/ISC 12th (especially Math, Physics, Chemistry)

**What U of T values:** Strong academics AND demonstrated interest in engineering through projects, competitions, or self-directed learning. The supplementary profile matters — don't treat it as an afterthought.

## Curriculum & Structure

4-year BASc (Bachelor of Applied Science). No separate MEng at undergrad level — the BASc is a professional engineering degree.

- **Year 1:** Common engineering core — calculus, physics, chemistry, programming, engineering design. All students take a large team design project ("Praxis") from day one
- **Year 2 onward:** Specialize in your chosen discipline (Computer, Electrical, Mechanical, Civil, Chemical, Materials, or Engineering Science)
- **Engineering Science** — U of T's elite program: a rigorous, theory-heavy stream that produces top researchers and entrepreneurs. Very selective (top 5% of admits)
- **PEY (Professional Experience Year)** — optional 12-16 month paid internship between Years 3 and 4. Average salary: CAD 50,000-65,000. Top placements pay CAD 80,000+
- **Capstone design projects** — Year 4 features major industry-sponsored design projects
- **Minors and certificates** — in AI, business, robotics, sustainability, and more

## Campus Life

U of T's St. George campus is in the heart of downtown Toronto, surrounded by hospitals, tech companies, and cultural attractions.

- **Toronto is Canada's New York** — diverse, dynamic, and expensive. 50%+ of Toronto's population was born outside Canada. Your child will feel welcome
- **Indian community is enormous** — Toronto has the largest Indian population in Canada. Diwali is celebrated citywide. Indian restaurants, grocery stores, and temples are everywhere
- **Cost of living is high** — Toronto is Canada's most expensive city. Budget CAD 1,500-2,000/month for rent + food. On-campus residence is guaranteed for first year
- **Academic intensity** — U of T Engineering is demanding. The "Skule" culture is proud of its rigor. Study groups and engineering clubs are essential
- **Iron Ring ceremony** — a unique Canadian engineering tradition where graduating engineers receive an iron ring symbolizing their ethical obligation
- **500+ student clubs** — including active Indian and South Asian associations
- **Safe city** — Toronto is consistently ranked among the safest major cities globally
