## Why McGill?

McGill is Canada's most internationally recognized university — often called the "Harvard of Canada." Founded in 1821, it has produced 12 Nobel laureates and consistently ranks among the world's top universities. The Faculty of Engineering has particular strengths in aerospace (Montreal is a global aerospace hub — Bombardier, Pratt & Whitney, and CAE are headquartered here), AI, and mining engineering.

The biggest draw for Indian families: **Montreal is remarkably affordable**. Despite being a world-class city, rent and living costs in Montreal are 30-40% lower than Toronto or Vancouver. Your child gets a globally prestigious degree in a vibrant, bilingual city at a fraction of what comparable programs cost elsewhere.

## How to Get In

McGill's engineering admission is straightforward — **no supplementary application, no interview, no essays**. It's almost entirely grades-based.

**Application process:**
- Apply directly through McGill's online portal (Minerva)
- Deadline: **January 15** for Fall admission
- IELTS 6.5+ or TOEFL 90+
- 88%+ in Indian boards
- McGill primarily looks at Math, Physics, and Chemistry grades

**This is a major advantage for Indian students.** If your child excels in board exams but isn't strong at essays or extracurriculars, McGill levels the playing field.

## Curriculum & Structure

4-year BEng degree. Semester system (Fall and Winter).

- **Year 1:** Common engineering core — calculus, linear algebra, physics, chemistry, programming, and engineering graphics
- **Years 2-4:** Specialization with increasing depth and elective flexibility
- **Aerospace cluster** — Montreal is the world's third-largest aerospace hub. McGill's partnerships with Bombardier, Pratt & Whitney, and the Canadian Space Agency give students unique opportunities
- **AI and ML** — McGill is home to MILA (Quebec AI Institute), the world's largest academic AI research lab. Engineering students can take courses and do research through MILA
- **Internship program** — while not mandatory co-op like Waterloo, McGill's engineering internship program connects students with paid placements. Many students do summer internships in Montreal's aerospace and tech sectors
- **Research opportunities** — McGill undergraduates can participate in faculty research from Year 2 onward
- **Engineering Games** — McGill teams regularly compete in Quebec and Canadian engineering competitions

## Campus Life

McGill's downtown campus sits at the foot of Mont Royal in the heart of Montreal, one of North America's most vibrant cities.

- **Montreal is magical** — a bilingual (French-English) city known for its festivals, food, nightlife, and cultural scene. Jazz Fest, Just for Laughs, and the Grand Prix are annual highlights
- **Incredibly affordable** — rent in Montreal is CAD 600-900/month (half of Toronto/Vancouver). Groceries, dining, and entertainment are all cheaper. This is McGill's hidden advantage
- **Indian community is growing** — Montreal's Indian population is smaller than Toronto's but growing fast. Good Indian restaurants and grocery stores in Parc-Extension neighborhood
- **French adds value** — learning French in Montreal is an asset, not a burden. McGill is English-medium, but casual French exposure gives your child a third language. Quebec immigration programs (PEQ) also favor French speakers for PR
- **Winter is serious** — Montreal winters are cold (-15°C to -25°C) and snowy. But the city embraces it with underground malls, heated bus shelters, and winter festivals
- **Underground city (RESO)** — Montreal's underground network of tunnels connects shops, metros, and buildings. Very useful in winter
- **Legal drinking age is 18** — Quebec's lower drinking age means the social scene is different from Ontario (19). Parents should be aware
