## Why UBC?

UBC is Canada's second-ranked engineering school, set on one of the most beautiful campuses in the world — 400 hectares of forest and gardens overlooking the Pacific Ocean and Coast Mountains. Beyond the scenery, UBC Engineering is a powerhouse in clean energy, mining, biomedical engineering, and software.

Vancouver's tech sector is booming — Amazon, Microsoft, Apple, and EA Games all have major offices here. The city's proximity to the US West Coast (Seattle is 2 hours away) creates additional opportunities. For Indian families, Vancouver has a very large and established Indian community, particularly Punjabi, making the cultural transition smoother.

## How to Get In

UBC Engineering accepts **25-35%** of international applicants — more accessible than U of T but still competitive.

**Application process:**
- Apply directly through UBC's online portal
- Complete the **Personal Profile** — short-answer questions about experiences, challenges, and why engineering
- Deadline: **January 15** for Fall admission
- IELTS 6.5+ or TOEFL 90+
- 88%+ in Indian boards (Math, Physics, Chemistry)

**UBC's Personal Profile** is genuinely important — not a formality. Write thoughtful answers about your experiences and motivations.

## Curriculum & Structure

4-year BASc. Common first year, then specialization.

- **Year 1:** All engineering students take the same courses — math, physics, chemistry, computing, engineering design. At the end of Year 1, you rank your preferred specialization
- **Years 2-4:** Specialize in your chosen discipline with increasing depth
- **Co-op program** — UBC Engineering has a strong co-op program with 4-6 paid work terms. Average co-op salary: CAD 4,500-6,500/month. Many students earn CAD 50,000-70,000+ over their co-op terms
- **Engineering Physics** — UBC's elite stream, similar to U of T's Engineering Science. Very rigorous, small cohort, produces top researchers
- **Capstone projects** — Year 4 industry-sponsored design projects with real clients
- **MINE 2-4-2** — UBC has one of the world's top mining engineering programs, with strong industry connections
- **Sustainability focus** — UBC is a leader in green building and sustainable engineering research

## Campus Life

UBC's Point Grey campus is a self-contained community on Vancouver's western tip — surrounded by forest, beaches, and ocean.

- **The campus is breathtaking** — mountains, ocean, and forest. Wreck Beach, Pacific Spirit Park, and Japanese Garden are all on campus
- **Vancouver is spectacular** — skiing in winter, hiking and kayaking in summer, all within 30 minutes of campus
- **Indian community is very large** — Surrey and other Vancouver suburbs have massive Punjabi communities. Vaisakhi parade in Surrey is one of the largest outside India. UBC has active Indian student associations
- **Cost of living is high** — Vancouver is Canada's most expensive city after Toronto. Budget CAD 1,300-1,800/month. First-year residence is guaranteed
- **Mild winters** — unlike most of Canada, Vancouver rarely drops below 0°C. Expect rain (lots of it) rather than snow
- **Transit connected** — SkyTrain and bus connect campus to downtown Vancouver
- **Outdoor lifestyle** — if your child loves nature, UBC is paradise. Hiking, skiing, surfing, and kayaking are all accessible
