## Why ANU?

The Australian National University is Australia's top-ranked university overall and a research powerhouse. Located in Canberra (Australia's capital), ANU has a unique relationship with government research agencies like CSIRO, the Defence Science & Technology Group, and Geoscience Australia — creating unmatched opportunities for engineering students interested in research, defence, space, and public policy.

ANU's engineering programs are smaller and more intimate than the mega-faculties at Melbourne or UNSW. Class sizes are manageable, professors are accessible, and the research-to-student ratio is the best in Australia. ANU is also a leader in **renewable energy engineering** and **cybersecurity** — two fields with enormous growth potential.

For Indian families, ANU offers the **most affordable option among Australia's top 5 universities**: lower tuition than Melbourne/UNSW/Sydney, and Canberra's living costs are lower than Sydney or Melbourne. The generous Chancellor's International Scholarship makes ANU even more financially attractive.

## How to Get In

ANU accepts **35-50%** of international engineering applicants, with slightly lower grade requirements than Melbourne or UNSW.

**Application process:**
- Apply directly through ANU's online portal
- Deadline: **December 15** for Semester 1 (February start); **May 15** for Semester 2 (July start)
- IELTS 6.5+ or TOEFL 80+
- 82-87%+ in Indian boards
- No entrance exam, no interview

**ANU automatically considers all applicants for scholarships** — no separate scholarship application needed for most awards.

## Curriculum & Structure

4-year Bachelor of Engineering (Honours) or flexible double degree options. Accredited by Engineers Australia.

- **Year 1:** Engineering foundations — math, physics, programming, and engineering practice
- **Years 2-3:** Core specialization with elective flexibility
- **Year 4:** Advanced topics + Honours research project
- **Flexible double degrees** — ANU's most distinctive feature. Popular combinations include Engineering + Science, Engineering + Commerce, and Engineering + International Relations. These add 1-2 years but produce uniquely versatile graduates
- **Renewable Energy Engineering** — ANU's world-leading program trains engineers for solar, wind, battery storage, and grid integration. Australia's clean energy transition creates enormous demand
- **CSIRO partnerships** — ANU's proximity to CSIRO (Australia's national science agency) gives students access to cutting-edge labs and research projects
- **Defence and space** — Canberra is Australia's defence capital. ANU engineers work on cybersecurity, satellite systems, and defence technology projects
- **Research-intensive** — ANU has Australia's highest research output per capita. Undergraduate students can join research groups from Year 2

## Campus Life

ANU's campus is in the heart of Canberra, adjacent to Lake Burley Griffin and surrounded by national institutions (Parliament House, National Gallery, War Memorial).

- **Canberra is different** — it's a planned city, quiet and orderly. Nothing like the buzz of Sydney or Melbourne. Some students love the focus; others find it too quiet
- **Very safe** — Canberra is one of the safest cities in Australia (and the world)
- **Affordable** — rent is AUD 700-1,100/month (cheaper than Sydney/Melbourne). Groceries and dining are reasonable
- **Indian community is growing** — Canberra's Indian population is smaller than Melbourne's or Sydney's but growing rapidly. ANU has an active Indian Students' Association
- **Residential colleges** — ANU has a college system where many students live, eat, and socialize. This creates a tight-knit community similar to Oxbridge
- **Nature at your doorstep** — hiking in the Brindabella Ranges, skiing at Perisher/Thredbo (2 hours away), and kangaroos on campus are all part of the ANU experience
- **Cold by Australian standards** — Canberra has genuine winters (0-5°C) unlike coastal cities. It's Australia's coldest capital, but nothing like Canadian or UK winters
- **Sydney is 3 hours away** — easy road trip or bus ride for weekends and events
