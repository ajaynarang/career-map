## Why ANU?

The Australian National University is Australia's top-ranked university overall (QS #1 in Australia) and unique in being located in Canberra, the nation's capital. ANU's engineering programs benefit from close ties to the federal government, CSIRO (Australia's national science agency), and defence contractors — creating opportunities that don't exist anywhere else in Australia.

ANU is particularly strong in Renewable Energy Engineering (it houses the ANU College of Engineering with world-leading solar research), Systems Engineering, and Computer Science. For students interested in defence, cybersecurity, or government-related engineering, ANU's Canberra location is a decisive advantage.

## How to Get In

ANU accepts **30-40%** of international engineering applicants.

**Application:**
- Apply directly through ANU's online portal
- No entrance exam or interview
- 82-88%+ in Indian boards
- IELTS 6.5+ (6.0 in each band)
- Two intakes: **February** and **July**
- Deadline: **December 15** for February; **May 15** for July

## Curriculum & Structure

4-year Bachelor of Engineering (Honours). Semester system.

- **Flexible degree structure** — ANU allows significant freedom in choosing electives across the university
- **Double degrees are popular** — BE/BSc, BE/BEcon, BE/BA are common. Add 1-2 years
- **Renewable Energy Engineering** — ANU's unique strength. The university is home to some of the world's leading solar energy researchers
- **Research-intensive** — ANU has the highest research funding per student in Australia. Undergraduate research opportunities are abundant
- **CSIRO partnership** — CSIRO labs are literally on campus. Students can do internships and thesis projects with Australia's premier research organization
- **Innovation Hub** — ANU's engineering innovation programs connect students with Canberra's growing tech and startup scene

## Campus Life

ANU's campus is in the center of Canberra, surrounded by parks, Lake Burley Griffin, and national institutions.

- **Canberra is unique** — it's a planned city, clean, safe, and quiet. Population is only 450,000. It's very different from Sydney or Melbourne
- **Most affordable major city** — rent is AUD 200-280/week for shared accommodation. Cheaper than Sydney or Melbourne
- **Indian community** — moderate size, growing. Indian restaurants and cultural events are available
- **Residential campus** — ANU has one of Australia's strongest residential college systems. Most first-year students live on campus (AUD 200-350/week)
- **Nature and outdoors** — Canberra is surrounded by national parks, bush walking trails, and ski fields (2 hours drive to Thredbo/Perisher)
- **Government city** — Parliament House, national museums, and galleries are walking distance. If your child is interested in policy or government tech, Canberra is ideal
- **Quiet nightlife** — Canberra is not a party city. Students who want big-city excitement on weekends can take the 3-hour bus to Sydney
- **Cold for Australia** — Canberra has cold winters by Australian standards (0-5°C). Not as extreme as Canada but colder than Sydney or Melbourne
- **Post-study work bonus** — studying in Canberra (a designated regional area) can qualify for an additional year on the post-study work visa
