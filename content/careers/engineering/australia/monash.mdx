## Why Monash?

Monash University is one of Australia's most innovative engineering schools, with particular strengths in materials science, renewable energy, aerospace, and robotics. Monash is part of the prestigious Group of Eight (Go8) — Australia's equivalent of the Ivy League — and its engineering faculty has a strong industry focus.

What sets Monash apart is its **global presence**. Monash has campuses in Melbourne, Malaysia, South Africa, and a research centre in India (IIT Bombay partnership). This international network creates unique opportunities for cross-campus study and global industry connections.

For Indian families, Monash offers slightly lower entry requirements and fees than Melbourne or UNSW, a strong Indian student community (Monash has one of the highest Indian student populations in Australia), and Melbourne's excellent quality of life.

## How to Get In

Monash Engineering is accessible, with **40-55%** acceptance for international students and lower grade thresholds than Melbourne.

**Application process:**
- Apply directly through the Monash portal or through authorized agents
- Deadline: rolling admissions, but aim for **October-November** for February intake
- IELTS 6.5+ or TOEFL 79+
- 80-85%+ in Indian boards (lower than Melbourne's 85%+)
- No entrance exam, no interview

**Monash is an excellent choice for students with good (not exceptional) grades** who want a top-tier Australian engineering education.

## Curriculum & Structure

4-year Bachelor of Engineering (Honours). Fully accredited by Engineers Australia.

- **Year 1:** Engineering foundations — math, physics, programming, and introduction to engineering practice
- **Years 2-3:** Core specialization courses
- **Year 4:** Advanced topics + Honours research project
- **Industry-Based Learning (IBL)** — Monash's signature program: a 6-12 month paid industry placement integrated into the degree. Students work at companies like BHP, Boeing, Bosch, and Aurecon
- **Robotics & Mechatronics Engineering** — a standout program combining mechanical, electrical, and software engineering with a robotics focus
- **Double degrees** — popular options include Engineering + Commerce, Engineering + Science, and Engineering + Design
- **Monash Motorsport** — students design and build Formula SAE race cars. An excellent hands-on learning experience
- **Study at overseas campuses** — unique opportunity to spend a semester at Monash Malaysia or participate in global programs

## Campus Life

Monash's Clayton campus is in Melbourne's southeast suburbs, about 20 km from the CBD. It's a large, self-contained campus with all facilities.

- **Melbourne lifestyle** — all the benefits of Melbourne (food, culture, sport, safety) at a slightly lower cost than living near the CBD
- **Very large Indian community** — Clayton and surrounding suburbs (Glen Waverley, Dandenong) have massive Indian populations. Indian grocery stores, restaurants, and temples are within walking distance of campus
- **Affordable by Melbourne standards** — rent near Clayton is AUD 800-1,200/month (cheaper than inner Melbourne). Good student share housing available
- **Campus is a mini-city** — restaurants, gyms, libraries, health services, and sports facilities all on campus. The campus bus network connects to train stations
- **Active student life** — Monash has 100+ clubs including a large Indian Students' Association, cricket clubs, and Bollywood dance teams
- **Safe suburban setting** — Clayton is a quiet, safe suburb. Good for focused study
- **F1 and cricket** — the Australian Grand Prix and Boxing Day Test at the MCG are Melbourne traditions your child will love
