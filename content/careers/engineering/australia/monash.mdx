## Why Monash?

Monash University is Australia's largest university and a member of the prestigious Group of Eight (Go8). Its engineering programs are strongly industry-focused, with mandatory 12 weeks of professional practice and strong connections to Australia's manufacturing, mining, and tech sectors.

Monash has a unique advantage for Indian students: **Monash has a campus in India (IIT Bombay partnership)** and campuses in Malaysia, Indonesia, and South Africa. This global network provides unique exchange and research opportunities. The engineering faculty is particularly strong in Materials Science, Robotics, and Chemical Engineering.

## How to Get In

Monash has a **35-45%** acceptance rate for international engineering students — the most accessible major engineering university in Australia.

**Application:**
- Apply directly through the Monash portal
- No entrance exam, interview, or supplementary essays
- 80-85%+ in Indian boards
- IELTS 6.5+ (6.0 in each band)
- Two intakes: **February** and **July**

Monash's lower grade requirements and straightforward application make it an excellent option for students who want a strong engineering education without ultra-competitive admissions.

## Curriculum & Structure

4-year Bachelor of Engineering (Honours). Semester system.

- **Common first year** — engineering students share foundational courses before specializing
- **IBL (Industry Based Learning)** — Monash's structured industry placement program. 12 weeks minimum of professional experience
- **Robotics & Mechatronics Engineering** — a unique specialization combining mechanical, electrical, and software engineering
- **Global exchange** — Monash's international campuses enable unique exchange programs (Malaysia, South Africa, Indonesia)
- **Research-active** — Monash has Australia's largest engineering faculty with strong research output
- **Double degrees** — BE/BSc, BE/BA, BE/BBiomedSc, and other combinations available

## Campus Life

Monash's Clayton campus (main engineering campus) is in Melbourne's southeastern suburbs, about 25km from the CBD.

- **Clayton is suburban** — not a city campus. But the campus itself is large and well-equipped. Regular shuttle buses to nearby train stations
- **Indian community is huge** — the Clayton/Glen Waverley area has one of the largest Indian populations in Melbourne. Indian restaurants, temples, and grocery stores are walking distance from campus
- **More affordable than CBD** — rent in Clayton/surrounding suburbs is AUD 200-280/week for shared accommodation. Cheaper than living near University of Melbourne
- **Monash student culture** — diverse, inclusive, and active. 100+ clubs and societies
- **Caulfield campus** — Monash's second major campus in Caulfield (closer to the city) houses some programs
- **Melbourne's lifestyle** — all the benefits of Melbourne (food, culture, sport) are accessible via public transport
- **Part-time work** — many students work part-time at campus facilities, nearby shopping centers, or in the CBD
