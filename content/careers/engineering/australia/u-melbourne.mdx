## Why Melbourne?

The University of Melbourne is consistently ranked #1 in Australia and among the top 25 globally for engineering. Its unique "Melbourne Model" follows a broad undergraduate education philosophy similar to US universities — students study a general Bachelor of Design (Engineering) before specializing, producing graduates who can think across disciplines.

Melbourne the city is regularly ranked the world's most liveable city — and for good reason. Excellent public transport, world-class food, vibrant arts scene, and a massive Indian community make it one of the most comfortable cities for Indian students to settle into. Melbourne is also Australia's tech and startup hub, with Google, Atlassian, REA Group, and numerous startups headquartered here.

## How to Get In

Melbourne is more accessible than its global ranking suggests, with **40-55%** acceptance for international engineering students.

**Application process:**
- Apply directly through the University of Melbourne portal
- Deadline: **October 31** for February intake (Semester 1); **April 30** for July intake (Semester 2)
- IELTS 6.5+ or TOEFL 79+
- 85%+ in Indian boards (Math and Physics required)
- No entrance exam, no interview, no supplementary essays — admission is grades-based

**This straightforward process is a major advantage** for Indian students accustomed to exam-based systems.

## Curriculum & Structure

3-year Bachelor of Design (Engineering) + 2-3 year Master of Engineering (the "Melbourne Model").

**Important note for parents:** Melbourne follows a unique model. The undergraduate degree is a 3-year Bachelor of Design, which is NOT a professional engineering qualification. To become a qualified engineer, students must complete a subsequent 2-3 year Master of Engineering. Total time: 5-6 years.

- **Years 1-3 (Bachelor):** Broad engineering foundations with increasing specialization. The breadth component allows students to take subjects from other faculties (science, arts, commerce)
- **Years 4-6 (Master):** Professional engineering specialization with advanced research project
- **Alternative: Direct entry into 4-year Bachelor of Engineering Honours** — this is the more traditional pathway and is accredited by Engineers Australia as a standalone qualification
- **Industry projects** — partnerships with BHP, Rio Tinto, Aurecon, and other major companies
- **Study abroad** — exchange programs with 150+ universities globally
- **Research opportunities** — Melbourne is Australia's top research university. Undergraduate research assistantships available

## Campus Life

The University of Melbourne's Parkville campus is 2 km north of Melbourne's CBD — walking distance to the city center.

- **Melbourne is incredible** — consistently ranked the world's most liveable city. Art, food, sport (AFL, cricket, F1 Grand Prix), and culture are world-class
- **Massive Indian community** — Melbourne has Australia's largest Indian population. Diwali is celebrated across the city. Indian restaurants and grocery stores are everywhere, especially in suburbs like Glen Waverley, Box Hill, and Dandenong
- **Cost of living is moderate** — cheaper than Sydney. Budget AUD 1,500-2,000/month for rent + food. Student share houses are common and affordable
- **Excellent public transport** — trams, trains, and buses. The city center tram zone is free
- **Coffee culture** — Melbourne is famous for its coffee. Your child will become a coffee snob
- **Safe city** — Melbourne is very safe, especially around the university precinct
- **Sport-mad city** — if your child likes cricket, the MCG (Melbourne Cricket Ground) is iconic. The Australian Open tennis and F1 Grand Prix are annual highlights
