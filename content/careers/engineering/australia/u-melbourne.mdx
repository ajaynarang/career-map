## Why University of Melbourne?

The University of Melbourne is Australia's #1 university and consistently ranked in the global top 30 for engineering. What makes Melbourne unique in Australia is its **"Melbourne Model"** — a broad-based undergraduate education (similar to the US liberal arts approach) followed by a professional Master's degree. For engineering, this means a 3-year Bachelor of Science followed by a 2-year Master of Engineering, totaling 5 years for a fully accredited professional engineering degree.

Melbourne (the city) is consistently ranked the world's most livable city. It's diverse, culturally rich, safe, and has a growing tech sector anchored by Atlassian, REA Group, and dozens of startups.

## How to Get In

Melbourne's acceptance rate for international students is **35-45%** — quite accessible compared to US or UK elite universities.

**Application:**
- Apply directly through the University of Melbourne portal
- No entrance exam, interviews, or supplementary essays
- 85%+ in CBSE/ISC 12th boards with strong Math and Physics
- IELTS 6.5+ (minimum 6.0 in each band)
- Deadline: **October 31** for February start; **April 30** for July start

**Important:** Melbourne uses the Melbourne Model. You apply for a **Bachelor of Science** (majoring in computing, math, or a science), then progress to a **Master of Engineering** for professional accreditation. Alternatively, apply for the Bachelor of Design (for Civil/Structural/Environmental engineering pathways).

## Curriculum & Structure

The Melbourne Model is different from other Australian universities:

- **Years 1-3 (Bachelor of Science):** Broad-based education with an engineering-focused major (Computing & Software Systems, Mechanical Systems, Electrical Systems, etc.) plus breadth subjects from other faculties
- **Years 4-5 (Master of Engineering):** Professional engineering specialization with industry placements and a research project
- **Total duration: 5 years** for a fully accredited engineering qualification (vs. 4 years at other Australian universities)
- **Advantage of the Melbourne Model:** breadth of education, flexibility to change direction, and a Master's degree at the end
- **Industry placements** — built into the Master of Engineering program
- **Research opportunities** — strong connections to CSIRO and industry research labs

## Campus Life

The University of Melbourne's Parkville campus is 2km north of Melbourne CBD, in one of Melbourne's most charming neighborhoods.

- **Melbourne is incredible** — coffee culture, street art, live music, sports (AFL, cricket, tennis — the Australian Open is here), diverse food scene
- **Massive Indian community** — Melbourne has 200,000+ Indians. Suburbs like Glen Waverley, Clayton, and Dandenong have extensive Indian infrastructure — restaurants, temples, grocery stores, Bollywood cinemas
- **Cost of living** — Melbourne is expensive by Australian standards but cheaper than Sydney. Rent: AUD 250-350/week for a shared apartment. Indian groceries are affordable
- **Tram network** — Melbourne has the largest tram network in the world. The city center tram zone is free
- **Weather is mild** — Melbourne's weather is famously unpredictable ("four seasons in one day"), but winters rarely go below 5°C and summers average 25-30°C
- **Part-time work is easy to find** — many students work part-time in hospitality, retail, or tutoring to supplement their budget
