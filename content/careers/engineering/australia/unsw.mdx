## Why UNSW?

UNSW (University of New South Wales) has Australia's largest and most comprehensive engineering faculty. It's the #1 destination for Indian students wanting to study engineering in Australia, with a particularly strong reputation in Solar Energy (UNSW developed the world's most efficient silicon solar cell), Software Engineering, and Telecommunications.

UNSW's connection to industry is its superpower. The university has partnerships with over 200 industry organizations, and its co-op program (one of the first in Australia) gives students 60 weeks of paid industry experience before graduating.

## How to Get In

UNSW accepts **30-40%** of international engineering applicants.

**Application:**
- Apply directly through the UNSW portal or through an authorized agent
- No entrance exam or interview
- 82-88%+ in Indian boards with strong Math and Physics
- IELTS 6.5+ (6.0 minimum in each band)
- **Three terms per year** (UNSW uses a trimester system) — February, June, September intakes

**UNSW's trimester system** means more flexibility in when you start and potentially faster degree completion.

## Curriculum & Structure

4-year Bachelor of Engineering (Honours). UNSW operates on a **trimester system** (3 terms per year, 10 weeks each).

- **Direct entry into engineering** — unlike Melbourne, UNSW offers a traditional 4-year engineering degree with Honours built in
- **Industrial Training (co-op)** — UNSW Engineering requires 60 days of industrial training. The co-op program (optional but recommended) provides 18 months of paid work experience
- **Renewable Energy Engineering** — UNSW has a unique undergraduate degree in this field, leveraging its world-leading solar energy research
- **Photovoltaics & Solar Energy** — the only undergraduate program of its kind globally
- **Thesis Year** — the 4th year includes an Honours thesis and a capstone design project
- **Strong computing** — UNSW CS is excellent, particularly in cybersecurity, AI, and systems

## Campus Life

UNSW's main campus is in Kensington, a 15-minute bus ride from Sydney CBD and close to famous beaches like Coogee and Bondi.

- **Sydney is spectacular** — the harbour, the beaches, the Opera House. It's one of the world's great cities
- **Indian community is enormous** — Sydney has 150,000+ Indians. Harris Park (a suburb) is known as "Little India." Indian festivals, cinemas, and cultural events are abundant
- **Expensive** — Sydney is Australia's most expensive city. Rent: AUD 280-400/week for shared accommodation. Budget carefully
- **Beach lifestyle** — Coogee Beach is a 15-minute bus from campus. Many students surf, swim, or jog on the coastal walk
- **Part-time work** — many students work part-time. Sydney has more part-time job opportunities than smaller cities
- **Weather is warm** — Sydney rarely goes below 10°C in winter. Summers are hot (25-35°C). Sunshine is abundant
- **Trimester impact** — the 3-term system means shorter breaks but a more intense academic calendar
