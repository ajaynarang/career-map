## Why UNSW?

UNSW Sydney (University of New South Wales) has Australia's largest engineering faculty and is consistently ranked in the global top 30 for engineering. UNSW is particularly strong in solar energy (its researchers hold multiple world records for solar cell efficiency), computer science, and telecommunications.

UNSW has a distinctly practical, industry-connected approach. Its engineering graduates have the highest employer demand in Australia — UNSW produces more tech founders and CEOs than any other Australian university. Atlassian (makers of Jira and Confluence, valued at over USD 50 billion) was founded by UNSW engineering graduates.

For Indian families, UNSW offers excellent career outcomes, strong industry connections, and Sydney's large Indian community.

## How to Get In

UNSW is accessible with **35-50%** acceptance rates for international engineering students.

**Application process:**
- Apply directly through UNSW's online portal or through authorized agents
- UNSW operates on a **trimester system** with 3 intakes: February (T1), June (T2), September (T3)
- Deadline: varies by trimester — typically 2-3 months before start
- IELTS 6.5+ or TOEFL 90+
- 85%+ in Indian boards (Math and Physics required)
- No entrance exam, no interview

**The trimester system is an advantage** — if your child misses the February intake, they can start in June or September without waiting a full year.

## Curriculum & Structure

4-year Bachelor of Engineering (Honours). Fully accredited by Engineers Australia.

- **Year 1:** Engineering foundations — math, physics, computing, and introduction to your chosen discipline
- **Years 2-3:** Core specialization courses with increasing depth
- **Year 4:** Advanced topics + Honours thesis (individual research project)
- **Industrial training** — minimum 60 days of approved industrial training required for graduation. Most students complete this through summer internships
- **Renewable Energy Engineering** — UNSW's unique program, the only one of its kind in Australia. Trains engineers for the booming clean energy sector
- **Photovoltaics & Solar Energy** — UNSW's world-leading solar research group offers undergraduate pathways
- **Co-op program** — select students can join the UNSW Co-op Program, which provides paid placements and industry scholarships worth AUD 19,500/year
- **Design projects** — team-based projects with industry partners in Years 3-4

## Campus Life

UNSW's main campus is in Kensington, 7 km southeast of Sydney CBD, near the beaches of Coogee and Maroubra.

- **Sydney is Sydney** — the Harbour Bridge, Opera House, Bondi Beach, and one of the world's most beautiful harbors. It's an extraordinary place to live
- **Large Indian community** — Sydney has a massive Indian population, especially in western suburbs (Parramatta, Harris Park — sometimes called "Little India"). UNSW has a very active Indian Students' Association
- **Cost of living is high** — Sydney is Australia's most expensive city. Budget AUD 1,800-2,200/month for rent + food. Share housing is common and essential
- **Beach culture** — Coogee and Maroubra beaches are a short bus ride from campus. Many students surf, swim, or run along the coastal walk
- **Trimester system means faster completion** — students can take courses over 3 terms and potentially graduate sooner
- **Vibrant startup scene** — UNSW's Founders Program and connections to Sydney's startup ecosystem give entrepreneurially-minded students opportunities
- **Safe and well-connected** — excellent bus connections to the CBD, train to the airport, and light rail under construction
