## Why Australia for Engineering?

Australia is an increasingly popular destination for Indian engineering students, offering a unique combination of high-quality education, post-study work rights, and one of the best immigration pathways in the world. Engineering is on Australia's **Skills Priority List**, meaning engineering graduates have a direct pathway to Permanent Residency (PR) through the skilled migration program.

**The financial picture:** A 4-year engineering degree in Australia costs ₹1.3-1.6 Crore total — cheaper than the US or UK, and comparable to Canada. The ability to work 48 hours per fortnight during studies helps offset costs, and Australian wages are among the highest in the world.

## The Path

1. **Class 11-12:** Focus on board exams (Math, Physics, Chemistry). Prepare for IELTS (aim for 6.5+)
2. **Summer before 12th:** Research universities. Most Australian universities accept applications year-round for the February or July intake
3. **Apply directly** to universities through their online portals. No centralized system like UCAS or Common App. Most deadlines are October-November for February intake
4. **Receive offers** — conditional on 12th board results. Accept and pay deposit
5. **Apply for Student Visa (subclass 500)** — requires CoE (Confirmation of Enrolment), IELTS, financial proof, and OSHC (health insurance)
6. **February or July:** Start university

## What Parents Should Know

- **February is the main intake.** Unlike the US/UK/Canada (September start), Australian universities primarily start in February (Semester 1). July (Semester 2) intake is also available at most universities.
- **4-year Honours degree.** Australian engineering degrees are 4 years and include an Honours year with a research project. This is equivalent to a UK MEng and is fully accredited by Engineers Australia.
- **48 hours per fortnight work rights.** Your child can work up to 48 hours every 2 weeks during semester (and unlimited during breaks). At Australian minimum wage (~AUD 24/hour), this provides meaningful income.
- **Post-study work visa is generous.** After a 4-year engineering degree, graduates get a 2-year post-study work visa (Temporary Graduate visa, subclass 485). Studying in regional areas (Adelaide, Perth) can extend this to 3-4 years.
- **Engineering is on the Skills Priority List.** This means your child can apply for Permanent Residency through the skilled migration program after gaining work experience. Engineering is one of the most in-demand professions in Australia.
- **OSHC (Overseas Student Health Cover)** is mandatory. This is private health insurance that costs AUD 500-700/year. It covers doctor visits, hospital, and some medications.
- **Climate is a major advantage.** Most Australian cities have warm, sunny climates year-round. This is a significant quality-of-life factor compared to Canada or the UK.
- **Indian community is massive.** Australia has one of the largest Indian diaspora populations globally. Melbourne and Sydney in particular have huge Indian communities with temples, festivals, restaurants, and grocery stores.
