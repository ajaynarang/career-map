## Why Australia for Engineering?

Australia has quietly become one of the best destinations for Indian engineering students. The universities are globally ranked (Melbourne, UNSW, and Sydney are all top-50 for engineering), the post-study work visa is generous (2-4 years), and the country's immigration system actively welcomes skilled engineers through its Skilled Migration program.

**The lifestyle advantage is real:** Australia offers a quality of life that's hard to match — warm weather, outdoor culture, safe cities, and a large, established Indian community. For families who want their child to have a good life during and after university (not just a degree), Australia is compelling.

## The Path

1. **Class 11-12:** Focus on strong board results (80%+) and IELTS preparation (aim for 6.5-7.0)
2. **After 12th:** Apply directly to Australian universities — most have rolling admissions and multiple intakes (February and July)
3. **Offer and acceptance:** Most universities give offers within 2-4 weeks. Accept and pay deposit
4. **CoE (Confirmation of Enrolment):** Required for visa application
5. **Student visa (subclass 500):** Apply with CoE, OSHC (health insurance), proof of funds, and GTE (Genuine Temporary Entrant) statement
6. **February or July:** Start university

## What Parents Should Know

- **Two intakes per year.** Unlike most countries with a single September intake, Australian universities accept students in February (Semester 1, the main intake) and July (Semester 2). This flexibility is useful if your child's board results come at an awkward time.
- **IELTS requirements are lower.** Most Australian universities require IELTS 6.0-6.5 — lower than the UK or Canada. This makes Australia accessible to students who are still building English proficiency.
- **Post-study work visa is generous.** A 4-year engineering degree qualifies for a 2-year post-study work visa, with extensions possible for studying in regional areas (up to 4 years total). Engineering is also on the Skilled Occupation List, making permanent residency a realistic goal.
- **OSHC (Overseas Student Health Cover) is mandatory.** All international students must purchase OSHC for the duration of their visa. This costs approximately AUD 500-600/year but provides comprehensive health coverage.
- **The GTE requirement.** To get a student visa, your child must write a "Genuine Temporary Entrant" statement explaining why they want to study in Australia. This is a migration risk assessment — it's important to get it right.
- **Engineering is a 4-year Honours degree.** Unlike the UK's 3-year BEng, Australian engineering Bachelor's degrees are 4 years and include an Honours year with a research thesis. This is equivalent to a Master's-level qualification in many countries.
- **Engineers Australia accreditation matters.** For long-term career and migration purposes, choose programs accredited by Engineers Australia. All universities listed here have accredited programs.
