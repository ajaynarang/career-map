## Why University of Sydney?

The University of Sydney is Australia's oldest university (founded 1850) and one of its most prestigious. Its engineering programs are comprehensive, with particular strengths in Aeronautical Engineering, Biomedical Engineering, and Civil Engineering. The beautiful Gothic campus (the "Harry Potter building") is iconic, and the university's prestige carries significant weight with Australian employers.

The University of Sydney's approach to engineering is distinctive: a strong emphasis on professional practice, ethics, and communication alongside technical excellence. Graduates are known for being "industry-ready" from day one.

## How to Get In

Acceptance rate is **30-40%** for international engineering applicants.

**Application:**
- Apply through the University of Sydney's online portal or through UAC (Universities Admissions Centre)
- No entrance exam or interview
- 82-88%+ in Indian boards
- IELTS 6.5+ (6.0 in each band)
- Deadlines: **January 15** for Semester 1 (February); **June 25** for Semester 2 (August)

## Curriculum & Structure

4-year Bachelor of Engineering (Honours). Semester system.

- **Combined degree options** — BE/BA, BE/BCom, BE/BSc are popular. Add 1-2 years for a powerful dual qualification
- **Industry placements** — minimum 12 weeks of professional work experience required
- **Aeronautical Engineering** — one of the few programs in Australia, with wind tunnel facilities and UAV research
- **Innovation and entrepreneurship** — the Sydney Knowledge Hub connects engineering students with startup mentorship
- **Dalyell Scholars stream** — an honours/enrichment program for high-achieving students with special seminars, research, and mentorship
- **Capstone project** — industry-sponsored final year design project

## Campus Life

The University of Sydney's Camperdown campus is 4km from Sydney CBD, easily accessible by bus.

- **Stunning campus** — the Quadrangle (main building) is one of the most photographed university buildings in the world
- **Same city advantages as UNSW** — Sydney's beaches, harbour, culture, and Indian community
- **Large international student body** — over 25% of students are international, with a significant Indian cohort
- **200+ clubs and societies** — including Indian Society, Engineering Society, and discipline-specific groups
- **Sydney is expensive** — similar costs to studying at UNSW. Rent AUD 280-380/week for shared accommodation
- **Inner-west location** — Newtown, Glebe, and Enmore (nearby suburbs) are vibrant, multicultural neighborhoods with affordable dining
- **Sport and fitness** — the university has excellent sports facilities including an Olympic swimming pool
