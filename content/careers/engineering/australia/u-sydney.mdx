## Why Sydney?

The University of Sydney is Australia's oldest university (founded 1850) and one of its most prestigious. The engineering faculty is known for producing well-rounded graduates who excel not just technically but also in communication, leadership, and ethical thinking. Sydney's engineering programs have a strong focus on interdisciplinary work — engineers here regularly collaborate with medical researchers, architects, and business students.

The university's location — a beautiful sandstone campus 3 km from Sydney's CBD — combined with Sydney's status as Australia's financial and commercial capital, means excellent internship and job opportunities. Major companies including Google, Amazon, and all four big Australian banks recruit heavily from Sydney.

For Indian families, the University of Sydney offers the **Sydney Scholars India Scholarship** specifically for Indian students, making it financially competitive.

## How to Get In

The University of Sydney accepts **40-55%** of international engineering applicants — making it accessible for strong students.

**Application process:**
- Apply directly through the University of Sydney portal
- Deadline: **January 15** for Semester 1 (February start); **June 25** for Semester 2 (August start)
- IELTS 6.5+ or TOEFL 85+
- 83-88%+ in Indian boards depending on program
- No entrance exam, no interview, no supplementary application

**The lowest barrier to entry among Australia's top 3 engineering schools.** Combined with the India-specific scholarship, Sydney is an excellent strategic choice.

## Curriculum & Structure

4-year Bachelor of Engineering Honours (BEng Hons). Fully accredited by Engineers Australia.

- **Year 1:** Engineering foundations with some breadth — math, physics, programming, plus electives from other faculties (arts, science, business)
- **Years 2-3:** Core disciplinary courses with increasing specialization
- **Year 4:** Advanced topics + Honours thesis (individual research project)
- **Dalyell Scholars program** — a selective stream for high-achieving students offering enriched coursework, mentoring, and research opportunities
- **Engineering & Commerce double degree** — a popular 5-year option combining engineering with a business degree. Excellent for students interested in management or consulting
- **Industry placement** — strongly encouraged, with partnerships across engineering and tech firms
- **Vertically Integrated Projects (VIPs)** — multi-year research projects where students from all years work together on real problems
- **Study abroad** — exchange agreements with 250+ universities globally, including MIT, Berkeley, and Imperial

## Campus Life

The University of Sydney's Camperdown campus is one of Australia's most beautiful — Gothic sandstone buildings, jacaranda-lined walkways, and a quadrangle modeled on Oxford.

- **Stunning campus** — the Quadrangle is one of the most photographed university buildings in the world. The campus feels like a blend of Oxford and a modern research university
- **Sydney lifestyle** — harbour, beaches, national parks, and world-class dining. The lifestyle is hard to beat
- **Large Indian community** — Sydney's Indian population is Australia's second-largest (after Melbourne). Harris Park near Parramatta is a vibrant "Little India." The university has an active Indian Society
- **Cost of living is high** — Sydney is expensive. Budget AUD 1,800-2,200/month. The university offers some affordable on-campus accommodation, but most students share houses in suburbs like Newtown, Redfern, or Marrickville
- **Sport and recreation** — Sydney University has Australia's strongest university sports program. Cricket, swimming, rowing, and rugby are popular
- **Student Union** — 200+ clubs and societies. The engineering student society (SUEUA) is very active
- **Public transport** — buses, trains, and light rail connect campus to all parts of Sydney
