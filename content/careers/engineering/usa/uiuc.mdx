## Why UIUC?

UIUC's Computer Science department is legendary. It's consistently ranked #5 in the US, and its alumni have shaped the tech industry — Marc Andreessen (co-author of Mosaic/Netscape), Max Levchin (PayPal co-founder), and the creators of YouTube all studied here. UIUC's CS program is on par with Berkeley and CMU, but at public university tuition.

Beyond CS, UIUC's Grainger College of Engineering is top-5 nationally across multiple disciplines. The university is a research powerhouse with the National Center for Supercomputing Applications (NCSA) on campus.

## How to Get In

UIUC admits by major, and **CS is extremely competitive** — roughly 5-7% acceptance rate for international CS applicants. Other engineering majors are more accessible at ~20-30%.

**Critical detail:** UIUC does not allow easy transfers into CS after admission. If your child is admitted to a different major, switching to CS is very difficult. Apply to CS directly if that's the goal.

**Timeline:**
- Early Action: November 1 (non-binding, strongly recommended)
- Regular Decision: January 5

## Curriculum & Structure

4-year Bachelor of Science. Semester system.

- **CS@Illinois** is rigorous — core courses include CS 225 (Data Structures), CS 374 (Algorithms), and CS 421 (Programming Languages)
- **CS + X programs** — unique combined majors: CS + Linguistics, CS + Music, CS + Advertising, CS + Astronomy. These are slightly less competitive to get into
- **Research Park** — 100+ companies have offices on UIUC's Research Park, creating internship opportunities without leaving campus
- **Strong graduate school placement** — many UIUC undergrads go on to PhD programs at MIT, Stanford, Berkeley

## Campus Life

Champaign-Urbana is a classic college town in central Illinois — flat, agricultural, and centered around the university.

- **Indian community is one of the largest** — 4,000+ Indian students. Multiple Indian restaurants, grocery stores. You won't miss home food
- **Very affordable** — rent is $500-800/month. Groceries and dining are cheap
- **Safe and walkable campus** — the campus is flat and well-connected by bus
- **Engineering Open House** (EOH) — annual event where students showcase projects. One of the biggest student-run events in the US
- **Winters are harsh** — Illinois winters bring subzero temperatures and snow. The flat terrain means brutal wind chill
- **Limited big-city access** — Chicago is 2.5 hours north by car/bus. Campus life IS the social life
