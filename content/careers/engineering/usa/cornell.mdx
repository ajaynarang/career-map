## Why Cornell for Engineering?

Cornell is the only Ivy League university with a dedicated College of Engineering — and it shows. While other Ivies treat engineering as a department within arts and sciences, Cornell built an entire college around it, with its own faculty, labs, budget, and admissions. The result: Cornell engineering is consistently ranked top-10 nationally and is especially dominant in computer science, operations research, and materials science.

**What makes Cornell unique for Indian families:** The **Tata Scholarship** — funded by the Tata Education and Development Trust — provides full funding (tuition, room, board, and travel) to approximately 5 Indian students per year across all Cornell colleges. This is one of the most generous India-specific scholarships at any American university. Beyond the Tata Scholarship, Cornell's financial aid meets the demonstrated need of aided international students.

Cornell's CS department is legendary — it's the intellectual home of the internet era. Researchers here built foundational algorithms, contributed to UNIX, and pioneered computational biology. The department is top-5 nationally and produces graduates who are aggressively recruited by every major tech company.

## How to Get In

Cornell's acceptance rate is **7.9%** — competitive but slightly more accessible than MIT/Stanford/Caltech. Engineering applicants apply directly to the College of Engineering.

**What Cornell looks for:**
- **Strong STEM fundamentals** — excellent grades in physics, math, and chemistry
- **Building and creating** — robotics projects, apps, hardware prototypes, research papers
- **"Why Cornell Engineering" essay** — this is critical. Cornell wants to understand why their specific programs, labs, and culture match your child's goals
- **Collaborative and curious** — not just achievers, but students who want to learn broadly (Cornell has a liberal studies requirement even for engineers)

**Timeline:**
- Early Decision: November 1 (binding — if admitted, must attend. Higher acceptance rate ~15-18% for engineering)
- Regular Decision: January 2
- Decisions: Late March

**Tip for Indian applicants:** Apply Early Decision if Cornell is your child's top choice. The ED acceptance rate is roughly double the regular rate. Also, mention the Tata Scholarship in your financial aid application.

## Curriculum & Structure

4-year Bachelor of Science in Engineering. Cornell's engineering curriculum balances technical depth with breadth.

- **First year:** Common engineering curriculum — math (calculus, linear algebra, differential equations), physics, chemistry, computer programming, and an engineering distribution course
- **Year 2:** Major-specific core courses begin. Students formally declare their major
- **Years 3-4:** Advanced technical courses, electives, and a capstone design project
- **Liberal studies requirement:** All Cornell engineers take 6+ courses in humanities, social sciences, and arts. Your child will be a well-rounded thinker, not just a technician
- **Cross-enrollment:** Engineers can take classes in any Cornell college — including the business school (Dyson), hotel school, and arts & sciences
- **Project teams:** Cornell has 30+ student-led engineering project teams — Formula SAE racing, CUAir (autonomous drones), CUSD (sustainable design), Mars Rover, and more. These are serious engineering efforts with corporate sponsors

## Research & Labs

- **Cornell Engineering Research:** Over $200M in annual research expenditure
- **Cornell High Energy Synchrotron Source (CHESS)** — one of the world's leading X-ray research facilities
- **Cornell Nanoscale Science and Technology Facility (CNF)** — national user facility for nanofabrication
- **Autonomous Systems Lab** — self-driving vehicles, drones, robotics
- **Cornell AI and Machine Learning** — one of the strongest research groups in the country
- **Sibley School of Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering** — wind tunnels, fluid dynamics labs, and aerospace design facilities

## Campus Life

- **Ithaca is small but beautiful** — Cornell sits on a hill above Cayuga Lake with dramatic gorges on campus. "Ithaca is gorges" is the local joke
- **Indian community is large** — Cornell has 300+ Indian international students and one of the oldest Indian student associations in the US. The annual Diwali celebration is a major campus event
- **Greek life** — significant at Cornell. About 30% of students join fraternities/sororities
- **The weather is real:** Ithaca winters are harsh — heavy snow from November to March, temperatures well below -10°C. Prepare your child psychologically
- **College town culture** — excellent restaurants, local farms, and a progressive community. Less urban excitement than NYC but strong quality of life
- **NYC connection:** Cornell Tech campus in Manhattan provides NYC internship and networking opportunities. Ithaca is a 4-hour drive or short flight from NYC
- **Slope Day** — end-of-year concert on Libe Slope is a beloved tradition
