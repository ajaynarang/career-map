## Why University of Michigan?

Michigan is the gold standard of public university engineering education. It consistently ranks in the top 5-7 nationally across nearly all engineering disciplines. What sets Michigan apart is the combination of academic excellence, school spirit (the "Michigan experience"), and a powerful alumni network — especially in the automotive and tech industries.

Michigan is one of the few universities offering a dedicated **undergraduate Robotics degree** — a field that's exploding in demand. Its proximity to Detroit means unmatched access to the automotive industry (Ford, GM, Rivian, and dozens of autonomous vehicle companies).

## How to Get In

Michigan's College of Engineering admits roughly **15%** of international applicants.

**What matters:**
- Strong academic record with rigorous STEM courses
- Good test scores (SAT 1480+ or ACT 33+)
- Michigan weighs extracurricular involvement and leadership
- The "Why Michigan" essay is important — show specific knowledge of programs

**Timeline:**
- Early Action: November 1 (non-binding, recommended)
- Regular Decision: February 1

## Curriculum & Structure

4-year Bachelor of Science in Engineering. Semester system.

- **Engineering 100** — first-year course that introduces design thinking and real-world problem-solving
- **Multidisciplinary Design Program (MDP)** — teams of students work on year-long projects with industry sponsors like Ford, Toyota, and NASA
- **Michigan Robotics** — the new Ford Motor Company Robotics Building houses a unique undergraduate robotics program
- **Study abroad engineering** — Michigan offers engineering-specific study abroad in Germany (TU Munich partnership), China, and more
- **SURE Program** — Summer Undergraduate Research in Engineering, providing paid research experience

## Campus Life

Ann Arbor is consistently rated one of the best college towns in America. It's a small city (120,000 people) centered around the university.

- **Indian community is large** — significant Indian student population with active India Association, Sahana (classical dance), and Hindi Film club
- **Ann Arbor is charming** — walkable downtown, excellent restaurants, safe neighborhoods
- **Michigan athletics** — "The Big House" (Michigan Stadium) holds 107,000 people. Football Saturdays are legendary
- **Detroit is 45 minutes away** — access to the automotive industry, museums, and the growing Detroit tech scene
- **Cold winters** — Michigan winters are serious. Heavy snow, temperatures regularly below -10°C
- **Strong sense of community** — the "Michigan Difference" is real. Alumni loyalty is extraordinary
