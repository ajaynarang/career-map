## Why UC Berkeley?

Berkeley is the best public university in the world for engineering. Its EECS program is on par with MIT and Stanford — and it costs significantly less. Berkeley's location in the San Francisco Bay Area gives students direct access to Silicon Valley, and its alumni network in tech is second to none.

What makes Berkeley unique: it's a public university with the research power of a private one. Berkeley has 7 Nobel laureates currently on faculty, the Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory on its doorstep, and a tradition of pushing boundaries — from the discovery of 16 chemical elements to the development of UNIX and BSD.

## How to Get In

Berkeley receives **130,000+ applications** per year — the most of any US university. International admission is highly competitive.

**Key differences from private universities:**
- Berkeley uses the **UC Application** (not Common App) — shared across all UC campuses
- **No interviews** — the application (essays + grades + activities) is everything
- **No Early Decision/Action** — single deadline of November 30 for fall admission
- **EECS is a separate major** in the College of Engineering (direct admission, ~4% accept rate). CS in the College of Letters & Science is slightly less selective but still very competitive

**For Indian applicants:** Berkeley is need-aware for international students and has limited financial aid for non-US citizens. Budget for full cost unless you have external scholarships.

## Curriculum & Structure

4-year Bachelor of Science (Engineering) or Bachelor of Arts (L&S CS).

- **EECS vs. L&S CS:** EECS (College of Engineering) is a combined EE+CS degree with direct admission. L&S CS (College of Letters & Science) is a CS-only degree where you declare the major after completing prerequisites
- **Legendary courses:** CS 61A, CS 61B, CS 61C (the intro CS series) and CS 170 (Algorithms) are world-famous, with lectures available free on YouTube
- **Research opportunities:** URAP (Undergraduate Research Apprenticeship Program) connects students with faculty research
- **Strong graduate school pipeline** — many Berkeley undergrads go on to PhD programs at top schools

## Campus Life

Berkeley's campus sits in the hills east of San Francisco Bay. The city of Berkeley is vibrant, progressive, and walkable.

- **Indian community is huge** — Berkeley has one of the largest South Asian student populations. Indian Students Association (ISA), Indus, and multiple cultural organizations are active
- **Bay Area access** — San Francisco is 30 minutes by BART (train). Silicon Valley is 45 minutes south
- **Weather is mild** — Mediterranean climate, much better than East Coast. Rarely below 5°C
- **Cal culture** — football games at Memorial Stadium, hiking in Tilden Park, Telegraph Avenue
- **Housing is expensive** — Berkeley/Oakland housing costs are high. Many students live in co-ops (shared housing) to save money
