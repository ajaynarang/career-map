## Why Georgia Tech?

Georgia Tech is the best value proposition in American engineering education. It's a top-10 engineering school globally with tuition roughly **half** of MIT or Stanford. Its Industrial Engineering program is #1 in the US, CS is top-5, and Aerospace is top-5. The co-op program (alternating semesters of study and full-time work) means many students graduate with 1-2 years of real work experience.

For Indian families who want top-tier engineering education without the $85K/year price tag of private universities, Georgia Tech is the answer. At ~$56,000/year total cost, it's still expensive for Indian standards, but the ROI is excellent.

## How to Get In

Georgia Tech's acceptance rate for international students is around **16%** — competitive but significantly more accessible than MIT or Stanford.

**What they look for:**
- Very strong math and science grades — Georgia Tech is unapologetically STEM-focused
- SAT Math score matters more than verbal for engineering applicants
- Demonstrated interest in engineering through projects, internships, or competitions
- Georgia Tech uses its **own application portal** (not Common App)

**Timeline:**
- Early Action 1: October 15
- Early Action 2: November 1
- Regular Decision: January 4

## Curriculum & Structure

4-year Bachelor of Science. Georgia Tech is on a **semester system**.

- **Threads model (CS):** Instead of a single CS major, students choose two "threads" from eight options (Intelligence, Systems Architecture, Information Internetworks, etc.). This creates a customized degree
- **Co-op program:** Alternate semesters of study and full-time paid work. Students graduate in 5 years but with 12-18 months of work experience and significant earnings
- **VIP (Vertically Integrated Projects):** Multi-year, multi-disciplinary team projects where freshmen work alongside PhD students on real research
- **Strong aerospace program:** Partnerships with NASA, Boeing, Lockheed Martin

## Campus Life

400-acre campus in Midtown Atlanta. Georgia Tech is an urban campus in a major city.

- **Atlanta is a hub** — CNN, Coca-Cola, Delta, UPS, and dozens of tech companies are headquartered here. Internship access is excellent
- **Indian community is strong** — India Club, Bollywood dance teams, and a large Indian graduate student population
- **Cost of living is reasonable** — Atlanta is significantly cheaper than San Francisco, New York, or Boston
- **Weather is warm** — hot summers, mild winters. Much more comfortable than northern cities
- **Football and basketball** — Georgia Tech is an ACC school. Sports culture is part of the experience
- **MARTA** — Atlanta's public transit connects campus to the city (though a car helps)
