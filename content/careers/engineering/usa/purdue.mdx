## Why Purdue?

Purdue is the most "Indian-friendly" top engineering school in America. It has the **largest international student population** among US public universities, and a huge Indian community. If your child wants a top-tier engineering education at a more accessible price point and acceptance rate, Purdue is an excellent choice.

Purdue's engineering school is ranked #4-6 nationally. Its Aerospace Engineering program has produced 25 astronauts (more than any other university) — including Neil Armstrong. Mechanical, Chemical, and Industrial Engineering are all top-10 nationally.

## How to Get In

Purdue's overall acceptance rate is ~49%, but **engineering is more competitive** at roughly 30%. For popular majors like CS, the admit rate for international students is even lower.

**What matters:**
- Strong math scores (SAT Math 750+ or ACT Math 33+)
- Good academic record with rigorous science courses
- Early application is strongly recommended

**Important:** Purdue admits by major. If CS is full, you may be offered admission to another engineering major. You can then try to transfer into CS (but this is competitive).

**Timeline:**
- Early Action: November 1 (recommended for scholarships)
- Regular Decision: January 15

## Curriculum & Structure

4-year Bachelor of Science. Semester system.

- **FYE (First-Year Engineering):** All engineering students start in a common first year, then apply to their specific major. GPA matters for getting your preferred major
- **Co-op and internship programs** are strong — many students do 2-3 internships before graduating
- **Purdue Online** resources are extensive — many lecture recordings available
- **Boeing, Rolls-Royce, and Cummins** have research partnerships directly with Purdue
- **Discovery Park** — Purdue's interdisciplinary research complex for nanotechnology, sustainable energy, and bioengineering

## Campus Life

Purdue's campus is in West Lafayette, a small college town in central Indiana. This is very different from a city campus.

- **Indian community is massive** — Purdue has 3,000+ Indian students. You'll find Indian grocery stores, restaurants, and cultural events throughout the area
- **Cost of living is low** — West Lafayette is one of the cheapest college towns in the US. Rent is $400-700/month
- **Safe and quiet** — small-town America. Very safe, very friendly, but limited big-city amenities
- **Boilermaker culture** — Purdue's sports teams are competitive (Big Ten conference). Game days are a big deal
- **Cold winters** — Indiana winters are cold (-15°C is common). Prepare accordingly
- **Driving helps** — while campus is walkable, a car is useful for grocery shopping and travel
