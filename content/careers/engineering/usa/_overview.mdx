## Why the USA for Engineering?

The United States has dominated global engineering education for decades. If your child gets into a top-20 US engineering school, they will have access to the best research labs, Silicon Valley internships, and a career network that spans the world. The starting salaries for engineering graduates — especially in CS, EE, and related fields — are the highest globally, often $80,000-$150,000+ (₹65 LPA-1.2 Crore+).

**The investment is real:** A 4-year degree at a top US university will cost ₹2-3 Crore. But many universities offer need-based financial aid to international students (MIT, Stanford, and Princeton meet 100% of demonstrated need). If your child gets in and your family qualifies, the actual cost can be significantly lower.

## The Path

1. **Class 9-10:** Build a strong extracurricular profile — the US values well-rounded applicants, not just exam scores
2. **Class 11:** Take SAT/ACT (aim for 1500+ SAT for top schools). Start TOEFL/IELTS preparation
3. **Class 12 (August-January):** Apply via Common App or Coalition App. Early Action deadlines: November 1. Regular Decision: January 1-15
4. **March-April of 12th:** Admission decisions arrive. Commit by May 1
5. **Fall semester starts:** August-September

## What Parents Should Know

- **Holistic admissions.** Unlike India's exam-only system, US universities evaluate grades, test scores, essays, extracurriculars, recommendation letters, and "fit." A perfect SAT score alone won't guarantee admission.
- **Financial aid exists but is competitive.** Only a handful of top universities are "need-blind" for international students (MIT, Harvard, Yale, Princeton, Amherst). Most are "need-aware" — meaning applying for aid can hurt your chances.
- **The 4-year liberal arts model is different.** Your child will take courses outside engineering (humanities, social sciences) in the first 1-2 years. This is by design — it produces well-rounded thinkers.
- **OPT + STEM OPT = 3 years of work** after graduation without needing an H-1B visa. This is a major advantage for engineering graduates who want to work in the US.
- **H-1B visa lottery** is a reality. After OPT, your child will need employer sponsorship and must go through a lottery system. It's not guaranteed, but top employers routinely sponsor.
- **Community matters.** Every major US university has an Indian student association. Your child will find a support system.
