## Why Carnegie Mellon?

If MIT is the king of engineering, Carnegie Mellon is the king of computer science. CMU's School of Computer Science is arguably the best CS program in the world — it pioneered AI research (the term "artificial intelligence" was coined here), robotics, and human-computer interaction.

For Indian students interested in software engineering, AI/ML, or robotics, CMU offers something few other universities can: direct admission into a dedicated CS school (not a department within an engineering college). The curriculum is deeper and more specialized than almost any competitor.

## How to Get In

CMU admits by school/college. The **School of Computer Science (SCS)** has a ~5% acceptance rate — more selective than MIT overall.

**What they value:**
- Strong math and CS background — AP CS, math olympiad, competitive programming
- **Projects matter more than grades** — CMU wants to see what you've built
- Clear articulation of why CMU specifically (not just "it's top-ranked")

**Application specifics:**
- CMU uses the Common App but has its own supplemental essays
- You apply to a specific school/college (SCS, Engineering, etc.) — choose wisely
- Early Decision: November 1 (binding) — significantly higher admit rate
- Regular Decision: January 1

## Curriculum & Structure

4-year Bachelor of Science. CMU operates on a **semester system**.

- **SCS curriculum is intense** — courses like 15-251 (Great Theoretical Ideas in CS), 15-213 (Computer Systems), and 15-451 (Algorithm Design) are legendary
- **Research is accessible** — the Robotics Institute, Language Technologies Institute, and Machine Learning Department are all within SCS
- **Interdisciplinary minors** — Human-Computer Interaction, Robotics, ML are available as minors
- **BXA programs** — unique combined degrees with the College of Fine Arts (CS + Art, CS + Music)
- **Workload is heavy** — CMU students call it "Carnegie Mellon" for a reason. Sleep is a luxury

## Campus Life

Pittsburgh is an affordable, livable city — a stark contrast to San Francisco or New York. The campus sits in the Oakland neighborhood, adjacent to the University of Pittsburgh.

- **Indian community is significant** — CMU has one of the highest percentages of Indian students among top US universities. Indian Graduate Students Association (IGSA) is very active
- **Pittsburgh is affordable** — rent, food, and entertainment cost 40-50% less than Bay Area or NYC
- **Tech scene is growing** — Google, Apple, Uber, and Duolingo all have Pittsburgh offices, partly because of CMU
- **Buggy (Sweepstakes)** — CMU's unique racing tradition where student organizations build and race unpowered vehicles
- **Winter is real** — Pittsburgh gets cold and grey (November-March). Factor this into your decision
