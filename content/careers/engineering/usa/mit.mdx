## Why MIT?

MIT is the world's #1 engineering school — full stop. It's not just a university; it's a culture of invention. The people who built the internet, designed modern cryptography, and pioneered artificial intelligence walked these halls. Your child won't just learn engineering — they'll be expected to push the boundaries of what's possible.

What makes MIT special for Indian families: MIT is **need-blind for international students**. If your child gets in, MIT guarantees to meet 100% of your family's demonstrated financial need. A family earning under $75,000/year pays nothing. This makes MIT potentially more affordable than many private Indian universities.

## How to Get In

MIT's acceptance rate is **3.9%** — roughly 1 in 25 applicants. For Indian applicants, it's even more competitive due to country-level quotas in the applicant pool.

**What MIT actually looks for:**
- Academic excellence is the baseline, not the differentiator. Nearly every admitted student has near-perfect scores.
- **"Match"** — MIT wants builders, makers, tinkerers. If your child has built robots, published research, won science olympiads, or created something real, that matters enormously.
- **Character** — collaboration over competition. MIT rejects brilliant students who seem like lone wolves.

**Timeline:**
- Early Action: November 1 (non-binding, recommended)
- Regular Action: January 1
- Decisions: Mid-March (EA: mid-December)

**Reality check:** Indian students who get into MIT typically have International Olympiad medals (Physics, Math, Chemistry, Informatics), published research, or truly exceptional projects. Good JEE coaching alone is not enough.

## Curriculum & Structure

4-year Bachelor of Science. MIT uses a unique course numbering system (Course 6 = EECS, Course 2 = MechE).

- **First year is pass/no-record** in the fall semester — takes pressure off the transition
- **UROP (Undergraduate Research Opportunities Program)** — 85%+ of MIT students do research with faculty, many starting freshman year
- **Cross-registration with Harvard** — your child can take classes at Harvard
- **IAP (January term)** — a month of workshops, hackathons, and short courses between semesters
- **Strong project-based learning** — 2.007 (MechE design) and 6.01 (intro to EECS) are legendary hands-on courses

## Campus Life

MIT sits along the Charles River in Cambridge, across from Boston. The campus is urban but self-contained.

- **Indian community is strong** — MIT has 200+ Indian international students and an active Indian Students Association (ISA) and Sangam
- **Living groups** are unique — students choose from traditional dorms, fraternities/sororities, or independent living groups, each with distinct cultures
- **Hacking culture** — elaborate (and technically illegal) pranks are a tradition. Students have placed a fire truck on top of the Great Dome
- **Boston is a college city** — 50+ universities in the area. Vibrant Indian food scene in Cambridge and Somerville
- **Weather warning:** Boston winters are harsh (November-March). Temperatures regularly drop below -10°C
