## Why Stanford?

Stanford is the birthplace of Silicon Valley. Google, Hewlett-Packard, Cisco, Sun Microsystems, Instagram, and Snapchat were all founded by Stanford students or faculty. If your child dreams of building the next big tech company or working at the frontier of AI, robotics, or clean energy — there is no better place on Earth.

Stanford's engineering school is ranked #2 globally and #1 in multiple sub-fields (CS, AI, EE). But what truly sets Stanford apart is its culture of entrepreneurship. Students don't just study engineering — they build companies while still in school.

## How to Get In

Stanford admits **3.6%** of applicants — making it the most selective university in the world. For international students, it's even harder.

**What matters:**
- Perfect or near-perfect grades and test scores (these are necessary but not sufficient)
- **"Intellectual vitality"** — Stanford's term for genuine curiosity and passion. They want students who learn for the joy of it
- **Impact** — what has your child done that changed something? A community, a field, a group of people?
- **Recommendation letters** — Stanford reads these carefully. Strong teacher endorsements matter

**Important for Indian applicants:** Stanford is need-aware for international students, meaning applying for financial aid can affect admission chances. However, they are still generous — many Indian students receive significant aid packages.

**Timeline:**
- Restrictive Early Action: November 1
- Regular Decision: January 2
- Decisions: December (REA), late March (RD)

## Curriculum & Structure

4-year Bachelor of Science. Stanford operates on a **quarter system** (fall, winter, spring + optional summer).

- **No core curriculum** — Stanford gives students enormous freedom in course selection
- **Coterm program** — complete a Master's degree alongside the Bachelor's in 5 years (very popular in CS and EE)
- **d.school (Hasso Plattner Institute of Design)** — Stanford's famous design thinking program. Open to all engineering students
- **Strong interdisciplinary options** — Symbolic Systems, CS + Philosophy, BioE + Pre-Med
- **Cardinal Quarter** — study abroad for a full quarter at Stanford's global campuses

## Campus Life

8,180-acre campus in the heart of Silicon Valley. The campus is stunning — sandstone buildings, palm trees, and the California sun.

- **Weather is perfect** — 300+ sunny days per year. A huge contrast to the US East Coast
- **Indian community is massive** — Stanford has one of the largest Indian student populations among top US universities. Asha for Education, SASA (South Asian Student Association), and Indian Cultural Association are very active
- **StartX** — Stanford's startup accelerator. Students launch companies while still studying
- **Athletics culture** — Stanford is a Division I school. Cardinal sports are a big part of campus life
- **Cost of living is HIGH** — Palo Alto/Bay Area is one of the most expensive places in the US. This is factored into financial aid calculations
