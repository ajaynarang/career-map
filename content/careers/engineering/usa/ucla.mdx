## Why UCLA for Engineering?

UCLA is the most applied-to university in the United States — and for good reason. The Henry Samueli School of Engineering and Applied Science combines world-class research, a location in the tech capital of the West Coast, and a campus culture that's vibrant, diverse, and deeply collaborative. UCLA engineering is top-15 nationally, with particular strength in computer science, electrical engineering, and bioengineering.

**The LA advantage is real.** UCLA sits in Westwood, surrounded by Silicon Beach (home to Google's LA office, Snap, TikTok's US HQ, and hundreds of startups), the aerospace corridor (SpaceX, Northrop Grumman, Boeing), and the entertainment technology industry. Internship and job opportunities are practically on campus. No other top engineering school has this combination of tech, aerospace, and creative industry access.

**For Indian families, UCLA offers a compelling value proposition.** As a public university, UCLA's tuition is significantly lower than private peers — roughly $46,000/year versus $65,000+ at MIT or Stanford. The Regents Scholarship can cover full non-resident tuition, making UCLA comparable in cost to top Indian private universities. And LA has one of the largest Indian diaspora communities in the US, with extensive cultural, food, and community support.

## How to Get In

UCLA's acceptance rate for engineering is approximately **6-7%** — extremely competitive for a public university.

**Key details for Indian applicants:**
- **UC Application (not Common App):** Apply through the University of California system at [apply.universityofcalifornia.edu](https://apply.universityofcalifornia.edu). Deadline: November 30
- **Test-free:** The UC system no longer considers SAT/ACT scores. Admission is based on GPA, coursework rigor, extracurriculars, and the UC Personal Insight Questions (PIQs)
- **8 PIQs (Personal Insight Questions):** You answer 4 of 8 prompts. These are UCLA's version of essays — shorter (350 words each) but equally important. They must show depth, not breadth
- **No Early Decision/Action:** All UC applications are Regular Decision. Decisions arrive in March

**What UCLA Engineering looks for:**
- **Academic excellence** — 3.9+ GPA with the most rigorous available coursework (AP, IB, or equivalent)
- **Impact beyond academics** — community projects, engineering clubs, research internships
- **Diversity of perspective** — UCLA values students who bring unique experiences and viewpoints
- **Fit with UCLA's collaborative culture** — team-oriented, interdisciplinary thinkers

**Reality check:** UCLA receives 175,000+ applications across all programs — more than any university on Earth. The engineering school is even more selective. Indian applicants are competitive, but the process is holistic, not score-driven.

## Curriculum & Structure

4-year Bachelor of Science. UCLA engineering is structured to build both depth and breadth.

- **First year:** Math (calculus, linear algebra), physics, chemistry, and introductory programming. Engineering fundamentals course introduces all majors
- **Year 2:** Major-specific core courses. CS students begin data structures, algorithms, and systems. Mechanical engineering students start thermodynamics and dynamics
- **Years 3-4:** Advanced electives, technical breadth, and capstone design project
- **Computer Science:** UCLA CS is top-10 nationally. Strengths in AI/ML, computer vision, cybersecurity, and systems. The department has close ties to the tech industry in LA
- **Capstone design projects:** Senior year culminates in a team-based engineering project, often sponsored by industry partners (SpaceX, NASA, Google)
- **Research opportunities:** UCLA's Undergraduate Research Centers connect students with faculty research from freshman year. The Engineering Research Scholars program is competitively selected
- **Quarter system:** UCLA runs on quarters (10 weeks each), not semesters. The pace is fast — exams come quickly. Students take 3-4 courses per quarter

## Research & Labs

- **Internet was born here:** UCLA's Leonard Kleinrock sent the first ARPANET message in 1969. The room where it happened (Boelter Hall 3420) is now a museum
- **WINLAB (Wireless Networking Lab)** — 5G/6G, IoT, and next-gen communications
- **Center for Vision, Cognition, Learning, and Autonomy** — computer vision and AI research (Judea Pearl, Turing Award winner, is faculty)
- **Plasma and Beam Physics Lab** — fusion energy, particle accelerators
- **California NanoSystems Institute (CNSI)** — nanotechnology, materials, and bionanotechnology
- **Smart Grid Energy Research Center** — renewable energy systems and grid optimization

## Campus Life

- **UCLA's campus is stunning** — 419 acres of parkland in Westwood, 5 miles from the Pacific Ocean. The Royce Hall quad is iconic
- **Indian community is massive** — UCLA has one of the largest Indian student populations of any US university. Active Indian Students Association, Diwali celebrations, Holi festival, and garba nights. LA's Little India in Artesia is 30 minutes away
- **Weather** — year-round sunshine, 70-80°F average. Your child will not miss Indian weather
- **Athletics culture** — UCLA has won 119 NCAA championships, more than any other university. Bruins football and basketball games are major social events
- **Dining** — UCLA is consistently ranked #1 for campus dining in the US. Extensive vegetarian and Indian food options
- **Safety** — Westwood is an affluent, safe neighbourhood. UCLA has its own police department and comprehensive safety services
- **Cost of living** — LA is expensive. Budget $15,000-18,000/year for room and board if living off-campus
