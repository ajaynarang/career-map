## Why UT Austin?

UT Austin is emerging as a tech powerhouse. Austin has become America's newest tech hub — Tesla moved its headquarters here, Apple is building a $1 billion campus, Google and Meta have major offices, and Samsung operates its largest chip fabrication plant outside South Korea nearby. For engineering students, this means unparalleled access to internships and jobs.

The Cockrell School of Engineering is top-10 nationally, with particular strengths in Petroleum Engineering (#1 in the US), ECE, CS, and Aerospace.

## How to Get In

UT Austin is moderately selective for international engineering students (~15% acceptance rate for Cockrell School).

**Important details:**
- UT Austin uses **ApplyTexas** or the **Common App**
- You apply directly to the Cockrell School of Engineering
- Internal transfers into engineering are very difficult — apply directly
- **No Early Decision/Action** — single deadline of December 1

**For Indian applicants:** UT Austin has limited financial aid for international students. Most Indian students pay full cost. However, the total cost (~₹50L/year) is still lower than private universities.

## Curriculum & Structure

4-year Bachelor of Science. Semester system.

- **Turing Scholars Program** — honors CS program with smaller class sizes and research focus
- **Integrated BSc/MSc** — 5-year combined degree options in several engineering disciplines
- **Entrepreneurship ecosystem** — Austin is a startup city, and UT's Longhorn Startup Lab connects students with the ecosystem
- **Strong industry partnerships** — Texas Instruments, Dell, Samsung, and NXP recruit heavily from UT
- **Study abroad programs** — engineering-specific programs in Germany, South Korea, and more

## Campus Life

UT Austin's 430-acre campus is in the heart of Austin, one of America's most vibrant cities.

- **Austin is incredible** — live music capital of the world, amazing food scene (including great Indian restaurants on the North side), outdoor activities (swimming holes, hiking)
- **Indian community is growing** — significant Indian student population with active cultural organizations
- **Weather is hot** — Austin summers hit 40°C+. Mild winters. If your child hates cold, this is the place
- **Longhorn culture** — UT football is massive. Game days are an experience (100,000+ stadium)
- **Cost of living is moderate** — lower than Bay Area or NYC, but Austin has gotten more expensive recently
- **No state income tax** — Texas has no state income tax, which benefits working graduates
