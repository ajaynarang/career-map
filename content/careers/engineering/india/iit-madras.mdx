## Why IIT Madras?

IIT Madras holds the distinction of being India's #1 university overall according to NIRF — not just in engineering, but across all disciplines. This is a university that has consistently outperformed every other institution in the country for research output, industry collaboration, and student outcomes.

What sets IIT Madras apart is its research culture. The campus houses India's first university-based research park, home to 100+ startups and R&D centers of companies like Boeing, Rolls-Royce, and Robert Bosch. Your child won't just study engineering — they'll work alongside industry professionals from their second year onwards.

CS graduates from IIT Madras command packages of ₹35-45 LPA on average, with top offers exceeding ₹1 Crore from firms like Google, Jane Street, and Tower Research.

## How to Get In

The path is **JEE Advanced** — same as all IITs.

1. Clear JEE Main and finish in the top 2.5 lakh
2. Score well in JEE Advanced (held in May-June)
3. Rank determines branch: **< 150 for CS**, < 600 for Electrical, < 1500 for Mechanical

**What parents should know:** IIT Madras CS cutoffs are slightly more accessible than IIT Bombay, but the placement outcomes are nearly identical. If your child's rank is between 100-150, IIT Madras CS is arguably the best value decision they can make.

## Curriculum & Structure

4-year BTech with flexibility built in:

- **Research Park access** — students can intern at on-campus startups and R&D labs
- **Strong interdisciplinary options** — minors in Data Science, AI, Entrepreneurship
- **BS + MS dual degree** option in select departments
- **Semester abroad** partnerships with universities like RWTH Aachen, NUS Singapore, and TU Munich
- **NPTEL** — India's largest online learning platform was built here

## Campus Life

620 acres of lush greenery inside the Guindy National Park — the only IIT campus with deer and blackbucks roaming freely.

- **Saarang** — one of India's biggest cultural festivals
- **Shaastra** — Asia's largest student-run technical festival
- **CFI (Centre for Innovation)** — a 24/7 maker space where students build satellites, race cars, and autonomous robots
- **IIT Madras Research Park** — students walk across to intern at real companies
- **Climate:** Chennai is hot, but the campus is heavily wooded and cooler than the city
