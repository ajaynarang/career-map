## Why Jadavpur University?

Jadavpur University (JU) is Eastern India's finest engineering college and one of India's best-kept secrets. It's a government university that costs less than ₹1 lakh for the entire 4-year BTech — yet its alumni are at Google, Microsoft, Goldman Sachs, and leading companies worldwide. On a pure ROI basis, JU may be the single best engineering college in India.

**The numbers speak:** At under ₹25,000 per year total cost, JU produces graduates who earn ₹10-28 LPA starting salaries. No private college comes close to this value.

**For Mechanical and Civil Engineering**, JU is particularly strong — it's consistently ranked among India's top 10 for these branches. The Mechanical Engineering department has produced some of India's most accomplished engineers, and the Civil Engineering programme has deep ties with India's infrastructure sector.

**The culture is different from IITs.** JU has a fierce intellectual tradition rooted in Kolkata's progressive, questioning culture. Students here don't just study engineering — they debate politics, participate in cultural movements, and develop a worldview. JU alumni often say the university shaped their thinking more than their technical skills.

## How to Get In

JU admits through two exams:

1. **WBJEE (60% seats):** The West Bengal state entrance exam. Top 200 for CS, top 1000 for ECE.
2. **JEE Main (40% seats):** A separate quota for JEE Main rank holders.

**WBJEE route (easier for most students):**
- Appear for WBJEE in April
- Rank under 3000 for most branches at JU
- CS requires rank under 200 — competitive but achievable

**JEE Main route:**
- JEE Main percentile of 97+ for CS at JU
- 95+ for other good branches

**Reality check:** JU is the most selective government college in Eastern India. About 1 lakh students take WBJEE, and JU CS has about 60 seats. But compared to IIT admissions, this is very achievable with focused preparation.

## What Makes It Special

- **Absurdly affordable** — under ₹1L for the full degree. This is a government university that's practically free
- **Strong alumni network** in Kolkata's industry and across global tech companies
- **Research culture** is stronger than most private colleges — JU faculty publish in top journals
- **Kolkata advantage** — rich cultural life, affordable city, great food, and intellectual atmosphere
- **Mechanical and Civil departments** are among India's oldest and most respected

## Campus and Student Life

Two campuses — the main campus in Jadavpur and the Salt Lake campus:

- **Jadavpur campus** is the engineering hub — historic, green, and located in South Kolkata
- **Cultural festivals** — JU's Sanskriti, tech fests, and department festivals are vibrant
- **Political and intellectual culture** — JU is known for its activist, questioning student body. This isn't for everyone, but for the right student, it's transformative
- **Kolkata city life** — world-class food (₹30 rolls to Park Street dining), metro connectivity, cultural events, book fairs, and film festivals
- **Hostel availability is limited** — most outstation students find PGs or hostels nearby at ₹5,000-8,000/month
- **Canteen culture** is legendary — the JU canteen is where ideas, friendships, and startups are born
