## Why Manipal?

Manipal Institute of Technology (MIT Manipal) is one of India's oldest private engineering colleges, established in 1957 — decades before most private colleges existed. It has a unique legacy: some of India's most successful entrepreneurs, including the founders of Infosys (NR Narayana Murthy's peers), are Manipal alumni.

**What makes Manipal genuinely different from other private colleges:**

- **The town IS the university.** Manipal is a small university town in coastal Karnataka. The entire town revolves around the university. There are no distractions of a big city, but the campus life is incredibly rich
- **International culture** — Manipal has students from 50+ countries. Walking around campus feels more international than most Indian colleges
- **Strong alumni loyalty** — Manipal alumni are fiercely loyal and actively help juniors with internships, jobs, and startup funding
- **Holistic development** — unlike purely placement-focused colleges, Manipal emphasizes sports, arts, and personal growth alongside academics

**The honest take:** Manipal's placements are good but not exceptional. The average CS package (₹12-20 LPA for top students) is lower than IITs or even VIT's best. But the overall experience — campus life, international exposure, personal growth — is arguably better than any other private college in India.

## How to Get In

The sole pathway is **MET (Manipal Entrance Test)**:

1. Register at [manipal.edu/mu/admissions](https://manipal.edu/mu/admissions.html) — registration opens November
2. Choose an exam date (April-May, multiple slots available)
3. Appear for MET — online, 2.5 hours, no negative marking
4. Counselling based on MET rank

**Cutoffs (approximate):**
- CS: MET score > 140 (out of 200)
- ECE: > 100
- Mechanical: > 70
- Most other branches: > 50

**Tip:** Register early and pick a comfortable exam slot. MET allows you to choose your date and time, reducing pressure.

## What Makes It Special

- **University town experience** — Manipal is a self-contained world. Everything your child needs is within walking distance
- **60+ years of heritage** — alumni network is massive and genuinely helpful
- **International students** — exposure to global perspectives without leaving India
- **Strong in Aeronautical Engineering** — Manipal's aero programme is one of the best outside IITs
- **Medical + engineering campus** — Kasturba Medical College is right next door, creating a unique interdisciplinary culture
- **Coastal Karnataka location** — beaches, temples, and natural beauty. Great weather year-round

## Campus and Student Life

600-acre campus in Manipal town, Udupi district, Karnataka:

- **TechTatva** — Manipal's annual tech festival, one of South India's largest
- **Revels** — cultural festival with national-level participation
- **150+ student organizations** — coding clubs, robotics, music bands, theatre groups, adventure sports
- **End Point** — the iconic campus hangout spot overlooking the valley and Arabian Sea
- **Sports infrastructure** — Marena (India's largest student activity center), Olympic pool, indoor stadium, multiple playing fields
- **Food scene** — campus canteens, plus Manipal town has excellent South Indian food, cafes, and restaurants. Affordable eating out
- **Hostel life** is mandatory for first-year students — rooms are well-maintained, and the hostel culture is a big part of the Manipal experience
- **Safety** — Manipal is one of the safest college towns in India. Parents can be at ease
