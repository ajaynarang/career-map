## Why IIT Bombay?

IIT Bombay is India's #1 engineering college — and it's not just about the ranking. It's about what happens after: Google, Goldman Sachs, and Jane Street recruit directly from campus. CS graduates routinely receive offers of ₹40-55 LPA, with some international offers crossing ₹1 Crore.

But IIT Bombay isn't just about placements. It's a place where your child will be surrounded by the brightest minds in the country, build lifelong friendships, and develop problem-solving skills that last a career.

## How to Get In

There's only one path: **JEE Advanced**.

1. First, clear JEE Main (January or April session) — your child needs to be in the top 2.5 lakh qualifiers
2. Then, appear for JEE Advanced (May-June)
3. Rank determines branch: **< 100 for CS**, < 500 for Electrical, < 1500 for Mechanical

**Reality check:** About 10 lakh students take JEE Main. About 2.5 lakh qualify for Advanced. About 16,000 get IIT seats across all IITs. IIT Bombay CS takes roughly the top 70-80 students. It's extremely competitive.

**But don't let that discourage you.** Even rank 3000-5000 gets you into IIT Bombay in branches like Engineering Physics or Energy Science — and these students can still get excellent placements.

## Curriculum & Structure

4-year BTech with a common first year. After the first year, your child picks their specialization. Key features:

- **Flexible minors and honors** — a Mechanical student can do a minor in CS
- **Research projects** from 2nd year onwards — many students publish papers
- **Strong coding culture** — regardless of branch, most students learn to code
- **Semester exchange** programs with MIT, Stanford, ETH Zurich

## Campus Life

550 acres on the banks of Powai Lake in Mumbai. The campus is essentially a small city:

- **Mood Indigo** — Asia's largest college cultural festival
- **Techfest** — one of the biggest tech festivals globally
- **20+ technical clubs** — robotics, coding, finance, astronomy
- **Hostel life** is a huge part of the experience — midnight discussions, competitive coding sessions, and lifelong bonds
