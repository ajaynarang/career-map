## Why IIT Kharagpur?

IIT Kharagpur is the oldest IIT — the one where it all began in 1951. It is also the largest, spread across a massive 2100-acre campus that is essentially a self-sufficient town. With over 20 departments and the widest range of programs among all IITs, IIT Kharagpur offers your child options that simply don't exist elsewhere.

Want to study Ocean Engineering? Only IIT Kharagpur. Architecture at an IIT? Only Kharagpur and Roorkee. Mining Engineering? Agricultural Engineering? IIT Kharagpur has them all, alongside powerhouse departments in CS, Electrical, and Mechanical.

CS placements are excellent at ₹30-45 LPA on average, and IIT Kharagpur's placement season is one of the largest in Asia — 300+ companies visit campus each year. The alumni network is vast and deeply loyal, with strong representation across every major tech company and several Fortune 500 firms.

Sundar Pichai, the CEO of Google and Alphabet, is an IIT Kharagpur alumnus.

## How to Get In

**JEE Advanced** — same path as all IITs.

- CS requires rank **< 300**
- EE/ECE: rank **< 1200**
- Other popular branches: rank < 3000-5000
- Unique branches (Agriculture, Mining, Ocean): rank < 8000-10000

**Parent perspective:** IIT Kharagpur has more seats than most IITs and the widest range of branches. Even at JEE Advanced rank 5000-8000, your child can get into IIT Kharagpur in a good branch. For students who want the IIT tag with slightly more accessible cutoffs, Kharagpur is a strong choice.

## Curriculum & Structure

4-year BTech (with 5-year Dual Degree options). What makes IIT Kharagpur unique:

- **Widest program range** — 20+ departments covering everything from CS to Agriculture to Law
- **5-year Dual Degree** — BTech + MTech in 5 years, saving one year compared to doing both separately
- **Rajendra Mishra School of Engineering Entrepreneurship** — a full school dedicated to startup education
- **Strong interdisciplinary culture** — students regularly take courses across departments
- **Law school on campus** — one of the few IITs with a law program, enabling unique dual qualifications
- **Semester exchange** with MIT, Stanford, Georgia Tech, and leading European universities

## Campus Life

2100 acres — the largest campus of any IIT, larger than many small towns:

- **Spring Fest** — Asia's largest social and cultural festival, drawing 75,000+ visitors
- **Kshitij** — one of India's biggest techno-management festivals
- **Gopali Youth Fest** — inter-IIT cultural event
- **Technology Students' Gymkhana** — one of the most active student governing bodies in India
- **40+ halls of residence** — each with its own identity, traditions, and inter-hall competitions
- **Self-contained campus** — hospital, shopping center, schools, post office, bank, and more
- **Kharagpur** is a small town, which means campus life is everything. Students develop deep bonds and the close-knit community is a lifelong asset
- **Kolkata** is 2 hours away by train for weekend trips
