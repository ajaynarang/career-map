## Why NIT Trichy?

NIT Trichy (NITT) is the undisputed #1 NIT in India and consistently ranks among the top 10 engineering colleges nationally. For parents, the value proposition is remarkable: tuition at ₹1.5L/year — roughly one-third of an IIT and one-fourth of BITS Pilani — with CS placements reaching ₹22-35 LPA.

Here's what makes NIT Trichy special: it's the best option for students who perform exceptionally in JEE Main but don't crack JEE Advanced. A JEE Main percentile of 99.5+ gets your child into NIT Trichy CS, and the career outcomes are comparable to mid-tier IITs.

NIT Trichy also has the strongest alumni network among all NITs. Graduates are well-represented at Google, Microsoft, Amazon, and Goldman Sachs. The brand recognition is strong enough that recruiters treat NITT on par with many IITs.

## How to Get In

Admission is through **JEE Main** (not JEE Advanced).

- CS requires JEE Main percentile **99.5+ (Home State)** or **99.8+ (Other State)**
- ECE: 99+ percentile
- Other branches: 97-99 percentile

**Home State vs Other State:** NITs reserve 50% seats for students from the home state (Tamil Nadu for NITT). If your child is from Tamil Nadu, the cutoffs are significantly lower. This is a major advantage worth considering.

**Parent strategy:** If your child scores well in JEE Main but doesn't clear JEE Advanced, NIT Trichy CS should be at the top of their preference list during JoSAA counselling.

## Curriculum & Structure

4-year BTech. Key features:

- **Strong industry connections** — regular guest lectures and workshops by tech leaders
- **Pragyan** technical festival attracts corporate sponsors and speakers
- **Coding culture** — NITT students consistently perform well in ICPC and other competitive programming contests
- **Research opportunities** — several faculty have international collaborations
- **Minor specialization** options available in select departments

## Campus Life

800 acres — one of the largest NIT campuses in India:

- **Festember** — one of south India's biggest cultural festivals
- **Pragyan** — flagship technical festival, ISO-certified and internationally recognized
- **Spider** — the student-run tech club is legendary, producing top competitive programmers
- **Strong sports culture** — Inter-NIT and Inter-IIT sports tournaments
- **Trichy** is a safe, affordable city. Good rail connectivity to Chennai, Bengaluru, and Coimbatore
- **Temple town** — close to historic temples, giving a unique cultural backdrop
