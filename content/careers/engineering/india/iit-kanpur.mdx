## Why IIT Kanpur?

IIT Kanpur is where India's computer science tradition was born. It was the first institution in India to offer a Computer Science degree, and that legacy shows. The CS department here is legendary for its theoretical rigor — if your child loves algorithms, mathematics, and the deep fundamentals of computing, IIT Kanpur is the place.

Many parents overlook IIT Kanpur because it's not in a metro city. That's a mistake. The slightly quieter campus means fewer distractions and a deeply academic culture. CS placements rival IIT Bombay and Delhi, with average packages of ₹35-50 LPA. Companies like Google, Tower Research, and Goldman Sachs recruit heavily from here.

IIT Kanpur also has a disproportionately strong track record in producing founders and researchers — Infosys co-founder N.R. Narayana Murthy and Flipkart co-founder Sachin Bansal are alumni.

## How to Get In

**JEE Advanced** is the only path.

- CS requires rank **< 250**
- Electrical Engineering: rank **< 1000**
- Other branches: rank < 3000-5000

**Parent perspective:** IIT Kanpur CS cutoffs are slightly more relaxed than Bombay and Delhi. For a student ranked 150-250 in JEE Advanced, IIT Kanpur CS is an exceptional choice — arguably better value than a non-CS branch at Bombay.

## Curriculum & Structure

4-year BTech. What makes IIT Kanpur's curriculum special:

- **Strongest theoretical CS department** in India — world-class faculty in algorithms, complexity theory, and formal methods
- **Flexible credit system** — students can take courses across any department
- **Undergraduate research** is heavily encouraged — many BTech students publish in top conferences
- **New York Office of Innovation** — IIT Kanpur has a US office for connecting students with Silicon Valley

## Campus Life

1055 acres along the banks of the Ganges. The campus is self-contained:

- **Antaragni** — annual cultural festival, one of north India's largest
- **Techkriti** — technical festival with international participation
- **Programming culture** — IIT Kanpur consistently ranks #1 among Indian colleges in competitive programming (ICPC)
- **Hall culture** — strong hostel traditions and inter-hall competitions
- **Airport access** — Kanpur airport has direct flights to Delhi, Mumbai, and Bengaluru
