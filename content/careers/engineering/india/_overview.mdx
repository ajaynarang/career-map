## Why India for Engineering?

India has the most competitive and meritocratic engineering admission system in the world. If your child scores well in JEE, they get into institutions that rival MIT and Stanford in placement outcomes — at a fraction of the cost.

**The numbers speak:** IIT Bombay CS graduates earn ₹40-55 LPA average starting salary. That's from a degree that costs ₹10L total. The ROI is unmatched globally.

## The Path

1. **Class 11-12:** Prepare for JEE Main + Advanced (alongside board exams)
2. **After 12th boards:** JEE Main (Jan + April) → JEE Advanced (May-June)
3. **Counselling:** JoSAA counselling (June-July) for IITs, NITs, IIITs
4. **Backup exams:** BITSAT, VITEEE, SRMJEEE run in parallel

## What Parents Should Know

- **Coaching is almost mandatory** for JEE. Budget ₹1-3L/year for good coaching (Allen, FIITJEE, Physics Wallah).
- **Mental health matters.** The preparation is a marathon. One exam doesn't define your child's future.
- **NITs are excellent.** Not getting into an IIT isn't failure — NIT Trichy, NIT Surathkal have outstanding placements.
- **BITS Pilani** is a hidden gem — no fixed branches, can change via CGPA, excellent placements.
