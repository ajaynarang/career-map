## Why NIT Surathkal?

NIT Surathkal (NITK) is consistently ranked #2-3 among all NITs in India, and it comes with something no other top engineering college offers: a campus right on the Arabian Sea. The beach is literally inside the campus. But beyond the stunning location, NITK delivers serious academic and placement outcomes.

For parents, the math is compelling: at ₹1.5L/year tuition, NITK offers CS placements averaging ₹20-30 LPA. The proximity to Bengaluru (350 km) means students have easy access to India's tech capital for internships and industry exposure.

NITK has a particularly strong alumni network in Bengaluru's tech ecosystem. Many graduates go on to work at Google, Microsoft, Amazon, and a thriving community of NITK alumni in startups means your child will have mentors and connections throughout their career.

## How to Get In

Admission through **JEE Main** via JoSAA counselling.

- CS: **99.3+ percentile (Home State / Karnataka)** or **99.7+ (Other State)**
- ECE/IT: 98.5+ percentile
- Other branches: 95-98 percentile

**Karnataka advantage:** If your child is from Karnataka, the Home State quota makes NITK CS significantly more accessible. This is a strategic consideration worth factoring into your planning.

## Curriculum & Structure

4-year BTech:

- **Strong coding culture** — NITK students perform well in national-level hackathons and coding competitions
- **Industry-oriented projects** — final year projects often done in collaboration with companies
- **IRIS** — the student-run technical club drives innovation and competitive programming
- **Exchange programs** — partnerships with universities in Germany, Japan, and South Korea
- **Proximity to Bengaluru** — students regularly attend tech meetups, conferences, and networking events

## Campus Life

300 acres on the coast of the Arabian Sea — one of the most beautiful campuses in India:

- **Beach inside campus** — students unwind at the beach after classes. Sunset views are legendary
- **Engineer** — annual technical festival, one of Karnataka's largest
- **Incident** — cultural festival attracting students from across south India
- **Strong hostel culture** — inter-hostel competitions in sports, tech, and culture
- **Mangaluru** — the nearby city is affordable, safe, and well-connected by air and rail to Bengaluru, Mumbai, and Delhi
- **Water sports and adventure** — surfing, kayaking, and trekking in the Western Ghats are regular weekend activities
