## Why IIT BHU?

IIT BHU is one of India's oldest engineering institutions — its roots go back to 1919 when Pandit Madan Mohan Malaviya founded it as the Banaras Engineering College within Banaras Hindu University. It became a full IIT in 2012, and since then, its placements, research output, and reputation have surged.

What sets IIT BHU apart from newer IITs is its **heritage and unique programmes**. It offers branches you won't find at most IITs — Mining Engineering, Ceramic Engineering, Pharmaceutical Engineering — alongside strong CS, ECE, and Mechanical departments. The BHU campus itself is 1,300 acres, making it one of the largest university campuses in Asia.

**The practical advantage:** IIT BHU's CS placements are now comparable to the top 5 IITs, with median packages of ₹30-45 LPA. But the JEE Advanced cutoff ranks are more accessible — CS admission requires a rank under 200, compared to under 80 at IIT Bombay. For a student ranked 100-500 in JEE Advanced, IIT BHU CS is often the smartest choice.

## How to Get In

The primary pathway is **JEE Advanced**, same as all IITs:

1. Clear JEE Main (top 2.5 lakh qualifiers)
2. Appear for JEE Advanced
3. Rank determines branch through JoSAA counselling

**Cutoff ranks (approximate, General category):**
- CS: < 200
- ECE: < 1500
- Mechanical: < 3500
- Mining/Metallurgy/Ceramic: < 6000-8000

**Note on CUET:** BHU also has some integrated programmes (outside IIT) that accept CUET scores. However, the BTech programme at IIT BHU is strictly through JEE Advanced.

## What Makes It Special

- **Oldest IIT lineage** — alumni network spans a century of engineers
- **Unique programmes** — Mining, Ceramic, and Pharmaceutical Engineering are niche and high-demand
- **BHU ecosystem** — IIT BHU students have access to the entire BHU campus, including its famous Faculty of Science, arts, and cultural events
- **Research funding** has grown dramatically since IIT status — labs and infrastructure are being rapidly upgraded
- **Varanasi culture** — living in one of the world's oldest cities adds a dimension no other IIT offers

## Campus and Student Life

The IIT BHU campus is within the larger 1,300-acre BHU campus on the banks of the Ganges:

- **Technex** — IIT BHU's annual tech festival, one of the biggest in North India
- **Kashiyatra** — the cultural festival, held in the spiritual capital of India
- **Strong coding culture** — competitive programming and hackathon teams consistently rank nationally
- **Ganga Aarti and Varanasi** — students experience a unique blend of modern engineering education and India's deepest cultural traditions
- **Hostel life** is vibrant — 15+ hostels with a strong senior-junior mentorship culture
- **Sports and fitness** — Olympic-size swimming pool, cricket grounds, gymnasium
