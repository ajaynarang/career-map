## Why DTU Delhi?

DTU (formerly Delhi College of Engineering, or DCE) is one of India's oldest and most respected engineering colleges, and it comes with a massive built-in advantage: the **Delhi quota**. 85% of seats are reserved for students who passed their Class 12 boards from Delhi, which means drastically lower cutoffs for Delhi students.

For a Delhi-based family, this is a game-changer. A JEE Main percentile of 95+ can get your child into DTU CS through Delhi quota — a score that would not even come close at any IIT or top NIT. And DTU CS placements are strong: ₹18-30 LPA average, with top packages from Google, Microsoft, and Goldman Sachs.

DTU also benefits from being in Delhi — the same internship access, industry exposure, and startup ecosystem advantages that IIT Delhi enjoys. Many DTU students intern at companies in Gurgaon and Noida during their studies.

## How to Get In

Admission through **JEE Main** via JAC Delhi counselling.

- **Delhi quota (85% seats):** JEE Main percentile **95+** for CS, **90+** for IT/ECE
- **Outside Delhi (15% seats):** JEE Main percentile **99+** for CS, **98+** for IT/ECE

**The Delhi advantage explained:** If your child studied in a Delhi school (Class 11-12), they qualify for Delhi quota. This makes DTU CS accessible to a much wider range of students compared to IITs or NITs. 85% of seats fall under this quota — it's by far the most significant advantage in engineering admissions for Delhi residents.

## Curriculum & Structure

4-year BTech:

- **Strong industry connections** — DTU's proximity to Gurgaon tech corridor means frequent industry interaction
- **Mathematics & Computing** — a unique program that combines CS with applied mathematics, highly sought after
- **Software Engineering** — separate from CS, with a focus on practical software development
- **Innovation and Incubation Centre** — supports student startups
- **Semester exchange** opportunities with international universities

## Campus Life

164 acres in Rohini, North Delhi. Well-connected by Delhi Metro:

- **Engifest** — DTU's annual cultural festival, one of Delhi's biggest college events
- **DTU's startup culture** — several successful startups have been founded by DTU alumni and students
- **Strong competitive coding community** — DTU students are regulars at ICPC and other national competitions
- **Metro connectivity** — Rohini Sector 18 metro station makes the campus easily accessible
- **Delhi NCR advantage** — weekends can mean networking events, tech meetups, and industry workshops in Gurgaon and Noida
- **Active societies** — ACM chapter, IEEE chapter, coding clubs, and 50+ student organizations
