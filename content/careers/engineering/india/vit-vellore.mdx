## Why VIT Vellore?

VIT (Vellore Institute of Technology) is India's largest private engineering university and consistently ranks in NIRF's top 15. But the real reason parents and students choose VIT is **placement reliability** — VIT has one of the most active placement cells in India, with 600+ companies visiting campus annually.

**The honest assessment:** VIT is not an IIT or NIT. The average student at VIT is not as strong as the average student at a top IIT. But VIT's top students — especially in CS/IT — get placed at Microsoft, Google, Amazon, and Goldman Sachs. The average CS placement is ₹8-12 LPA, which is very strong for a private college.

**VIT's biggest strength is its placement machine.** The placement cell is aggressive, well-connected, and ensures that almost every student who wants a job gets one. The 95%+ placement rate is not marketing — it's real. Companies trust VIT because they've been hiring from here for 20+ years.

**The tradeoff:** Fees are ₹16-20L for 4 years — significantly more than IITs (₹12L) or NITs (₹8L). But for students who don't crack JEE Main/Advanced at competitive ranks, VIT offers a clear path to strong placements.

## How to Get In

The sole pathway is **VITEEE** (VIT Engineering Entrance Exam):

1. Register at [vit.ac.in/admissions](https://vit.ac.in/admissions) — registration opens October-March
2. Appear for VITEEE in April (online, multiple slots)
3. Rank determines campus and branch during counselling

**Cutoffs (approximate):**
- CS/IT at Vellore: VITEEE rank < 5000
- ECE at Vellore: rank < 15000
- Mechanical at Vellore: rank < 25000
- Any branch at Chennai/AP/Bhopal campuses: rank < 50000

**Important:** Always prefer the **Vellore campus** — it has the best placements, infrastructure, and faculty. The Chennai campus is a distant second. Amaravati and Bhopal are much newer.

## What Makes It Special

- **Placement volume** — 600+ companies, 12,000+ offers per year across all campuses
- **Industry-aligned curriculum** — VIT updates courses frequently. Specializations in AI/ML, Data Science, Blockchain, and IoT are available
- **VITEEE rank-based scholarships** — top 500 ranks get 50-100% tuition waiver, making VIT as affordable as an NIT
- **International exposure** — semester abroad programs with 50+ partner universities globally
- **NIRF top 15** — consistent ranking validates quality

## Campus and Student Life

600-acre campus in Vellore, about 3 hours from Chennai:

- **Riviera** — VIT's annual cultural and tech festival, one of South India's largest college events
- **400+ student clubs** — coding, robotics, entrepreneurship, music, dance, sports
- **Modern infrastructure** — AC classrooms, well-equipped labs, international-standard sports facilities
- **Hostel is mandatory** — all students stay on campus. Rooms are decent, mess food is average (typical engineering college level)
- **Vellore is a small town** — campus is self-contained. Don't expect Bangalore/Mumbai-level nightlife. But this also means fewer distractions
- **Diverse student body** — students from every state in India, creating a truly national experience
- **Coding culture is strong** — competitive programming, hackathons, and open-source contributions are common among CS students
