## Why IIIT Hyderabad?

If your child is certain they want to study Computer Science, IIIT Hyderabad (commonly called IIITH) is one of the smartest choices in India. Unlike IITs and NITs that offer engineering across many branches, IIIT Hyderabad is laser-focused on computing. Every program here revolves around CS, AI, data science, or related fields.

The results speak for themselves: CS placements at IIITH match or exceed most IITs, with averages of ₹30-45 LPA. The research output per student is among the highest in India. IIITH's AI and Machine Learning research group is internationally recognized, with faculty who publish in top-tier venues like NeurIPS, ICML, and ACL.

**The parent advantage:** IIITH accepts students through both UGEE (its own exam) and JEE Main. This gives your child two shots at admission, unlike IITs which only accept JEE Advanced.

## How to Get In

Two pathways:

1. **UGEE (Undergraduate Entrance Exam)** — IIITH's own exam, consisting of an objective test followed by an interview. Typically held in May-June.
2. **JEE Main** — Students with 99+ percentile in JEE Main can apply directly.

**What makes UGEE special:** The interview component means IIITH selects for genuine passion for computing. A student who is deeply interested in CS but didn't crack JEE Advanced can still get in here.

## Curriculum & Structure

4-year BTech. The curriculum is research-driven and CS-heavy:

- **Research focus from day one** — students work on real research projects from their second year
- **Strong AI/ML curriculum** — IIITH was teaching Machine Learning and NLP long before they became buzzwords
- **Computational Linguistics** — unique program that combines CS with language technology
- **Industry collaboration** — research labs funded by Google, Microsoft, and Qualcomm on campus
- **Honors program** — top students can pursue advanced coursework and thesis work

## Campus Life

200 acres on Gachibowli — the heart of Hyderabad's tech corridor. Major IT companies are literally across the road:

- **Felicity** — annual cultural and technical festival
- **Proximity to Hitec City** — internship opportunities at hundreds of tech companies within a 10-minute drive
- **Research centers on campus** — KCIS (Kohli Centre on Intelligent Systems), LTRC (Language Technologies Research Centre)
- **Compact and focused** — smaller student body (about 2500 total) means closer mentorship and stronger peer network
- **Hyderabad advantage** — affordable city with excellent food, good weather, and a booming tech ecosystem
