## Why IIT Delhi?

IIT Delhi combines world-class engineering education with the advantage of being in India's capital. The Delhi location means better internship access, industry connections, and a thriving startup ecosystem right at your doorstep.

## How to Get In

Same path as all IITs: **JEE Advanced**.

- CS requires rank **< 200**
- Electrical Engineering: rank **< 800**
- Other branches: rank < 2000-4000

## Curriculum & Structure

4-year BTech. Strong emphasis on:

- **Entrepreneurship ecosystem** — IIT Delhi has one of India's strongest startup incubators
- **Industry collaborations** — direct research partnerships with Google, Microsoft, Samsung
- **Flexible curriculum** — can take courses across departments

## Campus Life

325 acres in Hauz Khas, South Delhi — one of Delhi's most vibrant neighborhoods. The location is a major advantage:

- Easy access to internships at MNCs in Gurgaon and Noida
- **Rendezvous** — annual cultural festival
- **Tryst** — technical festival
- Active startup culture — several successful startups have been born here
