## Why SRM Chennai?

SRM Institute of Science and Technology (formerly SRM University) is one of India's largest private engineering universities, with its flagship campus at Kattankulathur, about 40 km from Chennai city center. SRM is a solid backup option for students who want a guaranteed engineering seat with decent placements.

**The honest assessment:** SRM is not in the same league as IITs, NITs, or even VIT/BITS in terms of academic rigor or top placements. But it does several things well:

- **Accessibility** — SRMJEEE is an easy exam with no negative marking. Almost any student with basic PCM knowledge can get in
- **Placement reliability** — the placement cell is active and ensures most students get offers, even if not all are high-paying
- **CS specializations** — AI/ML, Data Science, and Cloud Computing specializations are well-structured
- **Infrastructure** — the Kattankulathur campus is massive and well-maintained

**Where SRM falls short:** The average placement package (₹6-8 LPA) is lower than VIT or top NITs. Mass recruiters (TCS, Infosys, Cognizant) dominate. To get ₹12+ LPA offers, your child needs to be proactive — self-learn, do projects, crack competitive interviews independently.

## How to Get In

Primary pathway is **SRMJEEE**:

1. Register at [srmist.edu.in/admissions](https://www.srmist.edu.in/admissions) — registration opens December
2. Appear for SRMJEEE in April (online exam, multiple dates)
3. Counselling based on SRMJEEE rank — choose campus and branch

**Cutoffs (approximate):**
- CS at Kattankulathur: SRMJEEE rank < 3000
- ECE at Kattankulathur: rank < 8000
- Mechanical: rank < 15000
- Any branch at Ramapuram/NCR/AP campus: rank < 30000

**Critical advice:** Always choose the **Kattankulathur campus**. SRM Ramapuram, NCR, and Amaravati campuses have significantly weaker placements and infrastructure.

## What Makes It Special

- **Easy admission** — SRMJEEE has no negative marking and NCERT-level difficulty. Good safety school
- **Large alumni network** — 50,000+ alumni globally, strong in Chennai's IT corridor
- **Industry collaborations** — partnerships with IBM, Cisco, and other tech companies for curriculum input
- **Deemed university status** — can set its own curriculum, leading to faster course updates
- **Research parks and incubators** — SRM has invested heavily in startup infrastructure

## Campus and Student Life

250-acre campus in Kattankulathur, a semi-urban area south of Chennai:

- **Milan** — SRM's annual cultural festival
- **Aaruush** — national-level tech fest with good participation
- **100+ student clubs** — coding, robotics, music, sports, and entrepreneurship
- **Modern campus** — AC labs, good library, international-standard sports complex
- **Hostel is available** but not mandatory — rooms are decent, mess food is average
- **Kattankulathur location** — far from Chennai city. Campus is self-contained, but the town itself has limited entertainment options
- **Chennai proximity** — weekend trips to the city are common. Chennai is a tech hub with great food, culture, and job opportunities
