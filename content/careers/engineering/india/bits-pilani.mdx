## Why BITS Pilani?

BITS Pilani is India's best private engineering college, and it operates on a philosophy fundamentally different from the IITs. Here, there are no fixed branches at admission. Your child enters BITS based on their BITSAT score and chooses their branch after the first semester based on their college GPA. This means a student who discovers a passion for CS after joining can still get into the CS department.

This flexibility is BITS Pilani's superpower. It also means that every CS student at BITS actively chose CS after experiencing the curriculum — they're motivated and self-selected.

BITS Pilani graduates are everywhere in Silicon Valley. Sundar Pichai (Google CEO), though an IIT Kharagpur alumnus, would tell you his company recruits heavily from BITS. Alumni include leaders at Razorpay, PhonePe, and dozens of successful startups.

**The honest conversation about fees:** BITS costs ₹5.5L/year — roughly 2.5x an IIT. For many families, this is significant. But consider the ROI: CS placements average ₹30-45 LPA, meaning the fee difference is recovered in the first year of work.

## How to Get In

BITS Pilani uses its own exam: **BITSAT** (not JEE).

- BITSAT is a 3-hour online test — Physics, Chemistry, Math, English, and Logical Reasoning
- Score **350+ out of 450** for CS at Pilani campus
- Score 320-340 for EE/ECE
- Score 280-300 for other branches

**Why this matters for parents:** BITSAT is a completely separate preparation track from JEE. However, the syllabus overlaps significantly with JEE Main. Most students prepare for both simultaneously. BITSAT also rewards speed — it's 150 questions in 3 hours.

**No fixed branches at entry** — your child selects their branch after the first semester based on CGPA. This is a unique advantage if they're unsure about their interests.

## Curriculum & Structure

4-year BE (Bachelor of Engineering). The BITS system is unique:

- **Practice School (PS)** — mandatory 6-month industry internship, considered a semester. Students work at Google, Goldman Sachs, Samsung, and other top companies
- **No fixed branches at entry** — branch allotment after first semester based on CGPA
- **Dual degree option** — can do BE + MSc (e.g., CS + Economics) in 5 years
- **Thesis option** — can replace Practice School II with a research thesis
- **Three campuses** — Pilani (Rajasthan), Goa, and Hyderabad. Pilani is the flagship.

## Campus Life

The Pilani campus is in a small town in Rajasthan — there's nothing around, which means campus life is everything:

- **BITS is famous for its fest culture** — OASIS (cultural), APOGEE (technical), BOSM (sports)
- **Club culture is intense** — coding clubs, finance clubs, robotics, music, drama
- **24/7 campus** — BITS students are famously nocturnal. The culture encourages self-paced learning
- **Strong alumni network** — BITSians look out for each other, especially in Silicon Valley and Bengaluru
