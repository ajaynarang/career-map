## Why UCL?

UCL (University College London) is London's oldest university and a top-10 global institution. Its engineering faculty is strong across the board, with particular strengths in Computer Science, Biomedical Engineering, and Civil Engineering. UCL is also more accessible than Imperial or Cambridge — acceptance rates are higher, and the culture is less pressure-cooker.

UCL's biggest advantage is its interdisciplinary approach. Located in Bloomsbury (central London), students can take modules from across UCL's vast range of departments. An engineer interested in entrepreneurship, design, or policy can build a truly unique education.

## How to Get In

UCL is more accessible than Imperial or Oxbridge, with acceptance rates of **15-25%** for engineering programs.

**Requirements:**
- UCAS application by January 29
- Strong Personal Statement focused on engineering
- IELTS 6.5+ (lower than Cambridge/Oxford's 7.0)
- 85-90%+ in Indian boards
- No entrance exam or interview for most engineering programs (a significant advantage over Oxbridge)

**UCL is a good "strategic choice"** in your UCAS 5 — less selective than Imperial/Oxbridge but still a globally top university.

## Curriculum & Structure

Both BEng (3 years) and MEng (4 years) available. Semester system.

- **Integrated Engineering Programme (IEP)** — UCL's distinctive approach embeds professional skills, interdisciplinary challenges, and real-world projects into every year
- **Scenarios** — team-based projects in each year where students from different engineering disciplines work together
- **Year in Industry** — optional placement year available (extends to 4 years BEng or 5 years MEng)
- **Strong CS department** — UCL CS is known for AI, machine learning, and cybersecurity research
- **Connected Curriculum** — UCL's strategy of linking teaching to research, meaning undergraduates engage with cutting-edge research

## Campus Life

UCL's campus is in Bloomsbury, one of London's most central and culturally rich neighborhoods.

- **Location is phenomenal** — British Museum, West End theatres, Kings Cross tech hub, and hundreds of restaurants are walking distance
- **Indian community is very large** — UCL has one of the highest proportions of Indian students in the UK. Hindu Society, Sikh Society, Tamil Society, and more
- **More relaxed than Imperial** — UCL has a broader student body (not just STEM) which creates a more balanced social environment
- **Student Union** — one of the most active in the UK with 300+ clubs and societies
- **London cost of living** — similar to Imperial. Budget £1,200-1,500/month. UCL halls range from £180-300/week
- **Diverse and inclusive** — UCL was the first UK university to admit students regardless of religion, and this progressive ethos continues
