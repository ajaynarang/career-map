## Why Imperial?

Imperial College London is the UK's #1 engineering university and consistently ranks in the global top 5. It's a STEM-focused institution — there are no humanities or social science departments — which means every resource is directed toward science and engineering.

Imperial's location in South Kensington, the heart of London, is unbeatable. Your child will study surrounded by the Science Museum, Natural History Museum, and the Royal Albert Hall. More practically, London's status as a global financial and tech hub means internship and job opportunities are extraordinary — from Goldman Sachs in the City to Google in King's Cross.

## How to Get In

Imperial is the most selective engineering school in the UK outside of Cambridge. Acceptance rates are **8-12%** depending on department.

**Application process:**
- Apply through **UCAS** by January 29 (2026 entry)
- Imperial requires a focused **Personal Statement** — demonstrate deep interest in your chosen engineering discipline
- Some departments (Computing, EEE) conduct **interviews** — typically online for international students
- **No entrance exam** for most engineering departments (unlike Oxford/Cambridge), though some may introduce aptitude assessments

**Grade requirements for Indian students:**
- CBSE/ISC: 90%+ overall, with 90%+ in Math, Physics, and Chemistry
- IB: 38-40 points with 6-7 in Higher Level subjects
- Offers are conditional — you must meet the grade threshold after board results

## Curriculum & Structure

Imperial offers both **BEng (3 years)** and **MEng (4 years)**. The MEng is strongly recommended — it leads to Chartered Engineer status and is the standard for serious engineering careers.

- **Year 1:** Core mathematics, physics, and engineering foundations
- **Years 2-3:** Specialization with increasing depth
- **Year 4 (MEng):** Advanced modules + major individual research project
- **Group projects** are a hallmark — from first year, students work in teams on real engineering challenges
- **Industrial placements** — a Year in Industry option is available, extending the degree to 5 years but adding paid work experience
- **Cross-department modules** — take courses from other engineering departments
- **i-Explore** — all students can take elective modules from outside engineering (business, languages, humanities)

## Campus Life

Imperial's main campus is in South Kensington — one of London's most prestigious (and expensive) neighborhoods.

- **London is London** — the cultural, economic, and social capital of Europe. Your child will never be bored
- **Indian community is massive** — Imperial has one of the highest percentages of Indian students in the UK. Indian Society is very active with Diwali celebrations, cultural shows, and networking events
- **Cost of living is high** — London is expensive. Budget £1,200-1,500/month for accommodation + food. Imperial halls of residence cost £180-350/week
- **Imperial is academically intense** — the workload is heavy. It's often called the "pressure cooker" of UK universities. Social life exists but is secondary to academics
- **Union and clubs** — Imperial has 380+ student clubs and societies
- **NHS healthcare** — free healthcare on the National Health Service after paying the Immigration Health Surcharge
