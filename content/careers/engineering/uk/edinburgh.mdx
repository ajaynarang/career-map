## Why Edinburgh?

The University of Edinburgh combines a world-class engineering education with one of the most beautiful cities in the world. Edinburgh's Informatics department is among the best in Europe for AI and machine learning — it's where much of the foundational work on neural networks happened.

For Indian families, Edinburgh offers a compelling value proposition: lower fees and living costs than London universities, a strong Indian student community, and Scotland's generally welcoming immigration policies. Edinburgh is also a tech hub — Skyscanner was born here, and Amazon, JPMorgan, and several fintech companies have major offices.

## How to Get In

Edinburgh is more accessible than the London elite, with **20-30%** acceptance rates for engineering.

**Application:**
- UCAS by January 29
- No entrance exam or interview for engineering
- Strong Personal Statement required
- IELTS 6.5+ (lower than Oxbridge)
- 85%+ in Indian boards

**Edinburgh is an excellent "insurance" choice** alongside Imperial or Oxbridge — still a globally excellent university but with higher acceptance rates.

## Curriculum & Structure

Scotland offers 4-year undergraduate degrees (compared to 3 years in England), leading to an Honours degree. MEng options are 5 years.

- **4-year Scottish degree** — the extra year allows broader study in Years 1-2 before specializing
- **Informatics is outstanding** — Edinburgh's School of Informatics is one of the largest CS departments in Europe
- **Year abroad options** — exchange programs with top global universities
- **Research-led teaching** — Edinburgh is a research-intensive university (Russell Group member)
- **Interdisciplinary opportunities** — the Scottish system allows students to take courses outside their discipline more easily

## Campus Life

Edinburgh is consistently ranked as one of the world's best cities to live in. The university is spread across the city rather than being a single campus.

- **Edinburgh is stunning** — Arthur's Seat (ancient volcano), Edinburgh Castle, cobblestone streets, and the Edinburgh Festival Fringe (world's largest arts festival)
- **Indian community is significant** — active Indian Students Association. Good Indian restaurants throughout the city
- **Cheaper than London** — rent is £500-700/month. Food and entertainment are noticeably cheaper
- **Safe city** — Edinburgh is one of the safest cities in the UK
- **Cold and dark winters** — Edinburgh is further north than most of Europe. Winter days are short (sunrise after 8:30am, sunset before 4pm). Summers are magical with very long days
- **Festivals** — August brings the Edinburgh Fringe, Film Festival, and International Festival. The city transforms
- **Scottish culture** — Hogmanay (New Year's), haggis, and a distinct cultural identity from England
