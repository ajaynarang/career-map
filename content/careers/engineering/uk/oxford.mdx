## Why Oxford?

Oxford's Engineering Science course is distinctive — it's one of the broadest engineering programs in the world, covering everything from biomedical devices to quantum computing under a single degree. While most UK universities force specialization at 18, Oxford (like Cambridge) lets students explore multiple engineering disciplines before choosing a focus.

Oxford's brand carries extraordinary weight globally. An Oxford engineering degree opens doors in finance (Goldman Sachs, McKinsey recruit heavily), tech, consulting, and academia. The tutorial system — one-on-one teaching with world experts — is an educational experience available nowhere else.

## How to Get In

Oxford's process mirrors Cambridge's in rigor:

1. **UCAS application** by **October 15**
2. **PAT (Physics Aptitude Test)** — a challenging physics and math exam taken in late October. This is a critical filter
3. **College selection** — like Cambridge, you apply to a specific college
4. **Interview** — typically December. Oxford conducts international interviews online or at centers in India. Expect challenging physics and math problems to solve in real-time
5. **Conditional offer** — A*A*A at A-Level (with A* in Math and Physics) or 95%+ in Indian boards

**Important:** You cannot apply to BOTH Oxford AND Cambridge in the same year through UCAS. Choose one.

**What Oxford values:** Raw intellectual ability and passion for engineering, demonstrated through the PAT score and interview performance. Extra-curriculars matter less than at US universities.

## Curriculum & Structure

4-year MEng in Engineering Science:

- **Years 1-2:** Broad-based — mathematics, mechanics, electronics, computing, thermodynamics, structures. All Engineering Science students take the same courses
- **Year 3:** Choose a specialization stream (Biomedical, Chemical, Civil, Electrical, Information, Mechanical) + group design project
- **Year 4:** Advanced specialization + individual 4th-year project (mini-thesis)
- **Tutorial system** — weekly one-on-one or small group sessions with a tutor (expert). You solve problem sheets and discuss them. This is where the real learning happens
- **Labs and practicals** — hands-on work from Year 1, though Oxford is more theoretical than Imperial
- **Collections** — informal exams held at the start of each term to test retention

## Campus Life

Oxford is a historic city of 155,000 people, even more dominated by the university than Cambridge.

- **College system** — your child will belong to a college that provides accommodation, meals, social life, and tutorial teaching. Each college has centuries of history
- **Indian community is active** — Oxford India Society and Oxford Hindu Society organize regular events
- **Beautiful but expensive** — Oxford's dreaming spires are stunning. Living costs are £1,000-1,300/month
- **Academically demanding** — Oxford terms are 8 weeks with weekly tutorials requiring intensive preparation. Problem sheets are demanding
- **Formal dining** — gown-wearing college dinners, a quintessentially Oxford experience
- **Oxford Union** — the famous debating society. Many students engage in activities beyond engineering
- **London is 1 hour by train** — easy access for internships, events, and job interviews
- **Rowing, rugby, and college sports** — inter-college competition is a big part of social life
