## Why Manchester?

The University of Manchester is where the modern world was invented — quite literally. The first stored-program computer was built here. Graphene was discovered here (earning the 2010 Nobel Prize). The splitting of the atom happened here. Manchester's engineering heritage is extraordinary, and its modern programs continue that tradition.

For Indian families, Manchester offers perhaps the best value in UK engineering education: lower fees than London, lower living costs (Manchester is 30-40% cheaper than London), India-specific scholarships, and a welcoming, multicultural city with the UK's second-largest Indian community.

## How to Get In

Manchester is the most accessible of the "elite" UK engineering universities, with **25-35%** acceptance rates.

**Application:**
- UCAS by January 29
- No entrance exam or interview
- 82-88%+ in Indian boards (lower threshold than Oxbridge or Imperial)
- IELTS 6.0-6.5 (more achievable than the 7.0 required by Oxbridge)
- Offers are typically conditional on board results

Manchester is an excellent choice for students who want a top-30 global university without the extreme selectivity of Oxbridge or Imperial.

## Curriculum & Structure

BEng (3 years) and MEng (4 years) available. Semester system.

- **Year in Industry** — paid placement year available, extending the degree by one year. Excellent for building UK work experience
- **Group design projects** — from Year 1, students work on team-based engineering challenges with real clients
- **Graphene Engineering Innovation Centre** — students can access world-leading materials science facilities
- **Strong aerospace and nuclear programs** — Manchester is home to the Dalton Nuclear Institute and has strong Rolls-Royce partnerships
- **Enterprise options** — Manchester's Masood Entrepreneurship Centre supports student startups

## Campus Life

Manchester's campus is in the city center, a 10-minute walk from Piccadilly Station and the Northern Quarter.

- **Manchester is the UK's "second city"** — vibrant music scene (Oasis, The Smiths, Joy Division), football (Manchester United and Manchester City), nightlife, and culture
- **Massive Indian community** — Manchester has the UK's second-largest South Asian population. The Curry Mile on Wilmslow Road is famous. Your child will NOT miss Indian food
- **Very affordable** — rent is £400-600/month. Manchester is 30-40% cheaper than London overall
- **Media and tech hub** — BBC MediaCityUK is in nearby Salford. Tech companies (Booking.com, Autotrader, THG) are based here
- **Sport mad** — if your child likes football, Manchester is heaven
- **Rainy** — Manchester's reputation for rain is well-earned. Invest in a good waterproof jacket
- **Friendly people** — Mancunians are known for being warm and welcoming
