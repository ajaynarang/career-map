## Why Australia for Finance?

Australia punches well above its weight in global finance. Its superannuation (pension) system — the world's 4th largest at AUD $3.5 trillion — means there is enormous demand for fund managers, actuaries, financial analysts, and risk professionals. The Big Four banks employ over 160,000 people combined. And Australia's fintech sector (Afterpay, now part of Block, was born here) continues to grow rapidly.

**The immigration advantage.** Accountants, auditors, and actuaries are on Australia's Skills Priority List, meaning finance graduates have a direct pathway to Permanent Residency through skilled migration. After a 4-year degree, your child gets a 2-year post-study work visa (extendable to 3-4 years if studying in regional areas). Finance is one of the professions Australia actively wants.

**The financial picture:** A 4-year commerce degree costs ₹1.4-1.7 Crore total — comparable to Canada and significantly cheaper than the US or UK. The ability to work 48 hours per fortnight at Australian wages (minimum AUD $24.10/hour) provides meaningful income during studies.

## The Path

1. **Class 11-12:** Focus on board exams (80%+ needed for top programs). Prepare for IELTS (aim for 6.5+)
2. **Summer before 12th:** Research universities. Australian universities accept applications year-round for February or July intake
3. **Apply directly** to universities through their online portals. Most deadlines are October-November for February intake
4. **Receive offers** — conditional on 12th board results. Accept and pay deposit
5. **Apply for Student Visa (subclass 500)** — requires CoE, IELTS, financial proof, and OSHC
6. **February or July:** Start university

## What Parents Should Know

- **February is the main intake.** Unlike most other countries (September start), Australian universities primarily start in February. July intake is also available.
- **Melbourne Model is unique.** The University of Melbourne follows a "Melbourne Model" — a broad 3-year Bachelor of Commerce followed by a specialized 2-year Master's (e.g., Master of Finance, Master of Actuarial Science). This is different from UNSW which offers a direct 4-year specialized degree.
- **CPA Australia and CA ANZ** are the two professional accounting bodies. Commerce graduates can pursue CPA or CA alongside their first job. Many employers sponsor the qualification.
- **Superannuation industry = unique opportunity.** Australia's pension fund industry is massive and growing. Roles in fund management, actuarial analysis, and investment consulting are abundant and well-paid.
- **Work rights are generous.** 48 hours per fortnight during semester at AUD $24+/hour means your child can earn AUD $15,000-20,000 per year while studying.
- **PR pathway through skilled migration.** Accountants and actuaries are on the Skills Priority List. After gaining work experience on the post-study work visa, your child can apply for PR through the skilled migration program (subclass 189/190/491).
- **Climate and lifestyle are excellent.** Melbourne and Sydney offer warm climates, safe environments, and large Indian communities. Quality of life is consistently among the world's highest.
