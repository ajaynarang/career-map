## Why University of Melbourne?

The University of Melbourne is Australia's #1 university and the anchor of Melbourne's financial ecosystem. Melbourne is Australia's financial planning capital, home to major superannuation funds, the Australian Securities Exchange (ASX), and the Asia-Pacific headquarters of numerous global financial firms. Melbourne consistently ranks in the top 3 most liveable cities in the world.

**The Melbourne Model is unique and powerful.** Unlike most Australian universities that offer 4-year specialist degrees, Melbourne follows a distinctive model: a broad 3-year undergraduate degree (Bachelor of Commerce) followed by a specialised 2-year Master's (Master of Finance, Master of Actuarial Science, etc.). This is similar to the US liberal arts model and produces versatile graduates who can adapt to different career paths.

**The actuarial science pathway is exceptional.** Melbourne's actuarial programme is the most prestigious in Australia and one of the best globally. The BCom (Actuarial Studies major) + Master of Actuarial Science pathway provides full accreditation with the Actuaries Institute. Given that actuaries are on Australia's Skills Priority List with starting salaries of AUD 75,000-90,000, this is an outstanding career path for PCM students.

**For Indian students:** Melbourne has the largest Indian community in Australia. The suburb of Dandenong, Clayton, and surrounding areas have extensive Indian restaurants, temples, grocery stores, and cultural organisations. Your child will find a strong support system from day one.

## How to Get In

1. **Apply directly** through the [University of Melbourne portal](https://study.unimelb.edu.au/how-to-apply) — no centralised application system
2. **Academic requirements:** 85%+ in Class 12 boards, with strong Mathematics (80%+). Specific prerequisites vary by major
3. **English language:** IELTS 6.5 (no band below 6.0) or TOEFL 79+
4. **No supplementary application** — Melbourne admits primarily on academic merit. No essays, interviews, or extracurricular profiles required
5. **Deadline:** October 31 for February intake (Semester 1); April 30 for July intake (Semester 2)
6. **Offers:** Typically within 4-6 weeks of application

**Reality check:** Melbourne's Commerce programme is less competitive to enter than Wharton or LSE, but the academic standard once enrolled is very high. The Melbourne Model means students who perform well in the BCom can progress to highly selective Master's programmes (Master of Finance acceptance rate is roughly 30%).

## Curriculum & Structure

3-year Bachelor of Commerce + optional 2-year Master's:

**Bachelor of Commerce (3 years):**
- **First year:** Introductory Microeconomics, Introductory Macroeconomics, Accounting, Quantitative Methods + breadth subjects (any faculty — science, arts, engineering)
- **Finance Major:** Corporate Finance, Investments, Derivatives, Financial Statement Analysis, International Finance
- **Actuarial Studies Major:** Financial Mathematics, Life Contingencies, Risk Theory, Statistical Techniques. Provides exemptions from Actuaries Institute exams
- **Economics Major:** Intermediate Micro/Macro, Econometrics, Development Economics, Game Theory
- **Breadth requirement** — the Melbourne Model requires students to take 25% of their courses from outside their faculty. A Commerce student might take courses in Computer Science, Psychology, or Philosophy

**Master's Pathways (2 years, after BCom):**
- **Master of Finance:** Advanced corporate finance, asset pricing, behavioural finance, fintech. Highly selective
- **Master of Actuarial Science:** Full professional accreditation pathway. Graduates can practice as actuaries in Australia and globally
- **Master of Management (Accounting):** CPA Australia accredited. The fast-track to CPA qualification

**Practical features:**
- **Student Managed Investment Fund** — Commerce students manage a real portfolio under faculty supervision
- **Bloomberg Terminal access** in the Finance Lab
- **Industry projects** — capstone subjects involve consulting projects with real companies
- **Exchange programmes** — semester abroad at over 150 partner universities globally (including Wharton, LBS, HEC Paris)

## Campus Life

The University of Melbourne's Parkville campus is one of the most beautiful in Australia:

- **Parkville campus** — tree-lined paths, historic sandstone buildings, and modern architecture. Located 2km north of Melbourne's CBD, easily accessible by tram (free tram zone covers the city centre)
- **Melbourne University Commerce Students' Society (MUCSS)** — runs career workshops, networking events, and the annual Commerce Ball
- **Finance Students' Association** — organises stock pitch competitions, firm visits, and career panels with industry professionals
- **Melbourne is a coffee and food capital** — arguably Australia's best city for dining, with an enormous range of Indian restaurants in the CBD, Footscray, and the south-eastern suburbs
- **Sport culture** — Australian Rules Football (AFL) is massive. Melbourne is the home of AFL, and game days at the MCG (Melbourne Cricket Ground) are an unforgettable experience
- **Work opportunities** — Melbourne has a strong part-time job market for students. Hospitality, retail, and tutoring are common. At AUD 24+/hour minimum wage, part-time work provides meaningful income
- **Safety** — Melbourne is consistently ranked among the world's safest cities. Public transport is reliable, and the city is very walkable
