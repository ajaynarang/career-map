## Why UNSW Business School?

UNSW Business School is the largest business school in the Asia-Pacific region and consistently ranked alongside Melbourne as Australia's best for finance. Its location in Sydney — Australia's largest city and financial capital — gives students direct access to the headquarters of major banks, asset managers, and the country's most dynamic fintech ecosystem.

**Sydney is Australia's Wall Street.** Macquarie Group (Australia's most prestigious investment bank), UBS Australia, JP Morgan Sydney, and Goldman Sachs Sydney are all headquartered here. UNSW's proximity means students can intern at these firms during the trimester, not just during breaks. The Martin Place financial district is a 30-minute train ride from campus.

**The Co-op Programme is UNSW's secret weapon.** UNSW Business offers a prestigious Co-op Scholarship Programme — a 4-year programme that includes 18 months of paid industry placements at top firms. Co-op scholars are placed at companies like CBA, Macquarie, PwC, and Deloitte, earning AUD 40,000+ during their placements while getting guaranteed work experience. The Co-op acceptance rate is very competitive (about 5%), but for those who get in, it's transformational.

**Actuarial Studies is outstanding.** UNSW's Bachelor of Actuarial Studies is one of only two fully accredited programmes in Australia (alongside Melbourne). Actuaries are among the highest-paid professionals in Australia, with starting salaries of AUD 75,000-90,000 and experienced actuaries earning AUD 200,000+.

## How to Get In

1. **Apply directly** through [UNSW's portal](https://www.unsw.edu.au/study/how-to-apply) or through a registered education agent
2. **Academic requirements:** 82%+ in Class 12 boards (varies by programme). Mathematics is essential. For Actuarial Studies: 90%+ in Mathematics
3. **English language:** IELTS 7.0 (no band below 6.0) for Business/Commerce; IELTS 7.0 for Actuarial Studies
4. **No supplementary application** — admission is primarily academic merit-based
5. **UNSW operates on trimesters** — three terms per year (February, June, September). Apply 3-4 months before your preferred start date
6. **Offers:** Typically within 2-4 weeks of application

**Reality check:** UNSW Commerce is less competitive to enter than UK or US top schools, but the trimester system means the pace is intense — 3 terms per year with shorter breaks. Students who thrive here are disciplined and organised.

## Curriculum & Structure

3-year Bachelor of Commerce (or 3-year Bachelor of Actuarial Studies):

**Bachelor of Commerce (3 years, trimester system):**
- **Core:** Accounting, Microeconomics, Macroeconomics, Business Statistics, Business Law, plus electives
- **Finance Major:** Corporate Finance, Investment & Portfolio Management, Financial Markets & Institutions, Options & Futures, Financial Modelling
- **Accounting Major:** Financial Accounting, Management Accounting, Auditing, Taxation. CPA Australia and CA ANZ accredited
- **Economics Major:** Intermediate Micro/Macro, Econometrics, International Economics, Development Economics
- **Information Systems Major:** For students interested in fintech — covers database management, systems analysis, and digital innovation

**Bachelor of Actuarial Studies (3 years):**
- Mathematics-intensive: Calculus, Linear Algebra, Probability, Statistics, Life Contingencies, General Insurance, Financial Mathematics
- Provides exemptions from up to 7 Actuaries Institute exams — saving years of study
- Can be combined with a double degree in Commerce, Science, or Information Systems

**Key features:**
- **UNSW Co-op Programme:** For elite students — 4-year programme with 18 months of paid industry placements + scholarship. Apply separately during HSC or after admission
- **Trading Room:** UNSW's Bloomberg-equipped trading room lets students practice with real financial data
- **Trimester system** means you complete your degree in the same calendar time but with more concentrated study periods
- **Double degree options:** BCom/BSc (4 years), BCom/BEng (5 years), BActSt/BSc (4 years). The BCom/BSc combination with Finance + Data Science is increasingly popular for quant careers
- **Industry Connections Programme** — structured mentoring with finance professionals

## Campus Life

UNSW's Kensington campus is in Sydney's eastern suburbs, 6km from the CBD:

- **Campus is large and modern** — recently renovated facilities, including the new UNSW Business School building with state-of-the-art lecture theatres and collaboration spaces
- **Sydney lifestyle** — Coogee Beach, Bondi Beach, and Maroubra Beach are all within 15 minutes. Sydney's climate is warm year-round (winter lows of 8-10 degrees Celsius)
- **UNSW Finance & Investment Society** — one of Australia's largest student finance societies. Runs stock pitch competitions, career panels, and networking events with Sydney's financial industry
- **Arc (student organisation)** — over 300 clubs and societies. Indian community is strong through the UNSW Indian Society
- **Part-time work** — Sydney has a strong job market for students. Finance students often find tutoring or part-time bookkeeping work at AUD 25-35/hour
- **Transport** — excellent bus connections to the CBD. The upcoming Metro line will further improve connectivity
- **Randwick neighbourhood** — affordable restaurants, cafes, and shops. Indian groceries and restaurants available throughout Sydney's western and south-western suburbs (Parramatta, Harris Park)

## UNSW vs. Melbourne for Commerce

| Factor | UNSW | Melbourne |
|--------|------|-----------|
| City | Sydney (larger, financial capital) | Melbourne (more liveable, cultural) |
| Degree structure | 3-year specialist BCom | 3-year broad BCom + 2-year Master's |
| Calendar | Trimesters (3 terms) | Semesters (2 terms) |
| Co-op programme | Yes (prestigious, paid) | No formal equivalent |
| Actuarial | Excellent (fully accredited) | Excellent (fully accredited) |
| Entry requirements | Slightly lower | Slightly higher |
| Investment banking | Strong Sydney connections | Strong Melbourne connections |

Both are outstanding. If your child prefers Sydney's energy and wants the Co-op advantage, choose UNSW. If they prefer Melbourne's lifestyle and the broad Melbourne Model education, choose Melbourne.
