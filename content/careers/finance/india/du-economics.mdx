## Why DU Economics?

Delhi University's BA Economics (Honours) programme — particularly at **Hindu College, Hansraj College, and Lady Shri Ram (LSR)** — is India's premier undergraduate economics degree. It's the most common launchpad for careers in investment banking, management consulting, policy, and academia.

**The placement reality at top DU economics colleges is exceptional:**

- Hindu College Economics graduates regularly receive ₹12-18 LPA offers from Goldman Sachs, JP Morgan, and McKinsey
- Hansraj Economics has a strong placement cell with consistent ₹8-15 LPA packages
- LSR Economics produces top performers who go on to IIMs, ISB, Harvard, and Oxford

**Why PCM students should seriously consider DU Economics:**

The BA Economics (Hons) curriculum at DU is heavily mathematical. It covers microeconomics with calculus, econometrics with statistics, game theory, and mathematical economics. PCM students have a **massive advantage** over commerce-background peers because of their comfort with calculus, linear algebra, and probability.

**The ROI argument:** A 3-year BA Economics at Hindu College costs ₹1.5-3L total. Graduates get ₹8-18 LPA placement offers. Post-MBA (from IIM/ISB, which DU economics feeds into), salaries jump to ₹25-50 LPA. This ROI rivals or exceeds IIT engineering.

## How to Get In

Entry through **CUET**:

1. Register for CUET at [cuet.samarth.ac.in](https://cuet.samarth.ac.in)
2. **Critical:** Choose Mathematics and Economics as domain subjects. Maths score carries heavy weight for economics admissions
3. Score in the top 1-3% percentile for top colleges

**College-wise cutoffs (approximate CUET percentile):**
- **Hindu College:** 98%+
- **Hansraj College:** 97%+
- **Lady Shri Ram (LSR):** 98%+ (women only)
- **Ramjas College:** 95%+
- **SGTB Khalsa:** 93%+
- **Dyal Singh College:** 90%+

**Key insight:** DU economics cutoffs for top colleges are only slightly lower than SRCC B.Com cutoffs. The competition is real, but CUET is NCERT-level — strong fundamentals in math and economics will get your child through.

## What Makes It Special

- **Feeder to finance and consulting** — DU Economics at top colleges is the #1 pipeline to Goldman Sachs, McKinsey, and BCG campus recruitment at the UG level in India
- **DSE pathway** — DU's Delhi School of Economics (MA Economics) is India's most prestigious economics master's programme. Having done BA Economics at DU gives a natural advantage for DSE admission
- **IIM feeder** — DU Economics graduates are overrepresented at IIM Ahmedabad and IIM Bangalore. The analytical foundation is strong
- **College ecosystem** — Hindu, Hansraj, and LSR have active economics societies that organize workshops, trading competitions, and speaker sessions with industry leaders
- **North Campus intellectual culture** — surrounded by St. Stephen's, SRCC, and other top colleges. Inter-college events, debates, and friendships enrich the experience

## Campus and Student Life

DU's North Campus (Hindu, Hansraj) and South Campus (LSR, Gargi) offer distinct experiences:

**North Campus (Hindu, Hansraj):**
- **Hindu College's Famous Five** — the economics, English, history, philosophy, and political science departments are legendary
- **Mecca** — Hindu College's annual cultural festival is one of Delhi's biggest college events
- **Hudson Lane and Kamla Nagar** — cafes, bookshops, and food joints that are integral to DU life
- **The Economics Society** — organizes Arthashastra (economics festival), trading competitions, and industry talks

**South Campus (LSR):**
- **LSR's reputation** — India's #1 women's college, with an alumni network that includes CEOs, journalists, and politicians
- **Tarang** — LSR's annual fest
- **South Delhi location** — Lajpat Nagar, GK, and Hauz Khas nearby

**Common to all:**
- **Inter-college events** — DU students freely attend lectures and events across colleges
- **Internship culture** — Delhi's proximity to corporates, NGOs, think tanks, and government makes internships easy to find
- **Metro connectivity** — Vishwavidyalaya station (North) and various South Delhi stations make commuting easy
- **Limited hostel seats** — most outstation students find PGs (₹10,000-18,000/month in North Campus area)
