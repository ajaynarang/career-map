## Why SRCC?

SRCC is India's most prestigious commerce college — and it's not even close. For over 90 years, SRCC has produced India's top business leaders, finance professionals, and entrepreneurs. When Goldman Sachs, McKinsey, or JP Morgan recruit in India at the undergraduate level, SRCC is their first stop.

**The ROI is extraordinary.** A 3-year B.Com (Hons) degree costs under ₹5 lakh total. Graduates receive placement offers of ₹12-25 LPA. That's a return on investment that rivals IITs — at a fraction of the cost.

**For PCM students:** Don't let the "commerce college" label put you off. SRCC's BA Economics (Hons) program is deeply quantitative and attracts many PCM students. The analytical skills from physics and math give you a genuine edge in economics, econometrics, and finance courses.

## How to Get In

There's one path: **CUET (Common University Entrance Test)**.

1. Register for CUET at [cuet.samarth.ac.in](https://cuet.samarth.ac.in) — registration opens January-March
2. Choose relevant subjects: Mathematics, Economics, English, and domain subjects
3. Appear for CUET in May
4. Score in the **top 0.5% percentile** — SRCC B.Com cutoffs are typically 99.5%+ in CUET
5. Participate in DU counselling (June-July) and select SRCC

**Reality check:** CUET replaced the old cutoff system in 2022. About 15 lakh students take CUET. SRCC B.Com (Hons) has roughly 250 seats. The competition is intense, but it's a fair, exam-based system.

**For PCM students specifically:** If you're taking CUET with Math, Physics, and Chemistry as your domain subjects, you can apply for BA Economics (Hons) at SRCC. The math paper in CUET plays directly to your strengths.

## Curriculum & Structure

3-year B.Com (Honours) or BA Economics (Honours). Key features:

- **Strong quantitative foundation** — financial accounting, statistics, econometrics, and business mathematics
- **Industry exposure from Year 1** — guest lectures from CEOs, fund managers, and policy makers
- **SRCC Business Conclave** — one of India's most prestigious business summits, organized by students
- **Research papers and projects** — students regularly publish in economics and business journals
- **Add-on courses** — financial modelling, Bloomberg Terminal training, and CFA preparation workshops

## Campus Life

Located in the heart of North Campus, University of Delhi. Small but vibrant campus:

- **Crossroads** — SRCC's annual business festival, attracting corporate leaders and 10,000+ participants
- **The Commerce Society, Economics Society, and Finance & Investment Cell** — run workshops, trading competitions, and speaker sessions throughout the year
- **North Campus culture** — surrounded by St. Stephen's, Hindu College, Hansraj, and a vibrant college ecosystem
- **Debating and MUN culture** is strong — SRCC students regularly win national competitions
- **Alumni network is SRCC's biggest asset** — SRCC alumni are in leadership positions at every major bank, consulting firm, and corporation in India
