## Why India for Finance?

India's finance sector is booming. With UPI processing 14+ billion transactions per month, a growing mutual fund industry (₹50 lakh crore+ AUM), and global banks setting up their largest offices in Mumbai and Bangalore, the demand for finance professionals has never been higher.

**The cost advantage is massive.** A CA qualification costs under ₹2 lakh total. An economics degree from SRCC or St. Stephen's costs ₹1-3 lakh total. Compare that to ₹55-70 lakh per year in the USA. For families where budget matters, India offers the best ROI in finance education anywhere in the world.

**The PCM advantage:** Actuarial science, quant finance, and algorithmic trading are the fastest-growing and highest-paying finance roles in India. These roles require strong math and programming skills — exactly what PCM students bring. A PCM student at ISI Kolkata or IIM IPM will out-earn most commerce graduates within 5 years.

## The Paths

### Path 1: Chartered Accountancy (CA)
- Register for CA Foundation after Class 10 (appear after Class 12)
- CA Foundation → CA Intermediate → 3-year Articleship → CA Final
- Total timeline: ~5 years after Class 12
- Cost: Under ₹2 lakh for the entire qualification
- Salary: ₹7-12 LPA starting, ₹20-50 LPA with 5-8 years experience

### Path 2: Top Commerce Colleges via CUET
- SRCC, St. Stephen's, Hindu College, Christ University
- B.Com (Hons) or BA Economics — 3-year degree
- CUET exam (May) is the gateway to all central universities
- Follow up with MBA (CAT) or CFA for career acceleration

### Path 3: IIM Integrated Program (IPM) via IPMAT
- IIM Indore and IIM Rohtak offer 5-year integrated programs
- Direct entry after Class 12 — no CAT required
- IPMAT exam in May (quantitative + verbal aptitude)
- Graduates get an IIM MBA without the CAT grind

### Path 4: Statistics & Quant Finance via ISI
- ISI Kolkata (B.Stat/B.Math) is India's top quantitative program
- ISI Admission Test in May — extremely competitive, math-heavy
- Feeds directly into quant trading firms, hedge funds, and data science

### Path 5: Liberal Arts Economics
- Ashoka University, FLAME, Krea University
- Broad economics education with exposure to data science, policy, and finance
- Own admission tests + interviews

## What Parents Should Know

- **CA is not the only option.** Many parents default to CA for finance careers. While CA is excellent, actuarial science, quant finance, and investment banking pay significantly more. Explore all paths before committing.
- **PCM students can do CA.** There's a myth that CA is only for commerce students. Many of India's top CAs have engineering backgrounds. PCM + CA is a power combination.
- **CUET has replaced individual entrance exams** for most central universities. One exam opens doors to 50+ universities. Prepare seriously — it's now the most important exam for non-IIT finance paths.
- **IIM IPM is highly selective.** IIM Indore IPM admits only ~150 students from ~50,000 applicants. But the payoff is enormous — an IIM MBA without CAT.
- **Coaching helps but isn't mandatory.** CUET coaching costs ₹30,000-1 lakh. IPMAT coaching costs ₹50,000-1.5 lakh. Self-study is viable with discipline.
- **Don't ignore internships.** From 2nd year onwards, push your child to intern at banks, CA firms, or fintech startups. Real-world experience matters more than marks after a point.
