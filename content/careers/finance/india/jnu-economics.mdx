## Why JNU for Economics?

JNU's Centre for Economic Studies and Planning (CESP) is widely considered **India's best economics department** — not in terms of placements, but in terms of intellectual depth, research quality, and the caliber of economists it produces. If your child wants to be an economist, policy maker, or academic researcher, JNU is the gold standard.

**Why JNU economics is unique in India:**

- **India's most rigorous economics training** — JNU economics is deeply theoretical and mathematically rigorous. The MA Economics programme is considered tougher and more respected than most, including DSE (Delhi School of Economics) in certain academic circles
- **Policy impact** — JNU economists have shaped India's economic policy for decades. Alumni include RBI governors, chief economic advisors, NITI Aayog members, and World Bank economists
- **Research powerhouse** — faculty publish in top international journals (American Economic Review, Econometrica, Journal of Development Economics)
- **Practically free** — ₹12,000/year tuition, ₹100-200/month hostel. The total cost of a 3-year BA is under ₹1 lakh
- **CUET entry** — accessible admission through a standardized exam

**The honest take:** JNU economics does not produce investment bankers or management consultants directly. If your child wants ₹30 LPA campus placements, JNU is the wrong choice. But if they want to understand how economies work, influence policy, prepare for the Indian Economic Service, or eventually become a professor/researcher — JNU is unmatched. Many JNU MA Economics graduates also go on to top MBA programmes (IIMs, ISB) or PhD programmes abroad (Harvard, MIT, LSE) with full funding.

## How to Get In

Entry through **CUET**:

1. Register for CUET at [cuet.samarth.ac.in](https://cuet.samarth.ac.in)
2. Choose Mathematics and Economics/Business Studies as domain subjects
3. Score in the top 3-5% percentile
4. Apply through JNU's admission portal

**CUET cutoffs for JNU Economics (approximate):**
- BA Economics: 95%+ CUET percentile
- MA Economics: Separate JNU entrance (JNUEE)

**For PCM students:** JNU economics welcomes PCM students. The mathematics background gives a genuine advantage in econometrics and mathematical economics courses.

## What Makes It Special

- **Intellectual culture** — JNU is not just a university, it's an intellectual movement. Debates, seminars, and discussions happen everywhere — in classrooms, hostels, dhabas, and on the lawns
- **Interdisciplinary breadth** — economics students interact with students from international relations, political science, history, and linguistics. This creates economists who understand context, not just models
- **Faculty are practicing economists** — many JNU faculty advise the government, write for newspapers, and engage in active policy debates
- **Progressive, questioning environment** — JNU teaches students to question assumptions, challenge orthodoxy, and think independently. This is invaluable for economists
- **Global network** — JNU alumni are at every major economic institution globally

## Campus and Student Life

1,000-acre forested campus in South Delhi:

- **Dhaba culture** — JNU's dhabas (Ganga Dhaba, Sabarmati Dhaba) are famous. Conversations over chai at midnight are a JNU tradition
- **Seminar culture** — public lectures by Nobel laureates, policymakers, and thinkers happen regularly. Attendance is open to all students
- **Political engagement** — JNU students are actively engaged in political and social discourse. This is not for everyone, but it sharpens analytical thinking
- **Hostel life** — guaranteed hostel at ₹100-200/month. The hostel is where real JNU learning happens — discussions, debates, and lifelong friendships
- **Delhi access** — proximity to Parliament, ministries, think tanks (CPR, ICRIER), and foreign embassies. Policy discussions aren't theoretical at JNU — they're tangible
- **Nature** — the campus is a biodiversity zone with deer, peacocks, and dense forest. Evening walks are restorative
- **Affordability** — JNU is possibly the cheapest quality education in India. A student from any economic background can thrive here
