## Why IIM Indore IPM?

The IIM Indore IPM (Integrated Programme in Management) is one of India's most strategic career moves for a Class 12 student. Here's why: it gives your child an IIM MBA — the most coveted management degree in India — without ever having to take the CAT exam. While their peers spend 4 years in college and then 2 more years preparing for and completing an MBA, IPM students finish everything in 5 years with an IIM degree in hand.

**The placement numbers tell the story.** IPM graduates receive the same placements as IIM Indore's flagship MBA batch. Average packages of ₹25-30 LPA, with top offers reaching ₹40-55 LPA from McKinsey, BCG, Goldman Sachs, and other elite recruiters. For a total investment of ₹30 lakh over 5 years, the ROI is outstanding.

**For PCM students:** IPMAT's Quantitative Aptitude section is heavily mathematical — exactly where PCM students excel. While commerce students struggle with probability, permutations, and algebra, PCM students breeze through. This is your competitive advantage.

## How to Get In

The admission process has two stages:

1. **IPMAT (IIM Aptitude Test):** Register at [iimidr.ac.in](https://www.iimidr.ac.in) — registration opens March, exam in May
   - Section 1: Quantitative Aptitude — MCQs (40 questions, 40 minutes)
   - Section 2: Quantitative Aptitude — Short Answer (20 questions, 40 minutes)
   - Section 3: Verbal Ability — MCQs (40 questions, 40 minutes)
2. **Personal Interview:** Shortlisted candidates (top 1,500-2,000 from ~50,000 applicants) are interviewed at IIM Indore or designated centres

**Reality check:** About 50,000 students take IPMAT. Only 150 are admitted. The effective acceptance rate is 0.3%. But for students who prepare specifically for IPMAT (it's different from JEE or CUET), the odds improve significantly.

**Preparation tip:** IPMAT quant is more about speed and accuracy than difficulty. Practice quantitative aptitude from RS Aggarwal and Arun Sharma. For verbal ability, read extensively and practice reading comprehension. Mock tests are essential — take at least 30 full-length mocks before the exam.

## Programme Structure

5-year integrated programme with a unique structure:

- **Years 1-3 (Foundation):** Economics, accounting, statistics, psychology, sociology, and liberal arts. This broad foundation is intentional — IIMs believe great managers need diverse perspectives
- **Years 4-5 (MBA):** Full IIM MBA curriculum alongside the regular 2-year MBA batch. Finance, marketing, operations, strategy — choose your specialization
- **Summer internships** in Years 2-4, with Year 4 summer internship being the critical "pre-placement" internship (like regular MBA students)
- **International exchange** opportunities with partner business schools in Europe, USA, and Asia
- **Live projects and case-study pedagogy** — the Harvard case method is central to IIM teaching

## Campus Life

IIM Indore's 193-acre campus is one of the most beautiful B-school campuses in India:

- **Fully residential programme** — hostel life is mandatory and a huge part of the experience
- **Student clubs** — Finance Club, Consulting Club, Entrepreneurship Cell, and 30+ others
- **Utsaha and Ahvan** — annual management and cultural festivals
- **Peer learning** — IPM students interact with MBA students, PhD scholars, and executive education participants, creating a rich learning environment
- **Indore is affordable** — the city is known for great food (famous street food!) and low living costs
