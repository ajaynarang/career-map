## Why ISI Kolkata?

ISI Kolkata is India's most elite institution for quantitative minds — and the best-kept secret in Indian education. Founded by P.C. Mahalanobis (the father of Indian statistics), ISI has produced more quant traders, data scientists, and mathematical economists per capita than any institution in Asia.

**The numbers are remarkable.** ISI B.Stat/B.Math graduates are recruited by the world's top quant trading firms — Jane Street, Tower Research, DE Shaw — at packages of ₹40-80 LPA. Some international offers cross ₹1 Crore. And the degree costs virtually nothing: no tuition, and hostel charges of ₹15,000 per year.

**Why PCM students should care:** ISI is tailor-made for students who love mathematics. If your child finds JEE Math easy and enjoys proof-based problem solving, ISI is where they belong. The ISI Admission Test is considered harder than JEE Advanced in pure mathematics — but for the right student, it's the most rewarding exam in India.

## How to Get In

The ISI Admission Test is a two-stage process:

1. **Register** at [isical.ac.in/admissions](https://www.isical.ac.in/admissions) — registration opens February, exam in May
2. **Stage 1 — Objective Test:** 30 multiple-choice questions in mathematics (2 hours). This is the screening round
3. **Stage 2 — Subjective Test:** 8 proof-based problems in mathematics (2 hours). You must write full proofs, not just answers
4. **Interview:** Shortlisted candidates are interviewed at ISI Kolkata (June-July)

**Reality check:** About 6,000-8,000 students apply. 60 seats total (30 B.Stat + 30 B.Math). The subjective test requires mathematical maturity beyond school level — familiarity with combinatorics, number theory, geometry, and algebra at the olympiad level is expected.

**Preparation tip:** Solve past ISI Admission Test papers (available on the ISI website). Study from "Challenges and Thrills of Pre-College Mathematics" (Venkatchala) and "Problem Solving Strategies" (Engel). If your child has done well in Math Olympiad (RMO/INMO), ISI is a natural fit.

## Curriculum & Structure

3-year B.Stat (Honours) or B.Math (Honours). This is not a typical college — ISI is a research institute:

- **Pure mathematics rigor** — real analysis, linear algebra, probability theory, and measure theory from Year 1
- **Statistics and data science** — statistical inference, stochastic processes, machine learning, and time series analysis
- **Computing** — R, Python, C programming integrated throughout the curriculum
- **Research orientation** — students work with faculty on research problems from 2nd year. Many publish papers before graduating
- **Small batch size** — 30 students per program means exceptional faculty attention (faculty-to-student ratio of roughly 1:3)

## Campus Life

ISI's Baranagar campus in North Kolkata is an oasis of intellectual pursuit:

- **The culture is intensely academic** — late-night math discussions, problem-solving sessions, and seminar attendance are the norm
- **Kolkata is affordable** — living costs are among the lowest of any major Indian city
- **Famous ISI seminars** — world-class mathematicians and statisticians regularly visit and lecture
- **Small, tight-knit community** — 30 students per batch means everyone knows everyone. The alumni bond is exceptionally strong
- **Library** — one of the best mathematics libraries in Asia, with access to every major journal

## Career Paths After ISI

ISI graduates follow three main paths:

1. **Quant Finance:** Jane Street, Tower Research, DE Shaw, Citadel — starting ₹40-80 LPA
2. **Higher Studies:** PhD at MIT, Stanford, Princeton, Cambridge in math, statistics, or economics
3. **Data Science/Tech:** Google, Microsoft, Amazon — starting ₹25-40 LPA

The ISI network in quant finance is legendary — ISI alumni at top firms actively recruit from their alma mater, creating a self-reinforcing pipeline.
