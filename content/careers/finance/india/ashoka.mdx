## Why Ashoka University?

Ashoka University has done something remarkable in Indian higher education: it has built, from scratch, a world-class liberal arts university that competes with the best globally. Founded in 2014 by a consortium of India's most successful entrepreneurs and business leaders (Ashish Dhawan, Pramath Raj Sinha, and others), Ashoka has attracted faculty from Harvard, Yale, Oxford, and MIT.

**For finance careers, Ashoka offers something unique:** a rigorous economics programme combined with the breadth of a liberal arts education. While SRCC and St. Stephen's teach economics within a traditional Indian university framework, Ashoka follows the American liberal arts model — your child will study economics alongside courses in philosophy, history, psychology, and computer science. This interdisciplinary approach produces graduates who can think across domains — exactly what top consulting firms and global organisations value.

**The financial aid story is important.** Ashoka's sticker price (₹9.5L/year) is high by Indian standards. But over 50% of students receive financial aid, and the Founder's Scholarship can cover up to 100% of costs. If your child is talented, Ashoka makes it work regardless of family income.

## How to Get In

Ashoka's admission process is holistic — more like US universities than Indian ones:

1. **Online Application:** Submit through [ashoka.edu.in/apply](https://www.ashoka.edu.in/apply). Includes academic transcripts, essays (why Ashoka, intellectual interests), and teacher recommendations
2. **Ashoka Aptitude Test:** Online test assessing quantitative reasoning, verbal ability, and analytical writing. Not as math-heavy as IPMAT — it tests how you think, not what you've memorised
3. **Interview:** Shortlisted candidates have a 20-30 minute interview (online or in-person). The panel wants to understand your curiosity and intellectual depth
4. **Offer:** Admission offers come with financial aid details. You'll know exactly what you'll pay before you commit

**Reality check:** Ashoka receives about 10,000-12,000 applications for roughly 600-700 seats across all programmes. For Economics specifically, the competition is stiff — Ashoka's Economics department has gained a reputation that attracts top students from across India.

**What Ashoka looks for:** Intellectual curiosity above all. They want students who read widely, ask questions, and have genuine interests beyond textbooks. A student who has read Amartya Sen, built a financial literacy project in their community, or written a blog analysing economic trends will stand out more than someone with 98% marks but no intellectual spark.

## Curriculum & Structure

4-year BSc in Economics or Economics & Finance:

- **Foundation Courses (Year 1):** Critical Thinking, Indian Civilisations, and introductory courses across disciplines. Every student, regardless of major, takes these — it's the liberal arts core
- **Economics Major (Years 2-4):** Microeconomics, Macroeconomics, Econometrics, Game Theory, Behavioural Economics, Development Economics, Financial Economics
- **Data Science integration** — Python, R, and data analysis are woven into the economics curriculum
- **Double major option** — Economics + Computer Science is a popular combination that opens doors to fintech and quant finance
- **Capstone project** in Year 4 — original research guided by faculty
- **Study abroad** — semester exchange at Yale, King's College London, Sciences Po, and other partner institutions

## Campus Life

A modern, purpose-built residential campus 1 hour from Delhi:

- **Fully residential** — every student lives on campus for all 4 years. This creates an intense intellectual community
- **Young Scholars Programme (YSP)** — intensive 3-week pre-semester programme for incoming students
- **Ashoka Economics Society** runs workshops, paper discussions, and invites policy makers and economists for talks
- **Entrepreneurship culture** — Ashoka students have launched fintech startups, investment clubs, and social enterprises
- **Faculty accessibility** — with a student-to-faculty ratio of 10:1, professors know students by name. Office hours culture is strong
- **Diverse student body** — students from 28+ states and 10+ countries. This diversity is a significant part of the learning experience

## After Ashoka

Ashoka Economics graduates follow diverse paths:

1. **Consulting:** McKinsey, BCG, Bain, Dalberg — ₹15-25 LPA starting. Ashoka is now a target school for all MBB firms
2. **Finance:** Goldman Sachs, JP Morgan, Avendus — roles in investment banking and equity research
3. **Higher Studies:** PhD at top global universities (Yale, Princeton, LSE, Oxford). Ashoka sends more students to economics PhD programmes abroad than most Indian universities
4. **Policy & Development:** World Bank, NITI Aayog, J-PAL — for students interested in economics as a force for social impact
5. **MBA:** Top performers enter IIM-A/B/C or international MBA programmes (Wharton, HBS, INSEAD)
