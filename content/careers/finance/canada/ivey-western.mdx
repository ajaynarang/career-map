## Why Ivey Business School?

Ivey is Canada's most intense business programme — and it produces an outsized share of Canada's business leaders. What makes Ivey unique globally is its commitment to the case method: 100% of Ivey's curriculum is taught through real-world business cases. This is the same method used at Harvard Business School, and Ivey is the only school outside Harvard that uses it exclusively at both the undergraduate and MBA levels.

**The placement record is outstanding.** Ivey HBA graduates consistently achieve the highest starting salaries among Canadian undergraduate business programmes. About 35% of graduates enter financial services (investment banking, asset management, private equity), and another 25% enter consulting. Bay Street firms consider Ivey a core target school alongside Rotman and Queen's Commerce.

**The case method advantage:** After 2 years of analysing 600+ real business cases and debating solutions in class, Ivey graduates walk into interviews with a skill that other business school graduates simply don't have: the ability to quickly analyse complex, ambiguous situations and recommend a course of action. This is exactly what banks and consulting firms test in their interview processes.

**The unique structure:** Ivey doesn't admit students directly into business school from high school. Instead, students get AEO (Advanced Entry Opportunity) — a conditional seat — and then spend Years 1-2 at Western University studying any subject (engineering, science, social science, or arts). They enter Ivey's HBA programme in Year 3. This means your child gets a broader education and a more mature perspective before diving into business.

## How to Get In

Ivey's admission is a two-step process:

### Step 1: AEO (Advanced Entry Opportunity) — Apply from High School
1. **Apply through OUAC** at [ouac.on.ca](https://www.ouac.on.ca) to Western University, with Ivey AEO as a supplementary application
2. **Academic requirements:** 90%+ in Class 12 top-6 courses, with strong Math and English
3. **AEO Application:** Short essays on leadership, community involvement, and career goals + extracurricular profile
4. **Deadline:** February for September intake

### Step 2: Activate AEO — Enter Ivey HBA in Year 3
1. Maintain 80%+ average in Years 1-2 at Western
2. Complete Ivey's prerequisite courses (Business 1220, Economics, Statistics)
3. Continue demonstrating extracurricular leadership
4. If conditions are met, you enter the Ivey HBA in September of Year 3

**Reality check:** About 3,000 students apply for AEO, and roughly 300 receive offers. But not all AEO holders make it to Ivey — you must maintain the required average and show continued leadership. About 600 students total enter Ivey HBA each year (including non-AEO students who apply in Year 2).

**For international students:** The AEO process is straightforward. The key differentiator is your supplementary application — demonstrate genuine leadership (not just participation) in extracurriculars and articulate clear career goals.

## Curriculum & Structure

The Ivey HBA is a 2-year intensive programme (Years 3-4 of undergraduate):

- **Year 3 (HBA1):** Core business courses — Finance, Accounting, Marketing, Strategy, Operations, Organisational Behaviour. All taught through cases. You'll analyse 300+ cases in this year alone
- **Year 4 (HBA2):** Electives and specialisations — Advanced Corporate Finance, Investment Management, Private Equity, Fintech, Entrepreneurial Finance. Plus a consulting project with a real company
- **Case-method teaching:** Every class, you read a 15-20 page case the night before, analyse it, and come to class ready to debate. Cold calls are common — the professor may ask you to "open" the case (present your analysis to the class). Participation counts for 30-50% of your grade
- **Summer internship between HBA1 and HBA2** — the critical recruiting moment. Bay Street banks and consulting firms hire summer interns in September-November of HBA1 (yes, recruiting starts in the first month of Ivey)
- **Dual degree options:** HBA + Engineering (5 years), HBA + Law (6 years), HBA + Science — these combinations are powerful and increasingly popular

**The first 2 years (pre-Ivey):** Students study any subject at Western. Popular choices for finance-oriented students include Economics, Applied Mathematics, or Computer Science. This broad foundation makes Ivey graduates more versatile than students who studied only business for 4 years.

## Campus Life

Western University's campus in London, Ontario, is consistently rated one of Canada's most beautiful:

- **Campus culture is vibrant** — Western is known for school spirit, strong social life, and a close-knit community. London (Ontario) is a mid-size city, so the university IS the social centre
- **Ivey Building** — a modern, purpose-built facility with tiered classrooms designed for case discussions, Bloomberg terminals, and meeting rooms
- **Ivey Finance Association (IFA)** — runs stock pitch competitions, networking events with Bay Street professionals, and interview preparation workshops
- **Ivey Case Competition Club** — competes in national and international case competitions. Ivey teams regularly win
- **London, ON is affordable** — living costs are significantly lower than Toronto (CAD 10,000-15,000/year for accommodation). This makes Ivey more affordable overall than Rotman despite similar tuition
- **Indian community at Western** — growing and active. Indian Students' Association organises cultural events, Diwali celebrations, and mentorship programmes
- **Purple and white pride** — Western Mustangs have one of the strongest varsity athletics programmes in Canada. Football games are a major social event

## Ivey vs. Rotman: Which to Choose?

| Factor | Ivey (Western) | Rotman (U of T) |
|--------|---------------|-----------------|
| Teaching style | 100% case method | Traditional lectures + cases |
| Entry point | Year 3 (after 2 years at Western) | Direct from high school |
| Living cost | Lower (London, ON) | Higher (Toronto) |
| Recruiting access | Bay Street recruits heavily from Ivey | Walk to Bay Street from campus |
| Class size | 600 (intimate, tight-knit) | 700 (larger, more diverse) |
| Alumni network | Extremely loyal, tight-knit | Large, diverse network |
| Best for | Consulting, IB, leadership roles | Finance, accounting, CPA track |

Both are excellent. If your child thrives in discussion-based learning and wants the most intense business training available, choose Ivey. If they want direct admission into business from Class 12 and prefer a large city, choose Rotman.
