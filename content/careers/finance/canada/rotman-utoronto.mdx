## Why Rotman Commerce?

Rotman Commerce at the University of Toronto is Canada's premier undergraduate business programme and the direct pipeline to Bay Street — Canada's equivalent of Wall Street. Toronto is Canada's financial capital, home to the headquarters of all five major Canadian banks, the Toronto Stock Exchange, and some of the world's largest pension funds (CPP Investments, Ontario Teachers' Pension Plan).

**The Bay Street advantage is real.** Rotman Commerce graduates dominate hiring at RBC Capital Markets, TD Securities, BMO, and every major financial institution in Canada. The programme's location in downtown Toronto means internship offices are often within walking distance of campus. Students routinely intern at banks during the semester, not just during summer.

**The immigration pathway makes this a compelling choice.** After a 4-year Rotman BCom, your child gets a 3-year Post-Graduation Work Permit (PGWP). After 1 year of Canadian work experience in finance (analyst roles at banks or accounting firms qualify), they can apply for Permanent Residency through Express Entry. The entire path from student to PR can take as little as 5 years.

**Compared to the US:** Rotman Commerce costs roughly half of Wharton or Stern (₹50L/year vs ₹73L/year). The starting salaries are lower (CAD 65-75K vs USD 85-95K), but the cost of living is lower, the immigration pathway is far clearer, and the total ROI — especially accounting for the near-guaranteed PR — is arguably better.

## How to Get In

1. **Apply through OUAC (Ontario Universities Application Centre)** at [ouac.on.ca](https://www.ouac.on.ca) — International students use OUAC 105
2. **Academic requirements:** 90%+ in Class 12 with strong performance in Mathematics and English. Calculus is preferred if available
3. **Supplementary Application:** This is critical and unique to Rotman Commerce. You'll complete 3 timed video responses and written short answers. Topics cover teamwork, ethics, career goals, and why Rotman
4. **Deadline:** January for September intake
5. **Offers:** Conditional offers arrive February-May, with final confirmation after board results

**Reality check:** Rotman Commerce receives about 5,000 applications for roughly 700 seats. The supplementary application is where most international students are differentiated — strong grades get you considered, but the video essays determine admission.

**Preparation tip for the supplementary application:** Practice speaking clearly and concisely on camera. The video essays are timed (1-2 minutes), so rehearse structuring your thoughts quickly. Be authentic — Rotman can spot rehearsed scripts.

## Curriculum & Structure

4-year Bachelor of Commerce (BCom) with three specialist streams:

- **Finance & Economics Specialist:** Corporate Finance, Investments, Financial Modelling, Derivatives, Econometrics. The most popular stream for students targeting banking careers
- **Accounting Specialist:** Prepares students for CPA (Chartered Professional Accountant) designation. Canada's CPA is highly respected globally
- **Management Specialist:** Strategy, marketing, organisational behaviour — for students targeting consulting or general management

Key features:
- **Rotman Research Opportunities** — work with faculty on cutting-edge finance research
- **Cross-disciplinary courses** — take courses in Computer Science, Statistics, or Math through U of T's Faculty of Arts & Science
- **Professional Experience Year (PEY)** — optional 12-16 month paid internship between 2nd and 3rd year. Typical PEY salaries: CAD 45,000-65,000
- **Bloomberg Terminal access** — Rotman's trading lab has Bloomberg terminals, giving students hands-on experience with professional tools
- **Case competitions** — Rotman students regularly compete in national and international case competitions (NIBC, Rotman International Trading Competition)

## Campus Life

U of T's St. George campus is in the heart of downtown Toronto:

- **Downtown location** — Bay Street (Canada's financial district) is a 10-minute walk. The Toronto Stock Exchange, major bank headquarters, and consulting firm offices are all within reach
- **Rotman Commerce Students' Association (RCSA)** — runs career workshops, networking events, and the annual Rotman Commerce Conference
- **Finance & Investment Club** — student-run investment fund, market analysis, and recruiter events
- **Toronto is multicultural** — one of the world's most diverse cities. Indian community in Brampton, Scarborough, and Mississauga is massive. Indian restaurants, temples, and grocery stores are everywhere
- **U of T campus is beautiful** — historic stone buildings modeled on Oxford, alongside modern towers. The Robarts Library is one of the largest in North America
- **Living costs in Toronto** — expect CAD 15,000-22,000/year for accommodation and expenses. On-campus residence in first year, then shared apartments
