## Why Columbia for Finance?

Columbia is the only elite university located in the financial capital of the world. The campus at Morningside Heights is a 30-minute subway ride from Wall Street, a 15-minute ride from midtown Manhattan (where Goldman Sachs, JP Morgan, and Morgan Stanley have their headquarters), and directly connected to the densest concentration of financial institutions on Earth.

**This location advantage is not theoretical — it's practical.** Columbia students attend recruiting events at bulge bracket banks on weekday evenings and return to campus for class the next morning. Spring internship fairs, "Superday" interviews, and networking coffees happen without the flight-and-hotel logistics that students at Stanford or Chicago face. For breaking into investment banking, private equity, or hedge funds, Columbia's proximity to Wall Street is an unmatched advantage.

**The 3-2 Program is extraordinary.** Select Columbia College undergraduates can apply to Columbia Business School during junior year and earn both a BA and an MBA in just 5 years total. This accelerates a career in finance by 2-3 years compared to the traditional path (4-year BA + 2-3 years of work + 2-year MBA).

**Financial aid is world-class.** Columbia is need-blind for international students and meets 100% of demonstrated need. Families earning under $66,000/year pay nothing.

## How to Get In

Columbia's acceptance rate is **3.9%**. The admissions process is highly holistic.

**What Columbia looks for:**
- **Intellectual vitality** — Columbia's Core Curriculum is central to its identity. They want students who love ideas broadly, not just finance
- **Quantitative strength** — strong math grades and test scores are essential for economics/finance tracks
- **NYC engagement** — the "Why Columbia" essay should demonstrate genuine understanding of how NYC and Columbia's specific resources align with your child's goals
- **Leadership with impact** — not just titles, but real contributions to organizations, communities, or projects

**Timeline:**
- Early Decision: November 1 (binding — higher acceptance rate ~10%)
- Regular Decision: January 1
- Decisions: Late March

**Strategy for Indian applicants:** Apply Early Decision if Columbia is the top choice. The ED acceptance rate is roughly 2.5x the regular rate. In the essays, show awareness of Columbia's Core Curriculum and how NYC specifically enables your finance/economics ambitions — not generic "I love New York" but specific resources, firms, and opportunities.

## Curriculum & Structure

4-year Bachelor of Arts. Columbia undergraduates enter through Columbia College (liberal arts) and choose economics or related majors.

- **Core Curriculum (first 2 years):** Literature Humanities, Contemporary Civilization, Art Humanities, Music Humanities, Frontiers of Science, University Writing. About one-third of your coursework. This makes Columbia economics students unusually well-read and articulate — a genuine advantage in finance interviews
- **BA in Economics:** Micro, Macro, Econometrics, and 6+ economics electives. Strong options in financial economics, game theory, industrial organization, and development
- **BA in Financial Economics:** Columbia's premier program for undergraduates targeting finance careers. Combines economics with accounting, corporate finance, and quantitative methods. Highly selective within the university
- **Economics-Mathematics:** The most quantitative track. Combines rigorous math (real analysis, probability theory) with economics. Ideal preparation for quant finance or PhD programs
- **3-2 Program (BA + MBA):** Apply in junior year to Columbia Business School. If admitted, complete the MBA in 2 years after graduating. Graduate at 23-24 with an MBA from a top-3 business school
- **Cross-registration:** Take courses at Columbia Engineering, Barnard College, and the School of International and Public Affairs (SIPA)

## Wall Street Pipeline

Columbia's finance recruiting infrastructure is extraordinary:

- **On-campus recruiting:** Goldman Sachs, JP Morgan, Morgan Stanley, Bank of America, and other bulge brackets recruit directly on campus every fall. Information sessions, resume drops, and first-round interviews happen at Columbia
- **Columbia Finance Society and Economics Society** — among the most active student finance organizations in the country. Resume workshops, mock interviews, and networking events throughout the year
- **Spring weeks and sophomore internships** — Columbia students access early-stage recruiting programs at major banks
- **Junior summer internships** — the critical gateway to full-time offers. Columbia places a significant percentage of interested students into IB/PE/HF internships
- **Alumni network on Wall Street** — Columbia's alumni presence in finance is second only to Wharton/Harvard. The network is active and eager to help current students

## Campus Life

- **Morningside Heights, Manhattan** — Columbia's campus is a green, gated oasis in upper Manhattan. Safe, walkable, and adjacent to Riverside Park
- **NYC is the ultimate campus extension** — museums, concerts, restaurants, and the world's most diverse cultural scene are accessible by subway
- **Indian community is large** — Columbia's South Asian community is one of the biggest in the Ivy League. Diwali celebrations, cultural shows, and networking events are regular. Jackson Heights in Queens is one of the largest Indian neighborhoods outside India
- **Finance culture is strong** — Columbia undergrads are career-oriented. Networking events, recruiting prep, and finance club activities are prominent parts of campus life, especially in sophomore and junior years
- **Housing:** Columbia guarantees 4 years of housing, which is unusual and valuable in NYC
- **Cost of living:** NYC is expensive, but Columbia's guaranteed housing and dining plans help control costs
