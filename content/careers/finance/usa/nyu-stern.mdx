## Why NYU Stern?

NYU Stern's greatest asset is four letters: N-Y-C. The school sits in Greenwich Village, Manhattan — literally 10 minutes by subway from Wall Street. No other business school on earth offers this level of proximity to the global financial capital. When Goldman Sachs or JP Morgan need interns, they walk to NYU. When Stern students want to network, they walk to Wall Street.

**The Wall Street pipeline is real.** Approximately 35-40% of Stern undergraduates enter investment banking or finance roles directly after graduation. The placement rate into bulge bracket banks (Goldman, JP Morgan, Morgan Stanley) is among the highest of any undergraduate programme globally. Stern is the #1 feeder school to Wall Street at the undergraduate level by volume.

**For Indian students, Stern offers a practical advantage:** location-based networking. While Wharton is in Philadelphia (90 minutes from NYC) and UChicago is in the Midwest, Stern students can attend Wall Street events on a Tuesday evening and be back on campus for class Wednesday morning. This proximity translates into more internship opportunities, more coffee chats, and ultimately more offers.

## How to Get In

1. **Apply via Common App** at [nyu.edu/admissions](https://www.nyu.edu/admissions/undergraduate-admissions.html). Select NYU Stern as your school
2. **Early Decision I (November 1)** or **Early Decision II (January 1):** Both are binding. ED gives a meaningful admissions advantage — ED acceptance rate is roughly double the regular rate
3. **Regular Decision (January 5):** Non-binding. Decisions in late March
4. **Stern-specific essays:** Why Stern? Why business? How will you contribute to the Stern community?

**Reality check:** Stern receives about 12,000 applications for roughly 700 seats. The overall acceptance rate is about 7%. The average SAT for admitted students is 1510+.

**What sets a strong Stern applicant apart:**
- **Genuine passion for business/finance** — not just good grades, but evidence of engagement (investment clubs, small businesses, financial literacy projects)
- **NYC connection** — demonstrating that you specifically want to be in New York and leverage its opportunities
- **Quantitative strength** — strong math scores and coursework
- **Diversity** — Stern values international perspectives and varied backgrounds

## Curriculum & Structure

4-year BS in Business with concentrations:

- **Stern Core (Years 1-2):** Financial Accounting, Statistics, Microeconomics, Marketing, Organizational Behavior, Information Technology. Stern's core is comprehensive
- **Finance Concentration (Years 2-4):** Corporate Finance, Investments, Financial Modelling, Derivatives, Fixed Income — the full Wall Street toolkit
- **Quantitative Finance** — for students who want to combine heavy math with finance. Covers stochastic calculus, options pricing, and algorithmic trading
- **Social Impact Core** — unique to Stern. Every student takes a course on business and society, with a required international study trip
- **Cross-registration at NYU** — take courses in Math, CS, or Data Science at NYU's Courant Institute (one of the world's top math departments)
- **Study abroad** — Stern's Global Exchange programme sends students to LBS (London), NUS (Singapore), HEC Paris, and HKUST

## Campus Life

NYU has no traditional campus — New York City IS the campus:

- **Greenwich Village** is one of NYC's most vibrant neighbourhoods — restaurants, cafes, bookstores, and nightlife within walking distance
- **Stern Student Government and Finance Society** — run speaker series with Wall Street executives, trading competitions, and career prep workshops
- **Finance clubs start recruiting in freshman year** — the culture is career-oriented from day one. Mock interviews, resume reviews, and networking workshops are constant
- **Indian community at NYU is large** — NYU has one of the biggest international student populations of any US university
- **Internships happen during the semester** — many Stern students intern part-time at NYC firms during the school year, not just during summer
- **Cost of living is high** — NYC is expensive. Budget for this. But the earning potential post-graduation more than compensates

## The Stern Advantage for Indian Students

Stern offers several specific advantages for Indian students targeting finance:

1. **Alumni network in India is strong** — Stern alumni hold senior positions at Goldman Sachs India, JP Morgan India, Kotak, and ICICI
2. **STEM-designated Business Analytics concentration** — qualifies for 3 years of OPT (instead of 1 year), giving more time to secure H-1B sponsorship
3. **NYC recruiting access** — all major banks have their headquarters or major offices in NYC. Proximity = more opportunities
4. **Stern-Courant dual degree** — combine Stern business with Courant math/CS for a devastating quant finance profile
