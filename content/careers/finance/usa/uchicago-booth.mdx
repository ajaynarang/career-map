## Why UChicago?

The University of Chicago has the most influential economics department in the world. The "Chicago School of Economics" — free markets, rational expectations, efficient markets hypothesis — has shaped global economic policy for 80+ years. More Nobel Prizes in Economics have been awarded to UChicago faculty and alumni than to any other university. Milton Friedman, Eugene Fama, Gary Becker, and Richard Thaler all taught here.

**For finance careers, UChicago offers something different from Wharton or Stern:** deep intellectual rigor. While Wharton teaches you how to do finance, UChicago teaches you how to think about finance. This distinction matters enormously for careers in quant trading, hedge funds, and economic research — the highest-paying corners of finance.

**The quant finance pipeline is world-class.** Citadel (Ken Griffin, UChicago alumnus) and other top quant firms recruit heavily from UChicago. The BA in Economics combined with Statistics or Mathematics produces graduates who can compete for the most intellectually demanding — and highest-paying — roles in finance.

**Financial aid matches the best.** UChicago meets 100% of demonstrated need for all students, including internationals. Families earning under $75,000 pay nothing. The Odyssey Program eliminates loans for families earning under $125,000.

## How to Get In

1. **Apply via Common App or Coalition App** at [collegeadmissions.uchicago.edu](https://collegeadmissions.uchicago.edu)
2. **Early Decision I (November 1)** or **Early Decision II (January 2)** or **Early Action (November 1, non-binding)** or **Regular Decision (January 2)**
3. **UChicago's famous "uncommon essay"** — one of the most creative college essay prompts in existence. Examples from past years: "Where have all the flowers gone?" or "What can actually be divided by zero?" These essays test creativity, intellectual depth, and willingness to play with ideas

**Reality check:** UChicago receives 38,000+ applications for about 1,800 seats. The acceptance rate is 5.2%. For Indian students, the key differentiator is intellectual curiosity — not standardized test scores (which UChicago has made test-optional) but genuine passion for ideas.

**What UChicago specifically values:**
- **Intellectual depth over breadth** — they want students who go deep into topics, not those who do 20 clubs superficially
- **Love of the "life of the mind"** — UChicago's motto is literally "Crescat scientia; vita excolatur" (Let knowledge grow; let life be enriched)
- **Independent thinking** — UChicago celebrates students who question conventional wisdom
- **The "uncommon essay" reveals everything** — spend significant time on this. It's your best opportunity to show who you really are intellectually

## Curriculum & Structure

4-year BA in Economics (the most popular major at UChicago):

- **Core Curriculum (Year 1-2):** UChicago's legendary Core requires all students to take sequences in Humanities, Social Sciences, Natural Sciences, Math, and Foreign Language. This means your child studies Plato, learns calculus, and reads great literature — all before specializing in economics
- **Economics Major (Years 2-4):** Price Theory (Micro), National Income (Macro), Econometrics — the "Chicago style" of economics is rigorous, mathematical, and empirical
- **Math-Econ specialization** — for students who want the strongest quantitative preparation. Covers real analysis, optimization, and advanced econometrics
- **Statistics major/minor** — UChicago's Statistics department is top-3 globally. Combining Econ + Stats is the ideal preparation for quant finance
- **Research with faculty** — UChicago has more economics research centres than any other university (Becker Friedman Institute, Stigler Center, Energy & Environment Lab, Crime Lab). Undergrads can join these
- **Quarter system** — UChicago runs on quarters (4 terms/year instead of 2 semesters), meaning students take more courses and cover more breadth

## Campus Life

UChicago's Hyde Park campus is an intellectual oasis on Chicago's South Side:

- **"Where fun goes to die"** — the famous (self-deprecating) student saying reflects UChicago's intense academic culture. Students study hard and argue about ideas for fun
- **Scav Hunt** — the world's largest scavenger hunt, a UChicago tradition where teams compete to find or create absurd items over 4 days
- **Doc Films** — the oldest continuously running student film society in the country, screening films every night
- **The campus is stunning** — Gothic architecture modeled on Oxford, with Harry Potter-esque libraries and cloistered courtyards
- **Chicago is a world-class city** — affordable compared to NYC, with excellent food (including a massive Indian community on Devon Avenue), music, art, and professional sports
- **Indian student community** — UChicago has a significant Indian student population and active South Asian student organisations

## After UChicago

UChicago Economics graduates have three primary paths:

1. **Quant Finance/Trading:** Citadel, Jane Street, Two Sigma, DE Shaw, Bridgewater — $200,000-400,000+ starting. UChicago is a top-3 feeder school for quant firms
2. **Investment Banking/Consulting:** Goldman Sachs, McKinsey, BCG — $140,000-160,000 starting. Strong Wall Street and consulting placement despite not being in NYC
3. **PhD in Economics:** UChicago sends more students to top economics PhD programmes than almost any other university. If your child wants to become an economist or professor, this is the best starting point
