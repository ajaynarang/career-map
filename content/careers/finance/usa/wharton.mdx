## Why Wharton?

Wharton is the undisputed #1 business school on the planet. Founded in 1881 as the world's first collegiate business school, Wharton has produced more Fortune 500 CEOs, hedge fund founders, and private equity leaders than any other institution. Warren Buffett studied here. Elon Musk studied here. Sundar Pichai (Google CEO) holds a Wharton MBA.

**For finance careers specifically, there is no better place.** Wharton's undergraduate programme (BSc in Economics with a Finance concentration) is the single most powerful credential for landing a career on Wall Street. Goldman Sachs, JP Morgan, Blackstone, and KKR recruit more heavily from Wharton than from any other school. The placement rate into investment banking and finance is approximately 40% of each graduating class.

**The financial aid story matters.** UPenn meets 100% of demonstrated financial need for all admitted students — including internationals. A family earning under $75,000/year pays zero. The average financial aid grant exceeds $60,000/year. This means Wharton could cost your family less than a private Indian university.

## How to Get In

Wharton's admissions are among the most competitive in the world:

1. **Apply via Common App** at [admissions.upenn.edu](https://www.admissions.upenn.edu). You apply to Wharton specifically — it's a separate school within UPenn
2. **Early Decision (November 1):** Binding — if admitted, you must attend. ED acceptance rate is roughly double the regular rate. If Wharton is your child's top choice, apply ED
3. **Regular Decision (January 5):** Non-binding. Decisions in late March
4. **Wharton-specific essay** required: Why business? Why Wharton? This must demonstrate genuine understanding of what Wharton offers

**Reality check:** Wharton receives 7,000+ applications for roughly 600 seats in the undergraduate business programme. The acceptance rate is approximately 5.5%. For Indian applicants, it's even more competitive.

**What Wharton looks for:**
- **Quantitative excellence** — near-perfect SAT Math, strong AP/IB Math results, or Math Olympiad achievements
- **Leadership and initiative** — not just club president, but students who built something: a business, a nonprofit, a financial literacy programme
- **Intellectual curiosity about business** — demonstrated through reading, coursework, or real-world projects
- **Diversity of experience** — Wharton actively seeks students from diverse backgrounds and countries

## Curriculum & Structure

4-year BSc in Economics (Wharton's undergraduate degree is technically a BSc in Economics, not a BBA):

- **Wharton Core (Years 1-2):** Microeconomics, Macroeconomics, Statistics, Accounting, Finance, Management, Marketing, Legal Studies. Every Wharton student takes these
- **Concentrations (Years 2-4):** Finance, Statistics & Data Science, Business Analytics, Actuarial Science, Real Estate, Entrepreneurship, and more. Students choose 1-2 concentrations
- **Wharton + Engineering (M&T Program):** A highly selective dual-degree programme for students interested in fintech, quant finance, or tech entrepreneurship. Your child earns both a Wharton BSc and an Engineering BSE in 4 years
- **Cross-registration at Penn** — take courses in Computer Science, Math, or any other school at UPenn
- **Wharton Research Scholars** — a selective programme for students doing original research in business/economics
- **Study abroad** — Wharton Global Modular Courses take students to financial centres worldwide (London, Hong Kong, Singapore)

## Campus Life

UPenn's campus is in University City, Philadelphia — a vibrant, walkable neighbourhood:

- **Wharton is intense.** Students are driven, ambitious, and career-focused from day one. Finance club recruitment, case competitions, and networking events are part of the culture
- **Wharton Investment & Trading Group (WITG)** — student-run fund managing real money. Excellent training ground for future traders
- **Penn Masala** (the famous a cappella group) and 500+ student organisations provide balance
- **Philadelphia is 90 minutes from New York** by train — many students commute to NYC for networking events and interviews
- **Indian community is strong** — Penn has a large Indian student body, and Philadelphia has a growing Indian diaspora
- **Huntsman Program** — for students interested in international business, combining Wharton with Penn's School of Arts & Sciences for a dual degree in business + international studies
