## Why Canada for Design?

Canada is an increasingly attractive destination for Indian design students, offering quality education at lower costs than the US or UK, with the clearest immigration pathway of any English-speaking country. OCAD University (Toronto) and Emily Carr University (Vancouver) are Canada's leading design schools, with growing reputations globally.

**The immigration advantage is massive:** After a 4-year design degree, your child gets a 3-year open work permit (PGWP). After 1 year of skilled work, they can apply for Permanent Residency through Express Entry. No other English-speaking country makes it this straightforward.

**The financial picture:** A 4-year design degree in Canada costs ₹1-1.5 Crore total — significantly less than the US (₹2.2-2.8 Crore) or UK (₹1.4-2.3 Crore). The ability to work 20 hours/week during studies helps offset costs.

## The Path

1. **Class 11-12:** Build a portfolio of 15-20 pieces. Prepare for IELTS (aim for 6.5+).
2. **October-February of 12th:** Apply directly to universities — each has its own application portal. OCAD deadline: typically February 1. Emily Carr: January 15.
3. **February-May:** Conditional offers based on portfolio review and predicted/actual grades.
4. **After 12th results (June-July):** Meet conditions, accept offer, apply for Study Permit.
5. **September:** Start university.

## What Parents Should Know

- **Portfolio is the primary admission criterion.** Canadian design schools evaluate mainly on portfolio quality. 15-20 pieces showing creative range, process, and personal expression. No standardized entrance exam.
- **No SAT/ACT required.** Admission is based on 12th board results (typically 70%+) and portfolio. This simplifies the application compared to the US.
- **OCAD University is Canada's largest and oldest art and design university.** Located in Toronto, OCAD offers programs in industrial design, graphic design, illustration, environmental design, and digital futures. It has a growing reputation for design + technology.
- **Emily Carr University in Vancouver** is the other leading option — particularly strong in communication design, industrial design, and interaction design.
- **Co-op and internship programs** are available at many Canadian design schools. Your child can gain paid work experience during their degree, offsetting costs and building their resume.
- **Canadian winters are cold.** Toronto and other eastern cities experience harsh winters (-20°C). Vancouver is milder but very rainy. Budget for proper winter clothing.
- **The tech industry in Toronto and Vancouver** is booming. UX/product designers are in high demand. Starting salaries: CAD 55,000-75,000 (₹33-45 LPA).
