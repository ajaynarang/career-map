## Why India for Design?

India's design ecosystem is booming. UX designers, product designers, and industrial designers are in massive demand across tech companies, startups, and manufacturing firms. NID and IIT IDC graduates are placed at Google, Microsoft, Samsung, Flipkart, and top design consultancies — often at ₹10-25 LPA starting salaries.

**The value proposition:** An NID degree costs under ₹4L/year. An IIT IDC B.Des costs about ₹2.5L/year. Compare that to Parsons at $55,000/year. The ROI from India's top design schools is extraordinary.

## The Path

1. **Class 11-12:** Prepare for UCEED and/or NID DAT alongside board exams. Build a daily sketching habit.
2. **UCEED exam:** Usually held in January. Tests visual perception, observation, drawing, analytical reasoning, and design thinking. Apply at [uceed.iitb.ac.in](https://uceed.iitb.ac.in).
3. **NID DAT Prelims:** March-April. Tests design aptitude through MCQ and sketch-based questions. Apply at [admissions.nid.edu](https://admissions.nid.edu).
4. **NID DAT Mains:** Studio test + interview at NID campus (May). Shortlisted candidates only.
5. **Counselling and allotment:** June-July for both UCEED and NID.

## What Parents Should Know

- **UCEED and NID DAT are unlike any academic exam.** They test creativity, visual perception, spatial reasoning, and observation skills. Standard coaching approaches don't work — your child needs genuine creative practice.
- **Coaching helps but isn't mandatory.** Silica Institute and Design Guru offer specialized UCEED/NID coaching. Self-study is possible with dedicated sketching practice and past paper analysis.
- **PCM students have a hidden advantage.** Most design applicants come from arts/humanities backgrounds. A PCM student who can also draw and think creatively stands out dramatically in interviews.
- **Design salaries vary enormously by college.** NID/IIT IDC graduates: ₹10-25 LPA. Tier-2 design school graduates: ₹3-6 LPA. The institution matters more in design than almost any other field.
- **UX/UI is the most employable specialization.** Every tech company, bank, and e-commerce firm needs UX designers. Starting salaries: ₹8-15 LPA in India, growing to ₹25-40 LPA with 5-7 years of experience.
- **Backup options exist.** NIFT entrance (fashion/textile design), CEPT University entrance, MIT Institute of Design Pune — all run separate entrance exams between January and May.
