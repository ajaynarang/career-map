## Why IIT Bombay IDC?

IDC School of Design at IIT Bombay is the IIM of design education. It combines the analytical rigor of an IIT with the creative depth of a design school — producing graduates who are equally comfortable with user research data and a sketchbook. IDC alumni lead design at Google, Microsoft, Samsung, and have founded some of India's most successful design-led startups.

**The placement numbers are remarkable:** B.Des graduates from IDC receive offers of ₹15-40 LPA, with top students placed at international companies. And the degree costs just ₹12L total over 4 years. No design school in the world offers this ROI.

**For PCM students specifically:** IDC was built for students who think analytically. The UCEED exam tests spatial reasoning, visual perception, and design thinking — skills that PCM students excel at. You don't need to be a traditional "artist" to crack UCEED.

## How to Get In

Entry is through **UCEED (Undergraduate Common Entrance Exam for Design)**.

1. **UCEED Registration (October-November):** Apply at [uceed.iitb.ac.in](https://uceed.iitb.ac.in). Fee: approximately ₹3,000-4,000.
2. **UCEED Exam (January):** 3-hour computer-based test. Tests visual perception, observation skills, analytical reasoning, and drawing ability. Includes NAT (Numerical Answer Type), MSQ (Multiple Select), and drawing sections.
3. **UCEED Scorecard (March):** Score is valid for admission to B.Des at IIT Bombay, IIT Delhi, IIT Guwahati, IIT Hyderabad, and IIITDM Jabalpur.
4. **Counselling (June-July):** Based on UCEED score (50%) + Class 12 board marks (50%).

**How competitive is it?** UCEED receives approximately 15,000 applications. IIT Bombay IDC offers roughly 40 B.Des seats. You need a UCEED rank under 300 for a realistic chance. It's intensely competitive — but remember, 15,000 is far fewer than the 10 lakh JEE aspirants.

## Curriculum & Structure

4-year B.Des with a project-based curriculum:

- **Year 1:** Foundation — drawing, materials, design thinking, basic coding, human factors
- **Year 2:** Core design courses — form studies, visual communication, interaction design
- **Year 3:** Specialization + industry internship (mandatory, 2-3 months). Many students intern at Google, Microsoft, Samsung, or design consultancies
- **Year 4:** Graduation project — a substantial design project that often becomes a portfolio centerpiece

**The IIT advantage:** B.Des students have full access to IIT Bombay's courses, clubs, and facilities. Many take electives in CS, mechanical engineering, or management — making them uniquely versatile designers.

## Campus Life

Being part of IIT Bombay's 550-acre Powai campus gives IDC students access to one of Asia's most vibrant college experiences.

- **IDC's own studios** — dedicated design studios, model-making workshops, and prototyping labs
- **Cross-pollination** — IDC students collaborate with CS, mechanical, and management students on projects
- **Techfest and Mood Indigo** — IIT Bombay's flagship festivals. IDC students often lead the design and creative direction
- **Mumbai** — India's commercial capital is a design playground. Students visit studios, attend industry events, and build networks
