## Why IIT Guwahati Design?

IIT Guwahati's Department of Design is one of India's best-kept secrets. Established with a strong focus on user-centered design and technology, it offers a rigorous B.Des program that combines design thinking with technical depth. The department is smaller than IIT Bombay's IDC — which means more personal attention, closer faculty mentorship, and a tight-knit community.

**For budget-conscious families:** The total cost of a B.Des at IIT Guwahati is approximately ₹11L over 4 years, with scholarships bringing it even lower. Placement outcomes — ₹10-25 LPA — make the return on investment outstanding.

## How to Get In

Entry is through **UCEED**, the same exam used for IIT Bombay IDC.

1. **UCEED exam in January** — register at [uceed.iitb.ac.in](https://uceed.iitb.ac.in)
2. **Counselling based on UCEED score + board marks** — IIT Guwahati typically fills seats at ranks 200-500 in UCEED. This means students who narrowly miss IIT Bombay IDC often get IIT Guwahati.
3. **Seat acceptance** during JoSAA-like counselling in June-July.

**Strategic insight:** UCEED gives you access to B.Des at IIT Bombay, IIT Delhi, IIT Guwahati, and IIT Hyderabad — all through one exam. IIT Guwahati is an excellent choice for students ranked in the 200-500 range.

## Curriculum & Structure

4-year B.Des with focus areas in:

- **User Experience Design** — digital products, interaction design, service design
- **Product Design** — physical products, consumer goods, industrial design
- **Communication Design** — visual communication, branding, information design

The curriculum integrates technology modules (basic coding, prototyping tools, data visualization) that make graduates more employable in the tech-driven design market.

**Internships:** Mandatory in the 3rd year. Students intern at Samsung, Microsoft, Flipkart, and design studios across India.

## Campus Life

IIT Guwahati's 700-acre campus on the banks of the Brahmaputra is one of the most beautiful in India.

- **Natural setting** — the campus is surrounded by hills and the river. The environment is peaceful and conducive to creative work
- **Strong technical culture** — design students collaborate with engineering and science students on interdisciplinary projects
- **Lower cost of living** — Guwahati is significantly cheaper than Mumbai or Delhi, reducing the financial burden
- **Northeast connection** — proximity to diverse cultures and craft traditions of the Northeast inspires unique design perspectives
