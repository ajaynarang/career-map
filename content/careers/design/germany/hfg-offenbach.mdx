## Why HfG Offenbach?

HfG Offenbach (Hochschule für Gestaltung) is one of Germany's most prestigious design schools — a spiritual successor to the Bauhaus tradition. Its Product Design (Produktgestaltung) program is legendary, producing graduates who design for BMW, Braun, Porsche, and some of the world's most innovative design studios. The school is small, intensive, and deeply craft-oriented.

**The cost argument is staggering:** Total tuition over the entire degree is approximately €1,500 (semester fees). Your child's only real expense is living costs — approximately ₹10-12L/year in the Frankfurt region. A 4-year degree that rivals RISD or Parsons in quality, for under ₹50L total including living. No design school on Earth offers better value.

**For Indian PCM students:** HfG values analytical thinking alongside creativity. The entrance exam tests problem-solving, spatial reasoning, and design thinking — areas where PCM students have natural advantages. But you must learn German — there is no shortcut.

## How to Get In

HfG Offenbach has a multi-stage admission process:

1. **Mappe (portfolio) submission:** Submit 15-25 original works — drawings, models, prototypes, photographs, experiments. The Mappe should show your creative process, curiosity, and range. HfG values conceptual thinking over technical polish.
2. **Pre-selection:** Based on the Mappe, HfG shortlists candidates for the Aufnahmeprüfung.
3. **Aufnahmeprüfung (entrance exam):** A 2-3 day intensive at HfG campus. Includes:
   - Practical design tasks (drawing, model-making, design problem-solving)
   - Portfolio presentation and discussion
   - Interview with faculty (in German)
4. **Admission decision:** Based on combined Mappe + Aufnahmeprüfung performance.

**Important for Indian students:**
- Your Class 12 boards may require validation through [uni-assist](https://www.uni-assist.de). Some students need a 1-year Studienkolleg before starting the degree.
- German B2 is mandatory at the time of enrollment. Plan your German language timeline carefully.
- Budget for a trip to Offenbach for the Aufnahmeprüfung — this cannot be done online.

## Curriculum & Structure

HfG offers a Diplom degree (equivalent to a Master's — German design schools traditionally award the Diplom rather than separate Bachelor's/Master's). The program runs 8-10 semesters (4-5 years).

**Product Design (Produktgestaltung):**
- **Foundation (semesters 1-2):** Drawing, model-making, materials, basic design principles
- **Core (semesters 3-6):** Product design projects, industrial design methods, user research, prototyping, manufacturing processes
- **Specialization (semesters 7-10):** Advanced projects, thesis project, often in collaboration with industry partners (BMW, Braun, Lufthansa)

**The studio model:** Students work in dedicated studio spaces. Each semester involves intensive design projects with critiques from faculty and visiting industry professionals. The learning is hands-on — expect to build physical models, prototype with 3D printers, and work in the metal and wood workshops.

## Campus & Location

HfG's campus is in Offenbach, directly adjacent to Frankfurt — one of Germany's most international cities.

- **Frankfurt proximity** — Germany's financial and design capital is a 15-minute train ride away. Students attend design exhibitions, industry events, and cultural institutions regularly
- **Well-equipped workshops** — metal workshop, wood workshop, ceramics studio, textile lab, 3D printing lab, photography studios, digital fabrication tools
- **Small school, strong community** — HfG has approximately 600 students total. This means close relationships with faculty and peers
- **International student support** — HfG has a growing international student body and offers orientation programs for newcomers
- **Affordable living** — Frankfurt area is not cheap by German standards, but far more affordable than London or NYC. Budget €700-900/month for a shared apartment (WG)
