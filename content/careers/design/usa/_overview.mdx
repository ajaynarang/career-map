## Why the USA for Design?

The United States is where modern design education was invented. Schools like RISD, Parsons, and ArtCenter have trained the designers behind Apple products, Nike shoes, Tesla interiors, and Instagram's interface. The US design job market is the largest in the world — UX designers, product designers, and industrial designers are in constant demand at tech giants, startups, and design consultancies.

**The career outcomes are compelling:** Graduates from top US design schools earn $75,000-$120,000 starting. Senior UX designers at Google, Apple, and Meta earn $150,000-$250,000+. And STEM-designated design programs (many B.Des/BFA programs now qualify) give your child 3 years of post-study work rights through OPT.

**The investment is real:** A 4-year design degree in the US costs ₹2.2-2.8 Crore. But top schools offer significant merit scholarships — RISD and Parsons regularly award $15,000-$30,000/year in merit aid to strong international applicants.

## The Path

1. **Class 11-12:** Build a strong portfolio of 15-20 pieces. Take SAT (aim for 1350+, though portfolio matters more). Prepare TOEFL/IELTS.
2. **August-September of 12th:** Common Application and individual school applications open. Start essays.
3. **November-January:** Portfolio submission deadlines. Most schools accept SlideRoom or school-specific portfolio platforms.
4. **January 1-15:** Regular Decision deadlines for RISD, Parsons, Pratt, SVA, CMU.
5. **March-April:** Decisions and financial aid packages.
6. **September:** Start university.

## What Parents Should Know

- **Portfolio is 60-70% of the decision.** Unlike Indian exams or US engineering admissions, design school admissions are portfolio-driven. Your child's body of creative work — sketches, projects, digital designs, photography — matters more than SAT scores.
- **Show process, not just outcomes.** US design schools want to see how your child thinks. Include rough sketches, iterations, failed experiments, and the story behind each piece.
- **SAT scores matter less here.** A 1300+ SAT is usually sufficient for top design schools. Parsons doesn't even require SAT for some programs.
- **STEM OPT is a game-changer for design.** Many US design programs are now classified as STEM, giving graduates 3 years of work rights (vs. 1 year for non-STEM). Verify STEM designation before applying — RISD's Industrial Design and Parsons' Design & Technology are STEM-designated.
- **Merit scholarships are substantial.** RISD, Parsons, and Pratt offer merit scholarships of $15,000-$30,000/year based on portfolio strength. Apply to multiple schools and compare offers.
- **New York City and Providence** are the two main design hubs. Parsons and SVA are in NYC — giving students access to the design industry during school. RISD is in Providence, RI — a smaller city but with deep creative community ties.
