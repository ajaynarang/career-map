## Why Parsons?

Parsons School of Design, part of The New School in New York City, is one of the world's most influential design schools. Its location in the heart of Manhattan — surrounded by design studios, tech companies, fashion houses, and advertising agencies — gives students unparalleled access to the industry while still studying. Tom Ford, Marc Jacobs, Donna Karan, and countless tech designers are Parsons alumni.

**For PCM students interested in design + technology:** Parsons' **Design & Technology** program is STEM-designated, meaning graduates get 3 years of US work rights through OPT. This program combines interaction design, coding, data visualization, and creative technology — ideal for analytical minds who want to design digital products.

**The NYC advantage:** No other design school offers what Parsons does — you study design while living in the design capital of the world. Students intern at Google NYC, Pentagram, R/GA, and Nike during the school year. The networking opportunities are unmatched.

## How to Get In

Parsons admissions center on the portfolio and the **Parsons Challenge**.

1. **Portfolio (8-12 pieces):** Show creative work across any medium — drawing, digital, photography, sculpture, coding projects, product prototypes. Parsons values diversity of exploration.
2. **Parsons Challenge:** A unique component. Parsons gives you a design prompt (changes each year), and you must create an original visual response plus write a 500-word essay about your process. This tests design thinking, not just execution.
3. **Personal essay** through Common Application or New School application.
4. **SAT/ACT:** Optional — not required and not considered unless submitted.
5. **TOEFL/IELTS:** Required for international students. Minimum TOEFL: 92. Minimum IELTS: 7.0.

**Application timeline:**
- Early Action: November 1 (non-binding)
- Regular Decision: January 15
- Decisions: mid-December (EA), late March (RD)

**Tip for Indian applicants:** The Parsons Challenge is where Indian students often shine. Approach it like a design problem — show research, iteration, and thoughtful reasoning alongside creative execution. This is where your PCM analytical skills become an advantage.

## Curriculum & Structure

4-year BFA or BBA (depending on program). All first-year students take a common core:

- **Year 1:** Integrative Studio — a cross-disciplinary foundation that exposes students to multiple design fields. Drawing, making, digital tools, and critical thinking.
- **Years 2-4:** Major coursework + electives across The New School (students can take courses in economics, philosophy, media studies, and social sciences at other New School colleges).
- **Internship:** Most students complete 1-2 internships during their time at Parsons. NYC location makes this seamless.

**Key programs for PCM students:**
- **Design & Technology (BFA):** Coding + design. Interactive installations, app design, AI-powered design tools. STEM-designated.
- **Product Design (BFA):** Physical product design — furniture, consumer goods, medical devices. Strong studio culture.
- **Communication Design (BFA):** Visual identity, branding, UI design, typography.

## Campus Life

Parsons is in Greenwich Village, Manhattan — one of NYC's most vibrant neighborhoods.

- **No traditional campus** — Parsons is an urban school. Studios, classrooms, and galleries are spread across several buildings in the Village. Your child will navigate NYC daily — this builds independence fast.
- **NYC is the classroom** — museums (MoMA, Met, Cooper Hewitt Design Museum), galleries, design firms, and tech companies are all within subway distance.
- **Expensive city, but opportunities abound** — NYC is costly, but part-time design work, freelancing, and internships help offset costs. Many students earn $15-$25/hour in design-related part-time work.
- **Diverse student body** — Parsons has one of the most internationally diverse student populations of any US design school. Indian students find community through The New School's South Asian student organizations.
