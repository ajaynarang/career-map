## Why RISD?

RISD (pronounced "RIZ-dee") is America's most prestigious design school and consistently ranked among the top 3 globally. Founded in 1877, RISD has produced some of the world's most influential designers — from the creators of Airbnb to lead designers at Apple and Nike. The school's philosophy centers on making: students work with physical materials, develop hand skills, and learn to see the world differently.

**For Indian families considering design:** RISD is to design what MIT is to engineering. A RISD degree carries immense weight globally. Industrial Design graduates are recruited by Apple, Tesla, and Samsung. Graphic Design graduates lead branding at top agencies. The RISD network opens doors that few other schools can match.

**The investment consideration:** At $79,000/year, RISD is expensive. But the school offers meaningful merit scholarships ($10,000-$30,000/year), and Industrial Design is STEM-designated — giving graduates 3 years of US work rights through OPT. Starting salaries of $65,000-$90,000 grow quickly to $100,000-$150,000+ within 5 years.

## How to Get In

RISD's admissions are portfolio-driven.

1. **Portfolio (12-20 pieces):** This is what matters most. Show a range of work — drawing, painting, digital design, photography, sculpture, whatever your child creates. Quality and creative thinking matter more than polish.
2. **Drawing Supplement:** 2-3 drawings responding to specific prompts RISD provides each year. This is unique to RISD and tests observational drawing ability.
3. **Common Application essay + RISD-specific writing.** A 650-word personal statement plus RISD's own questions.
4. **SAT/ACT:** Currently optional for most programs.
5. **TOEFL/IELTS:** Required for international students. Minimum TOEFL: 93. Minimum IELTS: 6.5.

**Application timeline:**
- Early Decision: November 1 (binding — only apply ED if RISD is your top choice)
- Regular Decision: January 2
- Decisions: December (ED), late March (RD)

**Portfolio tip for Indian students:** RISD values observational drawing — drawing what you see, not what you imagine. Include studies of objects, people, and spaces drawn from life. Also include 2-3 pieces that show design thinking or problem-solving.

## Curriculum & Structure

4-year BFA (Bachelor of Fine Arts). All students take a common Foundation Year:

- **Year 1:** Foundation Studies — drawing, design, and spatial dynamics. Every student, regardless of major, goes through this rigorous introduction to visual thinking.
- **Years 2-4:** Major coursework + liberal arts courses (30% of credits are in liberal arts — history, philosophy, writing). RISD believes designers must be broadly educated.
- **Senior year:** Thesis project — a substantial body of work exhibited at the year-end RISD Grad Show.

**Cross-registration with Brown University:** RISD students can take courses at Brown (Ivy League), and vice versa. This is a massive advantage — your child can study computer science, business, or engineering at Brown alongside their design education at RISD.

## Campus Life

RISD's campus is in downtown Providence, RI — a walkable, affordable New England city with a strong creative community.

- **RISD Museum** — one of the finest art museums in the US, right on campus. Students have unlimited access and use it as a learning resource.
- **Studios are open 24/7** — RISD students spend long hours making, building, and creating. The culture is intense and deeply supportive.
- **Providence is affordable** — unlike NYC, Providence has reasonable rents and a manageable cost of living.
- **Indian student community** — combined RISD and Brown University Indian student groups provide community and cultural events.
- **Career Fair and recruiting** — top companies (Apple, Google, IDEO, Nike) recruit on campus annually.
