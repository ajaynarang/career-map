## Why RCA?

The Royal College of Art is the world's #1 art and design institution — and has held that position in QS rankings for nine consecutive years. It is the only entirely postgraduate art and design university in the world. RCA graduates have designed the Dyson vacuum, the London Underground map redesign, and products for Apple, Tesla, and Samsung.

**Important note for families:** RCA is a **postgraduate-only** institution. Your child cannot join RCA directly after Class 12. The path is: complete a BA/B.Des at a strong undergraduate school (NID, IIT IDC, CSM, or another top program) → then apply to RCA for a 1-2 year Master's. This section is included because many families should plan their undergraduate choices with an RCA Master's as the long-term goal.

**Why plan for RCA now?** Because the undergraduate choices your child makes today determine whether they are competitive for RCA in 4-5 years. A strong BA from NID, IIT IDC, CSM, or RISD positions them well. The RCA degree then supercharges their career — graduates command premium salaries globally.

## How to Get In

RCA admissions are entirely portfolio and interview-based.

1. **Portfolio:** Submit a curated body of work demonstrating creative excellence, critical thinking, and technical skill. Quality and conceptual depth matter more than quantity.
2. **Written statement:** 500-word statement explaining why you want to study at RCA and what you plan to investigate.
3. **Two references:** Academic or professional.
4. **Interview:** Shortlisted candidates are interviewed by faculty. This can be done online for international applicants.

**Application timeline:**
- Round 1: October-November (early — better chance of scholarships)
- Round 2: January-February (main round)
- Decisions: Within 6-8 weeks of each round

**What RCA looks for:** Intellectual curiosity, conceptual thinking, technical skill, and a clear creative vision. They want students who will push boundaries, not just execute polished work.

## Key Programs for Design Students

**Innovation Design Engineering (IDE) — joint with Imperial College London:**
This is RCA's most famous program for students with a science/engineering background. It's a 2-year MA/MSc or MRes jointly awarded by RCA (#1 art/design) and Imperial (#6 engineering globally). Graduates earn two Master's degrees. IDE alumni have launched over 100 startups and work at Dyson, Tesla, and Google.

**Industrial Design (formerly Design Products):**
One of the world's top programs for physical product design. Students work with real materials, advanced prototyping, and industry partners. Graduates design for furniture, consumer electronics, automotive, and medical devices.

**Service Design:**
A growing field — designing experiences, systems, and services. Graduates work in consulting, healthcare, government, and tech.

## The RCA Experience

- **London as a design lab** — RCA's campuses are in Battersea and White City, surrounded by London's design industry. Museums (V&A, Design Museum), galleries, studios, and tech companies are accessible daily.
- **Collaboration is the culture** — RCA brings together designers, engineers, scientists, and artists. Cross-program collaboration is encouraged and common.
- **Industry connections** — leading companies (Dyson, Samsung, Google) run live projects with RCA students. Many students secure jobs through these collaborations.
- **2-year programs** — most Master's programs are 2 years (vs. 1 year at many UK universities), giving more time for deep creative development.
