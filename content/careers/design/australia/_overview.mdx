## Why Australia for Design?

Australia's design education is deeply practical and industry-connected. Schools like RMIT Melbourne, UTS Sydney, and Monash University have strong programs that prepare students for real-world design careers. Australia's creative economy is growing — and designers are in demand across tech, architecture, fashion, and sustainability sectors.

**The lifestyle factor is real:** Australia offers warm weather, safe cities, and a relaxed but professional culture. Melbourne is consistently ranked among the world's most livable cities and has a thriving creative scene. For Indian students who find UK/Canadian winters daunting, Australia's climate is a significant draw.

**The immigration pathway:** After a 4-year design degree, graduates get a 2-year post-study work visa (longer if studying in regional areas). Design-related occupations are growing in Australia's skilled migration lists, though the pathway is less direct than Canada's.

## The Path

1. **Class 11-12:** Build a portfolio of 15-20 pieces. Prepare for IELTS (aim for 6.5+).
2. **Apply directly** to universities through their online portals. Most accept applications year-round for the **February or July intake**. Deadlines: October-November for February intake.
3. **Receive offers** — conditional on 12th board results and portfolio review. Accept and pay deposit.
4. **Apply for Student Visa (subclass 500)** — requires CoE, IELTS, financial proof, and OSHC.
5. **February or July:** Start university.

## What Parents Should Know

- **February is the main intake** (unlike September in the US/UK/Canada). July intake is also available at most universities.
- **Portfolio is important but not always required.** Some Australian design programs admit based on academic results alone. RMIT and UTS typically require a portfolio for competitive programs.
- **RMIT Melbourne is the standout.** Globally ranked in the top 15 for Art & Design (QS 2026), RMIT is Australia's leading design school. Its industrial design, graphic design, and communication design programs are well-regarded internationally.
- **48 hours per fortnight work rights** provide meaningful income. At Australian minimum wage (~AUD 24/hour), your child can earn AUD 400-500/week during semester.
- **OSHC (Overseas Student Health Cover)** is mandatory. Costs AUD 500-700/year.
- **Melbourne's creative scene** is exceptional — street art, design studios, independent fashion, and a thriving food culture. The city itself is an education in design.
- **Living costs are manageable.** Melbourne and Sydney are expensive by global standards but cheaper than London or New York. Shared apartments: AUD 200-300/week.
