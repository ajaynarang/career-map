## Class 9-10: Building the Foundation

Design is one of the most misunderstood career paths among PCM families. It's not "just art" — it's problem-solving with a creative lens. And PCM students have a significant advantage in product design, UX/UI, and industrial design because they think analytically.

### For all paths:
- **Start sketching regularly** — even 15 minutes a day builds visual communication skills.
- **Learn basic design tools:** Figma (free — [figma.com](https://www.figma.com)), Canva, and Adobe XD. These are industry-standard.
- **Observe everyday products** — why is this chair shaped this way? Why are some apps easier to use? Design thinking starts with observation.
- **Maintain 80%+ in school** — NID, IIT IDC, and international schools all care about academics.
- **Build a habit of documenting ideas** — keep a sketchbook or digital journal of design observations.
- **Learn basic coding** — HTML/CSS/JavaScript basics make you a stronger UX designer. [freeCodeCamp](https://freecodecamp.org) is free.

### If targeting India (NID/IIT IDC):
- Understand the two main exams: **UCEED** (for IIT B.Des programs) and **NID DAT** (for National Institute of Design).
- Start solving visual aptitude puzzles — UCEED tests spatial reasoning, observation, and visual perception.

### If targeting USA:
- Build a **portfolio** of 15-20 pieces — sketches, digital designs, photography, product concepts.
- Research: Parsons, RISD, CMU, ArtCenter, Stanford d.school (graduate level).

### If targeting Germany:
- **START GERMAN NOW.** Most design programs require B2 German. Reach A2 by end of Class 10 at [Goethe-Institut](https://www.goethe.de/ins/in/en/index.html).
- German design schools (HfG Offenbach, Bauhaus University Weimar, UdK Berlin) are world-class and nearly free.

## Class 11: The Critical Year

### For India (UCEED/NID DAT):
- **UCEED preparation:** The exam tests visual perception, observation, drawing, analytical reasoning, and design thinking. It's unlike any academic exam — you need a creative mindset.
- **Join UCEED coaching** — Silica, Design Guru, or other specialized coaching. Self-study is possible but coaching significantly helps for the studio test.
- **NID DAT Prelims** tests Design Aptitude (MCQ + sketch-based). **DAT Mains** includes a studio test and interview.
- Practice: Draw from observation daily. Study color theory, typography basics, and form language.
- **Research B.Des programs:** IIT Bombay (IDC), IIT Delhi, IIT Guwahati, IIT Hyderabad (through UCEED). NID Ahmedabad, NID Bengaluru, NID Kurukshetra (through NID DAT).

### For USA:
- **Take the SAT** — aim for 1350+. Design schools weigh the portfolio more, but SAT is still required.
- **Build your portfolio** — focus on process, not just finished work. Show sketches, iterations, and thinking behind each piece.
- Take **AP Studio Art** if available.
- Some top design programs: Parsons (NYC), RISD (Providence), Carnegie Mellon (Pittsburgh), ArtCenter (LA), Pratt Institute (NYC).

### For Germany:
- **Reach German B1.** Take Goethe B1 exam.
- Start preparing your **Mappe (portfolio)** — German design schools require 15-25 original works showing your creative process.

### For UK:
- Research **Foundation courses** — many UK design schools require a 1-year Foundation Diploma before the degree (total: 4 years).
- Top schools: **Royal College of Art** (postgrad), **Central Saint Martins (UAL)**, **Goldsmiths**, **Edinburgh College of Art**.
- UCAS deadline: January 31.

### For Canada & Australia:
- Focus on **grades** and **IELTS** (target 6.5+).
- Portfolio is essential for most design programs.
- Canada: OCAD University, Emily Carr. Australia: RMIT, University of Technology Sydney (UTS).

## Class 12: Execution

### Month-by-month (India path):
- **July-September:** Intensive UCEED/NID DAT preparation. Practice 50+ past papers. Sketch daily for 1-2 hours.
- **October-November:** UCEED registration at [uceed.iitb.ac.in](https://uceed.iitb.ac.in). NID DAT registration at [admissions.nid.edu](https://admissions.nid.edu).
- **January:** UCEED exam (usually third week of January). Continue NID DAT prep.
- **February-March:** Board exams. Score well — UCEED uses a combined score (exam + board marks).
- **March-April:** NID DAT Prelims. UCEED results.
- **April-May:** NID DAT Mains (studio test + interview at NID campus). Shortlisting based on UCEED for IIT B.Des.
- **June-July:** Final allotment. Counselling.

### Backup exams:
- **NIFT entrance** (January) — [nift.ac.in](https://www.nift.ac.in). For fashion/textile design.
- **CEPT entrance** (May) — for design programs at CEPT University, Ahmedabad.
- **MIT Institute of Design, Pune** — separate entrance.

### For USA applicants:
- **August:** Common Application and individual school applications open.
- **November-January:** Portfolio submission deadlines. Most schools accept SlideRoom portfolios.
- **January 1-15:** Regular Decision deadlines for Parsons, RISD, Pratt.
- **March-April:** Decisions and financial aid packages. RISD and Parsons offer significant merit scholarships.

### For Germany:
- **Complete German B2** by March.
- **Submit Mappe** — many schools have specific submission windows. Check each university's website.
- **Apply through [uni-assist](https://www.uni-assist.de)** or directly to the university. Deadline: July 15 for winter semester.
- Some schools require an **Aufnahmeprüfung** (entrance exam/interview) — prepare to present your portfolio.

## Smart Strategy: What the Best Students Do

1. **IIT Bombay IDC is the IIM of design.** Their B.Des graduates get placed at ₹15-30 LPA in product design roles at Google, Microsoft, Samsung, and startups. Entry through UCEED — one of the most competitive design exams in India (acceptance rate ~2%).
2. **NID is the gold standard for industrial and communication design.** NID Ahmedabad graduates are highly respected globally. Fees are very reasonable (under ₹4 lakh/year).
3. **UX/UI Design is the most employable design specialization.** Companies across tech, finance, healthcare, and e-commerce need UX designers. Starting salary: ₹8-15 LPA in India, $85-120K in the USA.
4. **PCM students have a hidden advantage.** Design schools love applicants who can think analytically AND creatively. Most design applicants come from arts/humanities — a PCM student who can also draw stands out dramatically.
5. **Portfolio is everything.** For both Indian and international admissions, your portfolio matters more than test scores. Start building it in Class 9-10. Show process, not just outcomes.
6. **Germany for design is incredible value.** UdK Berlin and HfG Offenbach are world-renowned design schools with zero tuition. Total cost for a 4-year degree: under ₹25 lakh (including living expenses). Compare that to $50,000/year at Parsons.
7. **Honest salary reality in India:** Starting salaries in design vary wildly. NID/IIT IDC graduates: ₹10-25 LPA. Tier-2 design school graduates: ₹3-6 LPA. The college you attend matters enormously in design.
8. **Design thinking is a transferable skill.** Even if your child doesn't practice design long-term, design thinking training is valued in consulting (McKinsey has a design practice), product management, and entrepreneurship.
