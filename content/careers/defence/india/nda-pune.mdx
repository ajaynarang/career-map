## Why NDA?

The National Defence Academy in Khadakwasla, Pune is India's most prestigious military training institution and one of the world's few tri-service academies — training future officers of the Army, Navy, and Air Force under one roof. NDA has produced Field Marshals, Param Vir Chakra recipients, Chiefs of Army/Naval/Air Staff, and thousands of officers who have served India with distinction. An NDA cadet's journey is among the most transformative experiences any young person can have.

What makes NDA exceptional for families: **it costs nothing and pays you.** Cadets receive a monthly stipend of ₹56,100 during training, plus free accommodation, food, uniform, medical care, and even textbooks. The 3-year training includes a full university degree (BA/BSc/B.Tech from Jawaharlal Nehru University). No other career path in India offers a fully funded education, guaranteed employment, and a salary during training — all in one.

## How to Get In

NDA selection is a multi-stage process spanning 8-12 months:

### Stage 1: NDA Written Exam (UPSC)
- **Paper 1 — Mathematics (300 marks, 2.5 hours):** Covers Class 11-12 level — Algebra, Matrices, Trigonometry, Calculus, Statistics, Analytical Geometry. PCM students have a direct advantage here
- **Paper 2 — General Ability Test (600 marks, 2.5 hours):**
  - Part A: English (200 marks) — comprehension, grammar, vocabulary, usage
  - Part B: General Knowledge (400 marks) — Physics, Chemistry, General Science, History, Geography, Current Affairs
- **Exam schedule:** NDA-I (April), NDA-II (September). Register at [upsc.gov.in](https://www.upsc.gov.in) — registration opens 3-4 months before each exam
- **Cutoff:** Typically 340-370 out of 900 (varies by year). About 8,000-10,000 candidates are shortlisted for SSB from 4-5 lakh applicants

### Stage 2: SSB Interview (5 days)
This is where NDA selection truly happens. The SSB (Services Selection Board) is conducted at one of several centers across India:

- **Day 1 — Screening:** Officer Intelligence Rating (OIR) test + Picture Perception and Description Test (PPDT). About 50-60% are screened out on Day 1
- **Day 2 — Psychology Tests:** Thematic Apperception Test (TAT), Word Association Test (WAT), Situation Reaction Test (SRT), Self-Description Test (SDT)
- **Day 3-4 — Group Testing:** Group Discussion, Group Planning Exercise, Progressive Group Task, Half Group Task, Individual Obstacles, Command Task, Lecturette
- **Day 5 — Personal Interview + Conference:** One-on-one interview with the Deputy President. Final conference with the full board

**SSB success rate:** Out of those who reach SSB, approximately 10-15% are recommended. The key qualities assessed: initiative, determination, courage, social adaptability, cooperation, sense of responsibility, and emotional stability.

### Stage 3: Medical Examination
- Rigorous medical screening conducted at Military Hospital
- Common disqualifiers: uncorrected vision problems, color blindness (for Navy/Air Force), flat feet, insufficient height/weight, and chronic conditions
- **Get a preliminary medical check-up done in Class 10-11** to identify and address any issues early

### Stage 4: Merit List
- Final merit list based on: Written exam marks + SSB interview marks
- Approximately 400 cadets are selected per cycle (across all categories)

## Training Structure (3 Years at NDA)

NDA training is divided into 6 semesters over 3 years:

- **Term 1-2 (Ragada period):** The most intense phase. Physical conditioning, drill, weapons training, outdoor exercises, cross-country runs, swimming, horse riding. Simultaneously, academic classes for the degree program begin. This period tests mental toughness and resilience
- **Term 3-4:** Advanced military training — map reading, tactical exercises, field craft. Cadets choose their wing: Army, Navy, or Air Force. Academic curriculum deepens
- **Term 5-6:** Leadership training — cadets take on positions of responsibility (Cadet Appointments). Final academic exams. Passing Out Parade preparation
- **The Passing Out Parade** at NDA is one of India's most iconic ceremonies. Family members attend. The graduating cadets receive their commissions and proceed to their respective service academies for specialized training

### After NDA:
- **Army cadets:** 1 year at Indian Military Academy (IMA), Dehradun
- **Navy cadets:** 1 year at Indian Naval Academy (INA), Ezhimala, Kerala
- **Air Force cadets:** 1.5 years at Air Force Academy (AFA), Dundigal, Hyderabad

## Career Progression & Benefits

### Pay and allowances (7th Pay Commission, 2026):
| Rank | Pay Level | Basic Pay | Approximate CTC |
|------|-----------|-----------|-----------------|
| Lieutenant | Level 10 | ₹56,100/month | ₹10-12 LPA |
| Captain | Level 10B | ₹61,300/month | ₹13-15 LPA |
| Major | Level 11 | ₹69,400/month | ₹16-20 LPA |
| Lt. Colonel | Level 12A | ₹1,21,200/month | ₹22-28 LPA |
| Colonel | Level 13 | ₹1,30,600/month | ₹28-35 LPA |
| Brigadier+ | Level 13A+ | ₹1,39,600+/month | ₹35+ LPA |

### Benefits (beyond salary):
- **Free furnished housing** (or House Rent Allowance if government housing is unavailable)
- **Free healthcare** for officer and family (at military hospitals) — for life, including after retirement
- **Subsidized CSD (Canteen Stores Department)** — significant savings on groceries, electronics, and vehicles
- **Children's education allowance** and access to Army/Navy/Air Force schools
- **30 days annual leave + 60 days casual leave**
- **Pension after 20 years of service** — approximately 50% of last drawn pay, with DA increases
- **Ex-servicemen benefits** — reservation in government jobs, housing schemes, and various welfare programs
