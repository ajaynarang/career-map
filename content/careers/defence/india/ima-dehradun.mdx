## Why IMA Dehradun?

The Indian Military Academy (IMA) in Dehradun is where India's Army officers are forged. Established in 1932, IMA has trained over 60,000 officers who have served in every conflict and operation the Indian Army has undertaken. The famous motto "Valour and Wisdom" captures IMA's philosophy: officers must be both warriors and thinkers.

IMA is relevant to two types of students: **(1) NDA graduates** who come here for their final year of Army-specific training after 3 years at NDA Khadakwasla, and **(2) graduate entry officers** who join through CDS (Combined Defence Services exam), Direct Entry, NCC Special Entry, or TES (Technical Entry Scheme). For students who missed NDA or chose to get a civilian degree first, IMA through CDS is the most common alternative route.

## Entry Routes to IMA

### Route 1: After NDA (3 years at NDA + 1 year at IMA)
- NDA graduates join IMA automatically after completing their 3-year training at Khadakwasla
- This is the most prestigious entry — NDA + IMA officers are called "Permanent Commission (PC)" officers
- Training at IMA: 1 year of intensive Army-specific combat and leadership training

### Route 2: CDS (Combined Defence Services) — for graduates
The most common entry for those who didn't join through NDA:

1. **Eligibility:** Bachelor's degree from any recognized university (for Indian Military Academy — Army). Age: 20-24 years at the time of joining
2. **CDS Written Exam (UPSC):** Conducted twice a year (February and September)
   - Paper I: English (100 marks)
   - Paper II: General Knowledge (100 marks)
   - Paper III: Elementary Mathematics (100 marks)
   - Cutoff typically: 120-140 out of 300
3. **SSB Interview:** 5-day assessment (same as NDA SSB). Tests Officer-Like Qualities
4. **Medical Examination:** Rigorous physical and medical screening
5. **Training at IMA:** 1 year (approximately 49 weeks)

### Route 3: TES (10+2 Technical Entry Scheme) — for PCM students
- **Eligibility:** Class 12 with PCM, scoring 70%+ aggregate
- **No written exam** — selection based on Class 12 marks + SSB interview
- **Training:** 1 year at Officers Training Academy (OTA) Gaya, followed by 4 years of engineering training at CME Pune / MCTE Mhow / MCEME Secunderabad
- **Outcome:** B.Tech degree + Army commission — your child becomes both an engineer and an officer
- **Apply at [joinindianarmy.nic.in](https://joinindianarmy.nic.in)** — notification usually in May-June for January joining

### Route 4: NCC Special Entry
- **Eligibility:** NCC 'C' certificate holders with a Bachelor's degree
- **No written exam** — direct SSB interview
- **Training at IMA:** 49 weeks
- This is why NCC in school is so valuable — it provides a direct path to IMA without any written exam

## Training at IMA (What Your Child Will Experience)

IMA training is among the most demanding and transformative experiences in India:

- **Physical training:** Daily PT at 5:30 AM — running, obstacle courses, swimming, horse riding, cross-country marches with full equipment (25-30 kg). The Chetwode Building drill ground is iconic
- **Weapon training:** Firing range practice with rifles, pistols, and light machine guns. Field exercises in the Doon Valley and surrounding hills
- **Tactical training:** Map reading, section and platoon-level tactical exercises, ambush drills, patrolling techniques
- **Leadership training:** Gentlemen Cadets (GCs) progressively take on leadership roles — drill commanders, platoon leaders, company appointments
- **Academic and professional studies:** Military law, administration, strategy, logistics, and ethics
- **Adventure activities:** Rock climbing, river crossing, mountaineering, skydiving (for selected cadets)
- **The Passing Out Parade (POP):** One of India's grandest military ceremonies. The Commandant administers the oath of allegiance. The "Slow March" through the Chetwode Building doors is iconic — the inscription reads: *"The safety, honour and welfare of your country come first, always and every time. The honour, welfare and comfort of the men you command come next. Your own ease, comfort and safety come last, always and every time."*

## Life After Commissioning

### Posting and career:
- Commissioned as **Lieutenant** in the Indian Army
- First posting typically at a regiment — infantry, armoured corps, artillery, engineers, signals, or other arms
- Postings across India — from the Siachen Glacier to the Rajasthan desert to the Northeast. Military life involves frequent transfers (every 2-3 years)
- Promotion timeline: Captain (after 2 years), Major (after 6 years), Lt. Colonel (after 13 years), Colonel (after 15+ years based on selection)

### Benefits:
- Free furnished housing (or HRA) + free healthcare (lifetime, including family) + subsidized canteen + children's education allowance + 30 days annual leave
- **Pension after 20 years** — approximately 50% of last drawn pay, with DA
- **Post-retirement:** Corporate sector, security consulting, government services, CAPF (Central Armed Police Forces), and more
