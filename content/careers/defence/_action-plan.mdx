## Class 9-10: Building the Foundation

Defence is not just a career — it's a calling. If your child feels drawn to serving the nation, this is the most structured and rewarding path available to PCM students.

### For all paths:
- **Build physical fitness NOW.** Defence selection includes rigorous physical tests. Start with:
  - Running: Build up to 2.4 km in under 10 minutes
  - Push-ups: 30+ in one set
  - Pull-ups: 8+
  - Swimming (not mandatory but a huge advantage)
- **Maintain 60%+ in school** — NDA requires only 60% in Class 12, but higher marks keep civilian backup options open.
- **Develop leadership skills** — join NCC (National Cadet Corps) if available at your school. NCC 'C' certificate holders get bonus marks and direct entry to some courses.
- **Read about military history and current affairs** — defence interviews test general awareness deeply.
- **Work on communication skills** — SSB (Services Selection Board) interviews are 5 days long and test personality, leadership, and communication.

### Understanding the two main entry routes:
- **NDA (National Defence Academy):** Entry after Class 12. Written exam (UPSC) + SSB interview. Trains you for Army, Navy, or Air Force. This is the most prestigious route.
- **CDS (Combined Defence Services):** Entry after graduation. For those who want a civilian degree first.

### NCC advantage:
- NCC 'C' certificate holders can enter the armed forces through the **NCC Special Entry Scheme** — no written exam, only SSB interview. If your school offers NCC, join in Class 9 itself.

## Class 11: The Critical Year

### NDA preparation:
- **NDA exam is conducted by UPSC twice a year** — April and September. You can appear while in Class 12 or after.
- **Exam pattern:** Paper 1: Mathematics (300 marks), Paper 2: General Ability (600 marks = English + General Knowledge).
- **Start solving NDA previous year papers** — 10 years of PYQs are available free at [upsc.gov.in](https://www.upsc.gov.in).
- **Math preparation:** NDA math covers Class 11-12 level — Algebra, Matrices, Trigonometry, Calculus, Statistics. Your PCM background is a direct advantage.
- **General Ability:** Cover Physics, Chemistry, General Science, History, Geography, Current Affairs. Read a newspaper daily (The Hindu or Indian Express).

### Physical preparation (intensify):
- Join a structured fitness program. Many coaching centers now offer combined NDA academic + physical training.
- Target: 2.4 km run in 9 minutes, 40+ push-ups, 10+ pull-ups.
- Practice outdoor activities — cross-country running, team sports (football, basketball, volleyball).

### SSB preparation (start early):
- SSB is a 5-day assessment that tests: Officer-Like Qualities (OLQs), leadership, teamwork, psychological stability.
- **Read *Let's Crack SSB Interview* by Arihant** — the best starting resource.
- Practice **group discussions** and **impromptu speaking** — SSB includes GD, lecturette, and personal interviews.
- Develop hobbies and be ready to talk about them passionately — SSB wants well-rounded individuals, not exam machines.

## Class 12: Execution

### Month-by-month:
- **September (of Class 11 or early Class 12):** NDA-I registration opens at [upsc.gov.in](https://www.upsc.gov.in). Register early — do not miss the deadline.
- **October-January:** Continue NDA preparation alongside board exam preparation. Focus on General Knowledge and English vocabulary.
- **February-March:** Board exams. Score at least 60% aggregate (NDA requirement). Aim higher (75%+) to keep backup options open.
- **April:** NDA-I written exam. This is your first real attempt if in Class 12.
- **May:** NDA-II registration opens (for September exam).
- **June-July:** NDA-I results. If shortlisted, you'll be called for SSB interview (scheduled 2-3 months after results).
- **August-September:** SSB interview (5 days at the selection center). NDA-II written exam.
- **October-November:** Final merit list. Medical examination for selected candidates.
- **January (next year):** Join NDA, Khadakwasla (Pune) for 3-year training.

### Parallel backup routes:
- **After graduation:** CDS exam (also UPSC), Technical Entry Scheme (TES for engineering graduates), SSC (Short Service Commission).
- **Indian Air Force AFCAT:** For graduates wanting to join the Air Force (if NDA Air Force slot is missed).
- **Indian Navy — 10+2 B.Tech Cadet Entry:** Register at [joinindiannavy.gov.in](https://www.joinindiannavy.gov.in). PCM students with 70%+ in Class 12 are eligible.

### Important deadlines (typical year):
| Exam | Registration | Exam Date | Result |

| NDA-I | January | April | June |
| NDA-II | June | September | November |
| Navy 10+2 Entry | October | November (shortlist based) | January |

## Smart Strategy: What the Best Students Do

1. **NDA is the gold standard.** NDA-trained officers are considered the best in the armed forces. The 3-year training at NDA Khadakwasla + 1 year at IMA/Naval Academy/Air Force Academy is world-class. And it's completely free — the government pays you a stipend during training.
2. **Physical fitness is a filter, not an afterthought.** Every year, thousands of students clear the written exam but fail the physical or medical test. Start fitness training in Class 9 — not in Class 12.
3. **SSB is where most people fail.** Out of 10-15 candidates who appear for SSB, only 1-2 are recommended. The key is genuine personality development — not rehearsed answers. SSB assessors are trained psychologists who can spot fakes instantly.
4. **NCC 'C' certificate is the cheat code.** It provides direct SSB entry (no written exam), bonus marks in NDA/CDS, and demonstrates genuine interest in defence to the SSB panel.
5. **Have a civilian backup plan.** Defence selection rates are low (~1-2% overall). Smart families prepare for NDA while also keeping JEE/other exam options open. Many IIT graduates later join the armed forces through CDS or TES.
6. **Women in defence:** Women can now join the armed forces through CDS, AFCAT, and Indian Navy SSC. Permanent commission is now available for women in all branches. NDA has started accepting women candidates since 2022.
7. **Pay and benefits are excellent.** An NDA graduate joins as a Lieutenant at age 22 with ₹56,000/month + DA + Military Service Pay. By age 30 (Captain/Major), effective CTC is ₹15-18 LPA. Plus: free housing, free healthcare, subsidized canteen, and a lifetime pension after 20 years. Very few civilian jobs offer this security.
8. **Respect the commitment.** Minimum service obligation after NDA is 10 years (Army) to 14 years (Navy/Air Force). Make sure your child understands this is not a "try it and leave" career.
