## Why India for Aviation?

India is the world's third-largest domestic aviation market and the fastest-growing. IndiGo alone has ordered 500+ aircraft for delivery through 2035. Air India (now Tata-owned) has ordered 470 aircraft. Akasa Air, a new airline, is rapidly expanding. Boeing projects India will need 2,440 new pilots by 2030. The demand for pilots is real and growing.

**The salary trajectory:** A First Officer (co-pilot) at a major Indian airline earns ₹15-25 LPA. A Captain earns ₹70 LPA-1.2 Crore. At IndiGo, senior Captains earn over ₹1 Crore annually. These are among the highest salaries in any profession in India.

**The cost reality:** CPL training in India costs ₹35-80 lakh — it's expensive, no question. But with airline salaries, the loan is repayable within 3-5 years of joining an airline. SBI and Bank of Baroda offer pilot training loans up to ₹30-40 lakh at 9-11% interest.

## The Path

1. **Class 10-11:** Get a preliminary medical checkup at a DGCA-approved medical center. Vision correctable to 6/6 is acceptable (glasses/lenses allowed). Color vision must be normal. **Do this early** — don't invest years of preparation only to discover a medical disqualification.
2. **Class 12:** Score minimum 50% in PCM (DGCA requirement). Higher marks keep backup options open.
3. **After Class 12 — DGCA Class 1 Medical:** Full medical examination at a DGCA-approved center. This is mandatory before starting flight training.
4. **SPL (Student Pilot License):** Apply at DGCA. Minimum age: 16 years. This allows you to begin flying training.
5. **Join a DGCA-approved flying school:** Start flight training. Total flying hours needed: 200 hours for CPL.
6. **DGCA CPL Ground Exams:** Air Navigation, Aviation Meteorology, Air Regulation, Technical General — four written exams. Can be taken during flight training.
7. **CPL (Commercial Pilot License):** After completing 200 flying hours + all ground exams + DGCA Class 1 Medical, apply for CPL.
8. **Type Rating + Airline Application:** After CPL, complete a type rating on a specific aircraft (Boeing 737 or Airbus A320 — costs ₹15-25 lakh additional) and apply to airlines.

## What Parents Should Know

- **DGCA Class 1 Medical is the first filter.** Get your child checked at a DGCA-approved center in Class 10-11. Disqualifying conditions: uncorrectable vision beyond limits, color blindness, certain heart conditions, epilepsy, severe psychiatric conditions. Vision correctable to 6/6 with glasses/contacts is acceptable for CPL (but not for military flying).
- **Only fly at DGCA-approved schools.** Verify at [dgca.gov.in](https://www.dgca.gov.in). Unapproved schools cannot log flying hours recognized by DGCA. This makes the entire training worthless. Always check approval status before paying fees.
- **The gap between CPL and first airline job is real.** Airlines typically hire pilots with 500-1500+ flying hours. After CPL, your child has 200 hours. Building additional hours means working as a flight instructor, flying for charter companies, or paying for additional hours. Budget ₹10-20 lakh extra and 1-2 years for this phase.
- **Weather delays are common in India.** India's monsoon season (June-September) grounds training aircraft for weeks. This extends training duration. A "18-month" course often takes 24-30 months in practice.
- **Airline hiring is cyclical.** During expansion phases (current era — IndiGo, Air India, Akasa all expanding), hiring is strong. During downturns (COVID was devastating for pilot hiring), it freezes completely. Have 2 years of financial runway after CPL.
- **Physical and mental fitness must be maintained throughout career.** Pilots undergo annual Class 1 Medical renewals. Any medical issue — even temporary — can ground you. A healthy lifestyle is not optional; it's a career requirement.
- **Cadet pilot programs are the safest route.** Airlines like IndiGo, Air India occasionally run cadet pilot programs — they select you through aptitude tests, train you (you pay the training cost), and guarantee a job upon completion. Watch for announcements on airline career pages.
