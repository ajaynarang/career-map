## Why IGRUA?

IGRUA (Indira Gandhi Rashtriya Uran Akademi) is India's premier government-run flying training institute — the gold standard for pilot training in the country. Established in 1985 with collaboration from the French government, IGRUA uses modern training aircraft (currently Diamond DA40s and DA42s), has excellent airfield infrastructure, and is the most affordable CPL training option in India.

**For parents worried about cost:** IGRUA is the cheapest legitimate CPL training in India at ₹40-47 lakh total. Private flying schools charge ₹60-80 lakh for the same qualification. IGRUA's government backing means lower fees, maintained aircraft, and a structured training program — no shortcuts, no compromise on safety.

**The reputation premium:** Every major Indian airline knows and respects IGRUA. When IndiGo, Air India, or Akasa Air see an IGRUA-trained pilot's resume, it carries weight. IGRUA graduates consistently have strong records in airline selection processes because the training is thorough.

**The catch:** IGRUA is very competitive. Only 60-80 seats per intake, and thousands apply. Getting in requires passing an entrance exam, a personal interview, and the DGCA Class 1 Medical.

## How to Get In

1. **IGRUA Entrance Exam:** Check [igrua.gov.in](https://www.igrua.gov.in) for exam notification (typically released July-September). The exam tests Physics, Mathematics, English, and General Knowledge.
2. **Application:** Apply online at IGRUA's website. Application fee: approximately ₹1,000-2,000.
3. **Entrance exam:** Computer-based or pen-and-paper test. Physics and Math questions are at the Class 12 level. English tests communication ability (ICAO English is critical for pilots).
4. **Interview:** Shortlisted candidates are called for a personal interview at IGRUA campus. The interview assesses motivation, communication skills, awareness of aviation, and temperament.
5. **DGCA Class 1 Medical:** Must be completed before joining. Get examined at a DGCA-approved medical examiner. Cost: approximately ₹5,000-10,000.
6. **Age requirement:** 17-25 years at the time of admission.

**Preparation tip:** IGRUA's entrance exam is straightforward compared to JEE or NEET. Focus on Class 12 PCM fundamentals, brush up on current affairs and aviation basics, and practice clear English communication. The interview is equally important — show genuine passion for flying and awareness of the aviation industry.

## Training Structure

IGRUA offers a structured CPL course covering:

**Phase 1 — Ground School (3-4 months):**
- Air Navigation — chart reading, route planning, GPS, VOR, NDB
- Aviation Meteorology — weather systems, turbulence, visibility, flight planning
- Air Regulation — DGCA rules, airspace classification, radio telephony procedures
- Technical General — aircraft systems, engines, instruments, aerodynamics
- Students prepare for and take DGCA CPL ground exams during this phase

**Phase 2 — Flying Training (12-18 months):**
- **Single-engine training** on Diamond DA40 NG — basic flying, circuits, cross-country navigation, night flying
- **Multi-engine training** on Diamond DA42 Twin Star — multi-engine operations, asymmetric flight (one engine failure), instrument flying
- **Instrument Rating (IR)** — flying solely by instruments in simulated low-visibility conditions. Mandatory for CPL.
- Total flying hours: 200 hours minimum (DGCA requirement). IGRUA typically completes 200-220 hours.

**Phase 3 — CPL Issuance:**
- After completing all flying hours + ground exams + flight test with a DGCA examiner, your child receives the Commercial Pilot License
- The CPL allows them to fly as a co-pilot (First Officer) on commercial aircraft

**Training aircraft:**
- Diamond DA40 NG — modern single-engine, glass cockpit, excellent safety record
- Diamond DA42 Twin Star — twin-engine, used for multi-engine rating and instrument rating
- IGRUA's fleet is well-maintained by the government — this is a significant advantage over some private schools where aircraft maintenance can be a concern

## Life at IGRUA

IGRUA is located in Fursatganj, a small town near Amethi in Uttar Pradesh. This is a rural, quiet area — very different from living in a city.

- **Residential campus** — all trainees live on campus in hostel accommodation. Meals provided.
- **Focused environment** — there are few distractions. The small town setting means trainees are fully immersed in flying training.
- **Disciplined schedule** — early morning flights (weather is best), ground classes, self-study, physical fitness.
- **Small batch size** — 60-80 per intake means personal attention from instructors. The instructor-to-student ratio is favorable.
- **Weather challenges** — Uttar Pradesh has hot summers (45°C+), monsoon rains (June-September ground most flying), and foggy winters (December-January). This extends training duration. A "15-month" course often takes 18-24 months.
- **Connectivity** — nearest city: Lucknow (3 hours by road). Nearest railway station: Rae Bareli. The remote location is a trade-off for lower costs and focused training.

**Honest reality for parents:** IGRUA's location is isolated. Your child will spend 18-24 months in a small town with limited entertainment, shopping, or social life beyond the institute. Some students thrive in this environment (fewer distractions = more focus on flying). Others find it challenging. Discuss this openly.
