## Why Embry-Riddle?

Embry-Riddle Aeronautical University is the world's #1 aviation and aerospace university — and it's not close. Founded in 1926, Embry-Riddle has trained more pilots, more aviation engineers, and more aerospace professionals than any other institution on Earth. When aviation professionals say "the Harvard of the skies," they mean Embry-Riddle.

**For Indian families serious about aviation:** Embry-Riddle offers something no other flight school can — a world-class university degree combined with FAA pilot certification. The BS Aeronautical Science program gives your child both a Bachelor's degree and FAA CPL + Instrument + Multi-Engine ratings. If they ever need to transition out of flying (medical issue, career change), they have a respected university degree. This dual qualification is the safest bet in aviation.

**The career pipeline is unmatched:** Embry-Riddle has direct hiring agreements with US airlines through "pathway programs" — Delta Propel, United Aviate, and others. These programs select students early and provide a guaranteed path to a major US airline. For students who want to fly in the US (the world's highest-paying aviation market), this is the most direct route.

**For India-bound students:** Even if your child plans to return to India, the Embry-Riddle name carries enormous weight. Indian airlines recognize ERAU graduates, and the FAA CPL can be converted to DGCA CPL for flying in India.

## How to Get In

**Degree Program (BS Aeronautical Science — 4 years):**
1. Apply via Common Application or Embry-Riddle's own application portal.
2. SAT: 1100+ (or ACT 22+). TOEFL: 79 or IELTS: 6.0.
3. Class 12 transcripts with strong PCM results.
4. Personal essay and recommendation letters.
5. FAA Medical Certificate (Class 1 or Class 2) — complete before starting flight courses.

**Flight-Only Program (CPL — 12-18 months):**
1. Apply directly to Embry-Riddle's flight training program.
2. No SAT required. TOEFL/IELTS required for international students.
3. FAA Medical Certificate required.
4. M-1 visa (vocational student) — the school provides the I-20 form.
5. TSA clearance (handled by the school).

**Application timeline:**
- Early Action: November 1
- Regular Decision: Rolling admissions (apply by March for best scholarship consideration)
- Flight-only: Rolling admissions year-round

## Training & Curriculum

**BS Aeronautical Science (4 years):**
The gold standard in aviation education. Combines:
- FAA flight training: PPL → Instrument Rating → CPL → Multi-Engine Rating → CFI (Certified Flight Instructor)
- Academic coursework: aerodynamics, jet propulsion, aviation weather, airline management, crew resource management, aviation safety
- Total flying hours: 250+ hours by graduation
- Graduates earn FAA CPL + Instrument + Multi-Engine + CFI certificates alongside a Bachelor's degree

**Flight-Only CPL Program (12-18 months):**
- Focused exclusively on flight training and FAA exams
- PPL → Instrument Rating → CPL → Multi-Engine Rating
- 250+ flying hours
- No university degree — just licenses

**Training fleet:**
- Cessna 172 Skyhawk (single-engine, primary training)
- Diamond DA42 (multi-engine)
- Redbird simulators (advanced training devices)
- The fleet is large (100+ aircraft at Daytona Beach) — no waiting for aircraft. This is a major advantage over smaller schools.

**Airline pathway programs:**
- **Delta Propel:** Selected Embry-Riddle students receive a conditional job offer from Delta Air Lines before graduation. After building 1,500 hours, they go directly to Delta.
- **United Aviate:** Similar pathway to United Airlines.
- These programs are competitive but available to strong students.

## Daytona Beach Campus

Embry-Riddle's main campus in Daytona Beach, Florida is purpose-built for aviation.

- **On-airport campus** — the campus is literally on Daytona Beach International Airport. Students walk from class to their aircraft.
- **Florida weather** — 300+ flying days per year. Warm, sunny, and ideal for flight training. Monsoon delays (India's biggest training bottleneck) simply don't exist here.
- **Dedicated flight line** — over 100 aircraft, multiple runways, and an air traffic control tower managed by Embry-Riddle students.
- **SimLab** — advanced flight simulation center with full-motion simulators for instrument training.
- **Research opportunities** — Embry-Riddle conducts aviation research in safety, unmanned systems, and space. Students can participate from their sophomore year.

## Cost & Financial Planning

| Component | 4-Year Degree Program | Flight-Only CPL |
|-----------|----------------------|-----------------|
| Tuition (academic) | $42,000-48,000/year | N/A |
| Flight fees | $20,000-25,000/year | $75,000-95,000 total |
| Room & Board | $13,000-15,000/year | $12,000-18,000/year |
| Total | $300,000-350,000 (₹2.5-2.9 Cr) for 4 years | $90,000-110,000 (₹75-92L) for 12-18 months |

**Financial aid:** Embry-Riddle offers merit scholarships of $5,000-$18,000/year for international students. The degree program is expensive, but scholarships can reduce costs significantly.

**Loans:** Indian banks (SBI, Bank of Baroda) offer pilot training loans, but they typically cap at ₹30-40 lakh. For the full degree program, families often need additional funding.

**ROI consideration:** US airline pilot salaries are the highest in the world. A Captain at Delta, United, or American Airlines earns $300,000-$400,000/year. Even at regional airlines, First Officers start at $55,000-$80,000. If your child plans to build a career in US aviation, the investment is recovered within 5-7 years.
