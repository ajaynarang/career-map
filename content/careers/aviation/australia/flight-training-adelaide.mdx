## Why Flight Training Adelaide?

Flight Training Adelaide (FTA) has become the go-to flight school for Indian students training in Australia. Located at Parafield Airport in Adelaide, South Australia, FTA has trained thousands of international students (with a particularly strong Indian student community) and has built a reputation for reliable, structured training with excellent weather conditions. Adelaide offers 300+ flyable days per year — comparable to Florida but with less air traffic congestion.

**For Indian families specifically:** FTA understands Indian students. They have experience with Indian documentation, DGCA conversion requirements, and the specific needs of students far from home. The Indian student community at FTA is large and supportive — your child won't be alone. Many FTA Indian alumni now fly for Qantas, IndiGo, Air India, and airlines across the Middle East.

**The Adelaide advantage:** Adelaide is classified as a regional area for immigration purposes, which means graduates may qualify for extended post-study work visa (3-4 years instead of 2) and additional points for Permanent Residency applications. Adelaide is also one of the most affordable capital cities in Australia — significantly cheaper than Sydney or Melbourne.

## How to Get In

FTA has straightforward admission:

1. **Online Application:** Apply at [ftadelaide.com.au/apply](https://www.ftadelaide.com.au/apply). Submit personal details, academic records, and passport copy.
2. **CASA Aviation Medical:** Complete a CASA Class 1 Medical Examination with a CASA-designated DAME (Designated Aviation Medical Examiner). This can be done in India if there's an Australian-approved medical examiner available, or upon arrival in Adelaide. Requirements:
   - Vision correctable to 6/6 (glasses/contacts acceptable)
   - Normal color vision
   - Normal hearing
   - No disqualifying medical conditions (cardiovascular, neurological, psychiatric)
3. **IELTS:** 6.0 overall (no band below 5.5). TOEFL 64 also accepted.
4. **CRICOS enrollment:** FTA issues a CoE (Confirmation of Enrolment) for the CRICOS-registered Diploma of Aviation program.
5. **Student Visa (subclass 500):** Apply with CoE, IELTS, financial proof (AUD 24,505/year living costs + tuition), and OSHC (Overseas Student Health Cover).
6. **Start training:** New intakes throughout the year — FTA has flexible start dates.

## Training Structure

FTA offers structured CPL training following CASA regulations:

**Phase 1 — Private Pilot License (PPL) (3-4 months):**
- 40-50 flying hours
- Basic flying skills, circuits, solo flights, cross-country navigation
- Training aircraft: Cessna 172 or Diamond DA40
- CASA PPL theory exam + flight test

**Phase 2 — CPL Theory (concurrent with flying):**
- 7 CASA theory exams: Air Law, Flight Planning, Human Factors, Meteorology, Navigation, Performance, Aerodynamics
- Self-study with FTA ground school support
- Exams can be taken at CASA-approved testing centers

**Phase 3 — CPL Flying + Command Instrument Rating (8-12 months):**
- Advanced single-engine flying: cross-country, night flying, advanced manoeuvres
- Multi-engine training: Diamond DA42 Twin Star
- Instrument Rating: flying by instruments in simulated and actual low-visibility conditions
- Total: 200+ flying hours across PPL and CPL phases
- CASA CPL flight test + Multi-Engine Command Instrument Rating (MECIR) test

**Total training duration:** 15-20 months from enrollment to CPL with MECIR.

**Training aircraft:**
- Cessna 172S — reliable single-engine trainer, traditional instruments or glass cockpit
- Diamond DA40 NG — modern single-engine, glass cockpit
- Diamond DA42 Twin Star — multi-engine, glass cockpit
- FTA's fleet is well-maintained with modern aircraft — this matters for both safety and for training on current technology

**Adelaide's flying conditions:**
- 300+ flyable days per year
- Minimal air traffic congestion (unlike Sydney or Melbourne)
- Varied terrain for training — coastal, plains, and hills within the training area
- Parafield Airport is a dedicated training airfield — no commercial jet traffic to compete with

## After FTA — Career Paths

**Option 1: Stay in Australia**
- Apply for Temporary Graduate visa (subclass 485) — 2-4 years
- Work as a flight instructor at FTA or other schools (builds hours + earns income)
- Apply to regional airlines (Rex, QantasLink, Alliance Airlines) at 500-1000 hours
- Progress to major airlines (Qantas, Virgin Australia) at 2000+ hours
- Apply for Permanent Residency through skilled migration

**Option 2: Return to India**
- Convert CASA CPL to DGCA CPL by passing DGCA ground exams (6-8 months)
- Apply to Indian airlines (IndiGo, Air India, Akasa Air, SpiceJet)
- Starting salary in India: ₹15-22 LPA as First Officer

**Option 3: International airlines**
- CASA CPL is respected globally and can be converted to most national licenses
- Middle Eastern airlines (Emirates, Qatar, Etihad) accept or convert CASA credentials
- Southeast Asian airlines also hire CASA-qualified pilots

## Life in Adelaide

Adelaide is consistently ranked among the world's most livable cities — and it's the most affordable capital city in Australia.

- **Affordable living** — shared apartment: AUD 150-250/week. Groceries and daily expenses are 20-30% cheaper than Sydney or Melbourne
- **Safe and clean** — Adelaide is one of Australia's safest cities. Low crime, organized public transport, and a relaxed pace of life
- **Excellent weather** — warm, dry summers and mild winters. Very different from the cold of Toronto or the rain of London
- **Indian community** — Adelaide has a growing Indian community with temples, grocery stores (especially around the northern suburbs near Parafield), restaurants, and cultural events. Diwali celebrations are a highlight
- **Part-time work** — students can work 48 hours/fortnight. Adelaide's hospitality, retail, and agriculture sectors offer part-time jobs at AUD 24+/hour minimum wage
- **Parafield area** — the suburbs near Parafield Airport are affordable and convenient for FTA students. Many Indian students share apartments in Mawson Lakes and Salisbury
- **Regional area benefits** — Adelaide is classified as regional for immigration, meaning graduates get extended work visa duration and additional PR points
