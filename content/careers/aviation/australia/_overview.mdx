## Why Australia for Aviation?

Australia is one of the best places in the world to learn to fly. The country offers excellent weather year-round (particularly in South Australia and Queensland), vast uncongested airspace for training, and modern flight training infrastructure. CASA (Civil Aviation Safety Authority) CPL is internationally respected and can be converted to DGCA, FAA, or other national licenses.

**The weather advantage:** Australian cities like Adelaide, Darwin, and Townsville offer 300+ flying days per year. Unlike India (monsoon), the US Northeast (winter), or the UK (persistent cloud), Australian weather allows consistent, uninterrupted training. This means faster completion times — 14-20 months is realistic.

**The lifestyle factor:** Australia offers warm weather, safe cities, and a high quality of life. For Indian students who are intimidated by US visa complexity or UK weather, Australia is an attractive middle ground.

**The career prospects:** Australia's aviation industry is growing. Qantas, Virgin Australia, Rex Regional Express, Bonza, and regional operators are expanding. Qantas alone has ordered 24 Airbus A350s for delivery from 2027. The pilot shortage is real in Australia — particularly for regional and charter operations.

## The Path

1. **Class 11-12:** Focus on PCM. Prepare for IELTS (aim for 6.0+). Get a medical checkup.
2. **Research Australian flight schools:** Flight Training Adelaide (FTA), Basair (Sydney), Airways Aviation (multiple locations), Oxford Aviation Academy Australia (now Airways Aviation), PAA (Parafield Airport).
3. **Apply to flight schools:** Most accept rolling applications for February and July intakes.
4. **CASA Medical:** Complete a CASA Class 1 Aviation Medical Examination with a CASA-designated DAME (Designated Aviation Medical Examiner).
5. **Student Visa (subclass 500):** Apply for a student visa with CoE, IELTS, financial proof, and OSHC.
6. **Begin training:** Ground school + flying. CASA CPL requires 200 flying hours minimum.

## What Parents Should Know

- **Choose a CRICOS-registered school for visa eligibility.** CRICOS (Commonwealth Register of Institutions and Courses for Overseas Students) registration is mandatory for the school to enroll international students. Verify at the CRICOS website before applying.
- **Flight Training Adelaide (FTA) is the most popular choice for Indian students.** FTA has trained thousands of Indian students and has dedicated support for international trainees. Adelaide's weather is excellent for flying.
- **CASA CPL requires 200 flying hours.** Training typically takes 14-20 months. Adelaide and Queensland locations offer the most consistent weather.
- **Post-study work visa varies.** The Temporary Graduate visa (subclass 485) duration depends on your qualification and location. A diploma may get 2 years; a degree gets more. Studying in regional areas (Adelaide qualifies) may extend the visa.
- **Building hours in Australia is possible.** Regional and charter operators in outback Australia hire low-hour pilots. The flying is demanding (unpaved strips, remote locations, varied weather) but it builds hours fast and produces skilled, adaptable pilots.
- **Australian pilot salaries are competitive.** Regional airline First Officers: AUD 60,000-80,000/year. Major airline Captains (Qantas): AUD 250,000-400,000/year.
- **CASA CPL to DGCA conversion:** If your child returns to India, they must pass DGCA ground exams to convert. Similar process to FAA or UK CAA conversion.
