## Why IISER Kolkata?

IISER Kolkata has carved a strong reputation in theoretical physics, mathematical sciences, and chemical sciences since its founding in 2006. Located near Kolkata — a city with deep scientific heritage (think C.V. Raman, S.N. Bose, J.C. Bose) — it inherits a culture of intellectual rigor and curiosity.

What distinguishes IISER Kolkata: its Department of Physical Sciences is particularly strong in theoretical and computational physics (quantum information, condensed matter, high-energy physics). The Department of Chemical Sciences has built an impressive portfolio in organic synthesis and catalysis. And the relatively new Computational and Data Sciences program bridges science with modern data-driven approaches.

**Career outcomes:** IISER Kolkata graduates consistently secure PhD positions at top global universities — ETH Zurich, Max Planck, University of Tokyo, UC Berkeley, and more. Those choosing industry enter data science and analytics roles at ₹10-18 LPA.

## How to Get In

Same three-channel system as all IISERs. IISER Kolkata admits approximately 200 students per year.

### Channels
1. **Channel 1:** KVPY/INSPIRE fellowship or National Olympiad qualifications
2. **Channel 2:** JEE Advanced qualified rank
3. **Channel 3:** Top 1% in Class 12 boards + IISER IAT score

All applications through [iiseradmission.in](https://www.iiseradmission.in). Common counselling across all 7 IISERs — you rank your IISER preferences and get allocated based on merit.

**Tip for parents:** IISER Kolkata is often slightly less competitive than IISER Pune in counselling cutoffs. This means equally strong academics but a slightly easier entry point. Smart families use this to their advantage.

## Curriculum & Structure

5-year BS-MS Dual Degree.

- **Years 1-3:** Core courses across all sciences + progressive specialization
- **Year 4:** Advanced courses and research rotations — students explore different labs before committing
- **Year 5:** Full research thesis with a faculty mentor
- **Computational and Data Sciences (CDS):** A unique interdisciplinary major combining statistics, machine learning, and scientific computing — excellent for students who want both science depth and industry-relevant skills
- **Research collaborations:** IISER Kolkata faculty actively collaborate with Saha Institute of Nuclear Physics, SINP, and Bose Institute — students benefit from this wider Kolkata science ecosystem
- **Semester exchange:** Partnerships with universities in Germany, Japan, and the USA for research internships

## Campus Life

IISER Kolkata's permanent campus at Mohanpur (Kalyani area, about 50 km from Kolkata city center) is a modern, self-contained campus.

- **Modern infrastructure** — purpose-built labs, central library, high-performance computing facility
- **Small batch intimacy** — 200 students per year means close-knit community and strong faculty mentoring
- **Cultural life** — annual Inquivesta (science and cultural fest), music and drama clubs, and a literary culture that reflects Kolkata's heritage
- **Hostel living** — on-campus, affordable, with mess facilities
- **Kolkata connection** — the city offers rich cultural experiences (Durga Puja, film, literature, food) and is significantly more affordable than Mumbai, Bangalore, or Delhi
- **Transport** — campus is accessible by local trains and bus from Kolkata. The upcoming metro extension will improve connectivity
