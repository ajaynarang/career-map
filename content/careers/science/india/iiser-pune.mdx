## Why IISER Pune?

IISER Pune is the flagship of the IISER system and India's best institution for integrated science education. Founded in 2006, it has rapidly risen to become a research powerhouse — faculty here publish in Nature, Science, and Physical Review Letters with a frequency that rivals much older institutions.

What makes IISER Pune special for your child: the BS-MS dual degree is a 5-year integrated program that combines rigorous coursework with a full year of research. Graduates leave with a Master's-level qualification and genuine research experience — making them top candidates for PhD programs at MIT, Stanford, Cambridge, Max Planck, and ETH Zurich.

**The research advantage:** IISER Pune's research output per faculty member is among the highest in India. Departments in Chemistry (especially Catalysis and Materials Chemistry), Physics (Astrophysics, Condensed Matter), and Math (Algebraic Geometry, Number Theory) are internationally recognized.

## How to Get In

IISER Pune admits through three channels. About 200-250 students are admitted per year.

### Channel 1: KVPY/INSPIRE + Olympiads
- Students with KVPY fellowship or National Olympiad qualifications (top performers in NSEP/NSEC/RMO/NSEJS)
- Strongest candidates; direct admission based on merit

### Channel 2: JEE Advanced
- Valid JEE Advanced rank (any rank within the qualified list)
- Seats allocated through IISER common counselling

### Channel 3: IAT (IISER Aptitude Test)
- Eligibility: Top 1% in Class 12 state/central board exam
- IAT is an aptitude test covering Physics, Chemistry, Math, and Biology
- Apply at [iiseradmission.in](https://www.iiseradmission.in)

**Timeline:**
- April-May: IAT registration opens
- June: IAT exam + JEE Advanced
- July: IISER common counselling across all 7 IISERs
- August: Classes begin

**Strategy tip:** Apply through ALL channels you're eligible for. Many students qualify through both JEE Advanced and IAT — use whichever gives you the better seat.

## Curriculum & Structure

5-year BS-MS Dual Degree with a mandatory research thesis.

- **Years 1-2:** Compulsory courses in Physics, Chemistry, Math, Biology, and Earth Science. Every student gets a broad foundation — even if you love Physics, you'll study organic chemistry and molecular biology
- **Year 3:** Begin specialization. Choose a major discipline and start electives
- **Year 4:** Advanced courses in your major + begin identifying research problems
- **Year 5:** Full research thesis (equivalent to a Master's thesis). Students work with a faculty advisor on original research. Many publish papers during this year
- **Lab courses from year 1:** Hands-on experimental work is integral — not just theory
- **Interdisciplinary encouraged:** A Math major can do research in theoretical physics; a Chemistry student can work on computational biology
- **Summer research internships:** IISER Pune students regularly intern at TIFR, JNCASR, Max Planck Institutes, and US universities

## Campus Life

IISER Pune's campus is in Pashan, a quiet locality in Pune surrounded by hills — ideal for focused study.

- **Small batches = strong bonds** — 200 students per batch means you know your classmates well. Faculty are accessible and often become mentors for life
- **150-acre campus** — modern labs, well-equipped library, sports facilities, and an astronomical observatory
- **Student-run science outreach** — Karavaan (annual fest), Science Activity Centre, and student journals
- **Pune is India's education city** — pleasant weather, cosmopolitan culture, affordable living, and close to Mumbai (3 hours)
- **Hostel life** — on-campus housing guaranteed. Clean rooms, Wi-Fi, and mess with varied cuisine
- **Research atmosphere** — evening talks, seminars, and journal clubs are common. The culture is academic without being cutthroat
