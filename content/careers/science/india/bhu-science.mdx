## Why BHU for Science?

Banaras Hindu University is one of India's oldest and most respected universities, founded by Pandit Madan Mohan Malaviya in 1916. Its **Faculty of Science** has over a century of research tradition and has produced generations of India's scientists, professors, and researchers.

**Why BHU science is worth considering:**

- **Century-old research tradition** — BHU's physics and chemistry departments have been producing quality research since before independence. The labs, libraries, and research culture have deep roots
- **Practically free** — at ₹5,000-10,000/year tuition, BHU science is one of the cheapest quality science educations in India
- **CUET entry** — easier admission than IISERs or IISc. A student who scores well in CUET Physics/Maths gets a seat at a 100-year-old institution
- **Integrated MSc option** — 5-year Integrated MSc programmes give a complete education without the uncertainty of a separate MSc admission
- **Gateway to research** — BHU science graduates consistently clear JAM, CSIR NET, and GATE. Many pursue PhDs at IITs, IISc, and abroad

**The honest take:** BHU is not IISc or IISER. Research infrastructure has improved but still lags behind these institutions. However, for the price and accessibility (CUET is far easier than JEE Advanced or IISER IAT), BHU offers remarkable value. It's especially suitable for students from UP, Bihar, Jharkhand, and MP who want to stay closer to home while getting quality science education.

## How to Get In

Entry through **CUET**:

1. Register for CUET at [cuet.samarth.ac.in](https://cuet.samarth.ac.in)
2. Choose Physics and Mathematics (and/or Chemistry) as domain subjects
3. Apply through BHU's admission portal
4. Merit-based admission using CUET scores

**Cutoffs (approximate CUET percentile):**
- BSc (Hons) Physics: 90%+
- BSc (Hons) Chemistry: 85%+
- BSc (Hons) Mathematics: 92%+
- Integrated MSc Physics: 88%+

**Advantage:** CUET cutoffs for BHU science are lower than for DU top colleges. A student scoring 90 percentile in CUET can get into BHU Physics — at DU, the same score might only get mid-tier colleges.

## What Makes It Special

- **Massive campus** — 1,300 acres, one of Asia's largest. The campus is a small city with its own hospital, temples, markets, and cultural venues
- **Research centres** — DST-FIST funded departments, Centre of Advanced Study in many departments
- **Interdisciplinary access** — science students can take courses at IIT BHU (located within the same campus) and other BHU faculties
- **IIT BHU is on the same campus** — collaboration between BHU science and IIT BHU is natural and common
- **Cultural immersion** — studying in Varanasi means experiencing India's oldest living city. The Ganga, temples, and cultural richness add depth to the education

## Campus and Student Life

BHU's 1,300-acre campus is essentially a self-sufficient town:

- **Spardha and Kashi Yatra** — BHU's sports and cultural festivals are among the largest in North India
- **Vishwanath Temple** — the famous BHU temple is on campus, a spiritual landmark
- **Library** — Sayaji Rao Gaekwad Library is one of India's largest university libraries with rare manuscripts and books
- **Hostel life** — affordable hostels with a strong community. Mess food is good and cheap (₹30-40/meal)
- **Varanasi ghats** — evening walks to Assi Ghat and Dashashwamedh Ghat are a BHU tradition
- **Student diversity** — students from across India, especially North and East India
- **Affordable city** — Varanasi is very affordable. An entire year of living expenses can be under ₹50,000
