## Why IIST?

IIST (Indian Institute of Space Science and Technology) is the world's first university dedicated entirely to space science and technology. Established by ISRO in 2007, IIST has a unique proposition that no other college in India — or the world — can match: **guaranteed placement at ISRO** for top-performing graduates.

**Why IIST is extraordinary:**

- **ISRO placement guaranteed** — top BTech graduates are directly absorbed into ISRO as Scientist/Engineer 'SC' grade officers. This is the only college in India where a government job at a premier organization is virtually guaranteed
- **Space science focus** — the curriculum is designed by ISRO scientists. Students learn from people who have actually built satellites, launched rockets, and planned Mars missions
- **World-class faculty** — IIST faculty includes former ISRO project directors and scientists with international research credentials
- **Affordable** — ₹1L/year with ISRO scholarships making it even cheaper. Most students pay very little out of pocket
- **Unique career path** — IIST graduates work on Chandrayaan, Gaganyaan, GSLV, and other missions. This is not just a job — it's a career in nation-building

**The honest take:** IIST is extremely niche. It's perfect for students who dream of building rockets, satellites, or working in space science. If your child wants to work at Google or start a fintech company, IIST is the wrong choice. But if they watch rocket launches with excitement and wonder how satellites work — IIST is their dream college.

**Important caveat:** IIST students who take the ISRO scholarship sign a service bond (typically 3-5 years). If they leave before the bond period, they must repay the scholarship. Students who don't take the scholarship have no bond and can join any company.

## How to Get In

IIST admits through **JEE Advanced**, but through a **separate counselling process** (not JoSAA):

1. Clear JEE Main (top 2.5 lakh qualifiers)
2. Appear for JEE Advanced
3. Apply separately to IIST at [iist.ac.in/admissions](https://www.iist.ac.in/admissions)
4. Cutoff ranks are more accessible than top IITs

**Cutoffs (approximate, General category):**
- BTech Aerospace: JEE Advanced rank 3000-6000
- BTech Avionics: rank 3000-7000
- BSc-MSc Dual Degree (Physics/Chemistry/Maths): rank 5000-10000

**Strategic advantage:** IIST counselling is separate from JoSAA. So a student can apply to IIST and also participate in JoSAA counselling for IITs/NITs. If they get into both, they can choose. There's no downside to applying.

## What Makes It Special

- **Only space university in the world** — unique, irreplaceable experience
- **ISRO campus integration** — VSSC (Vikram Sarabhai Space Centre), IIST's parent ISRO centre, is literally next door. Students interact with ISRO scientists daily
- **Real projects** — students work on actual satellite subsystems, not just textbook problems. IIST student satellites have been launched by ISRO
- **Chandrayaan and Gaganyaan connections** — IIST alumni are working on India's most ambitious space missions right now
- **International collaborations** — partnerships with NASA, ESA, JAXA, and DLR for student exchanges and research

## Campus and Student Life

100-acre campus in Valiamala, about 20 km from Thiruvananthapuram city:

- **Dhanak** — IIST's annual tech-cultural festival
- **Small batch = close community** — about 200 students per year. Everyone knows everyone, and seniors actively mentor juniors
- **Rocket launches** — students sometimes get to witness ISRO launches at Sriharikota. The excitement is indescribable
- **Sports and recreation** — good facilities for cricket, football, basketball, badminton
- **Kerala beauty** — beaches, backwaters, and Western Ghats are all within reach. Kovalam beach is a popular weekend destination
- **Hostel life** — all students stay on campus. Small community feel, but can feel isolating for some
- **Food** — Kerala cuisine in the mess is excellent. Thiruvananthapuram has great restaurants for weekend outings
- **Research opportunities** — students can do projects at VSSC, LPSC (Liquid Propulsion Systems Centre), and other ISRO centres in Trivandrum
