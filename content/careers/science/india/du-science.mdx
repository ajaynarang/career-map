## Why DU for Science?

Delhi University's BSc (Honours) Physics programme — especially at colleges like **St. Stephen's College, Hindu College, and Kirori Mal College** — has produced an extraordinary number of India's top scientists, physicists, and researchers. The list of DU physics alumni includes ISRO scientists, IIT professors, international researchers, and even successful entrepreneurs.

**Why DU science works:**

- **The college system** — DU is not one institution but 60+ colleges, each with its own culture. St. Stephen's (intellectual, elitist, literary), Hindu College (vibrant, ambitious, competitive), and Kirori Mal (grounded, research-oriented) each offer distinct experiences
- **Absurdly affordable** — a 3-year BSc costs ₹1.5-3L total. Quality education for the price of a few months of coaching
- **Delhi ecosystem** — students attend lectures at JNU, research talks at IIT Delhi, and events at CSIR labs. The city itself is a classroom
- **Gateway to top MSc/PhD programmes** — DU BSc graduates consistently clear JAM (for IIT MSc), CSIR NET, and get PhD admits at top global universities
- **CUET entry** — fair, exam-based admission. No subjectivity.

**The honest take:** DU BSc Physics alone doesn't lead to high-paying jobs directly. The 3-year degree is best seen as a stepping stone to MSc (at IITs, IISc, or abroad) and then PhD or specialized roles. Students who stop at BSc often end up in teaching or generic IT roles. But those who continue their education build exceptional careers in science and research.

## How to Get In

Entry through **CUET**:

1. Register for CUET at [cuet.samarth.ac.in](https://cuet.samarth.ac.in)
2. Choose Physics and Mathematics as domain subjects
3. Score well — cutoffs vary dramatically by college

**College-wise cutoffs (approximate CUET percentile):**
- **St. Stephen's College:** 99%+ (plus interview for Stephen's)
- **Hindu College:** 98%+
- **Kirori Mal College:** 96%+
- **Hansraj College:** 95%+
- **Ramjas College:** 92%+
- **SGTB Khalsa:** 90%+

**St. Stephen's note:** Stephen's is the only DU college that conducts interviews alongside CUET scores. The interview carries weight and evaluates breadth of knowledge, curiosity, and communication.

## What Makes It Special

- **Top colleges are world-class at Indian prices** — Stephen's physics department has produced Padma awardees and Royal Society fellows
- **North Campus culture** — the stretch from Stephen's to Hindu to Kirori Mal is India's most intellectually dense college ecosystem
- **Research exposure** — faculty at top DU colleges are active researchers. Students assist in labs and co-author papers
- **Flexibility** — after BSc, students can pivot to data science, finance, civil services, or continue in science. The options are wide open
- **Cultural richness** — fests, societies, debates, theatre, and music are integral to the DU experience

## Campus and Student Life

DU's North Campus is the heart of the science experience:

- **College-specific culture** — each college has its own societies, fests, and personality. Hindu's Doxa, Stephen's Stephanian, KMC's Kiran are distinct worlds
- **North Campus hangouts** — Tom Uncle's Maggi Point, Hudson Lane cafes, Kamla Nagar market. These are as much a part of DU as the classrooms
- **Societies and clubs** — debating, dramatics, photography, quiz, and science societies at every college
- **Library culture** — Delhi University's Central Science Library and college libraries are excellent
- **Hostel availability is limited** — most outstation students find PGs in North Campus area (₹8,000-15,000/month)
- **Metro connectivity** — Vishwavidyalaya metro station connects North Campus to all of Delhi
- **Inter-college friendships** — DU students freely attend events, lectures, and cultural programmes across colleges
