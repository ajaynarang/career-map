## Why IISc Bangalore?

IISc is India's undisputed #1 research institution. While IITs are engineering powerhouses, IISc is where India's most important scientific research happens — from quantum computing to materials science to climate modeling. The BS (Research) program, launched in 2011, gives undergraduates direct access to this research ecosystem from day one.

What makes IISc special: a 400-acre green campus in Bangalore with ₹1,800+ Crore annual research funding, 45+ departments, and a faculty that includes Fellows of the Royal Society, National Academy of Sciences members, and Bhatnagar awardees. Your child will learn from scientists who are defining their fields, not just teaching from textbooks.

**The career math:** IISc BS graduates who pursue PhDs (about 70%) go to MIT, Stanford, Caltech, Princeton, and Max Planck with full funding. Those entering industry earn ₹15-25 LPA in roles at Google, Microsoft Research, and Goldman Sachs — and this number climbs rapidly post-PhD to ₹40-80 LPA.

## How to Get In

IISc admits only **120-140 students** per year into the BS program — making it more selective per seat than any IIT.

**Two pathways:**
1. **JEE Advanced:** Top-ranked students are eligible. Typical cutoff is within top 5,000 ranks
2. **KVPY/INSPIRE:** Students with KVPY fellowship (now transitioning to INSPIRE-based selection) can apply directly

**Timeline:**
- May-June: JEE Advanced
- June-July: IISc application opens at [iisc.ac.in/admissions](https://www.iisc.ac.in/admissions)
- July: Merit list and counselling
- August: Classes begin

**Reality check:** IISc's BS program is intensely competitive. The batch is tiny, and every student is exceptional. But unlike IIT admissions where branch matters enormously, at IISc every student gets exposure to all sciences before specializing — there are no "bad branches."

## Curriculum & Structure

4-year BS (Research) with a mandatory research thesis in the final year.

- **First 2 years:** Core courses across Physics, Chemistry, Math, and Biology — every student gets a broad scientific foundation
- **Year 3:** Specialize in one department (Physics, Chemistry, Math, Biology, Materials, or Earth Science)
- **Year 4:** Advanced courses + substantial research thesis project with a faculty mentor
- **Research from year 1:** Students can join labs from the first year itself — this is encouraged and common
- **Interdisciplinary flexibility:** A Physics major can do research in a Materials Science or Astronomy lab
- **Summer research:** IISc has formal summer research programs, and students frequently intern at international labs (CERN, Max Planck, NIH)

## Campus Life

IISc's 400-acre campus in North Bangalore is one of India's most beautiful — tree-lined roads, historic buildings, and modern research facilities coexisting.

- **Tiny batch = close community** — 120-140 BS students means everyone knows everyone. Faculty-student ratio is outstanding
- **Gymkhana and cultural life** — annual Pravega (science festival) and cultural events, though the campus is quieter than IITs
- **Bangalore advantage** — India's tech capital, with access to startups, research labs (ISRO, DRDO, NAL), and a thriving science community
- **Hostel life** — guaranteed on-campus housing. Messes serve South Indian and North Indian food
- **Libraries and labs** — JRD Tata Memorial Library is one of India's best science libraries. Labs are well-funded and modern
- **Green campus** — home to 110+ species of birds, deer, and abundant greenery. A peaceful environment for deep thinking
