## Why JNU for Science?

JNU (Jawaharlal Nehru University) is known for social sciences and humanities, but its **School of Physical Sciences (SPS)** is a hidden gem for physics students. SPS has produced some of India's finest physicists, and its research output in condensed matter physics, theoretical physics, and material science rivals that of many IITs.

**Why consider JNU for science:**

- **Practically free education** — ₹12,000/year tuition, heavily subsidized hostels and mess. Total cost for a 5-year Integrated MSc-PhD is under ₹1.5 lakh
- **Research-first culture** — JNU's science departments are research-focused from day one. Faculty are active researchers publishing in top journals
- **PhD pipeline** — JNU physics graduates routinely get PhD admits at top global universities (MIT, Princeton, Max Planck) with full funding
- **CUET entry** — no need for JEE Advanced. A strong CUET score in Physics and Maths is sufficient
- **Delhi location** — access to other research institutions (IIT Delhi, NPL, IITD, JNU's own inter-school collaborations)

**The honest take:** JNU science is not for students who want high-paying industry placements immediately after graduation. It's for students genuinely passionate about physics and research. If your child wants to become a scientist, professor, or researcher, JNU is outstanding value.

## How to Get In

Entry through **CUET (Common University Entrance Test)**:

1. Register for CUET at [cuet.samarth.ac.in](https://cuet.samarth.ac.in)
2. Choose Physics and Mathematics as domain subjects
3. Score in the top 5% percentile
4. Apply through JNU's admission portal
5. Merit-based admission through CUET scores

**CUET preparation:** If your child is already studying Class 12 PCM well, CUET is NCERT-level. No coaching needed — just thorough NCERT revision and practice with CUET mock tests.

## What Makes It Special

- **Intellectual atmosphere** — JNU is India's most intellectually vibrant campus. Debates, discussions, and questioning are encouraged at every level
- **Interdisciplinary exposure** — science students interact with students from international relations, economics, linguistics, and social sciences. This broadens perspectives enormously
- **Faculty accessibility** — small batch sizes mean students get significant one-on-one time with faculty
- **Research infrastructure** — well-equipped labs funded by DST, CSIR, and international grants
- **Publication culture** — even MSc students co-author papers in international journals

## Campus and Student Life

1,000-acre green campus in South Delhi — one of the most beautiful university campuses in India:

- **Vibrant political and intellectual culture** — JNU is famous for its open discussions, debates, and diverse viewpoints
- **Diverse student body** — students from every state, every background. JNU's reservation and admission policies ensure genuine diversity
- **Affordable living** — hostel rooms at ₹100-200/month, mess food at ₹30-50/meal. JNU is possibly India's most affordable quality education
- **Delhi access** — AIIMS, IIT Delhi, and South Delhi are nearby. Students attend seminars, workshops, and cultural events across the city
- **Green, forested campus** — deer, peacocks, and abundant birdlife. Evening walks on campus are iconic
- **Language and culture festivals** — JNU celebrates every regional festival, creating a truly pan-Indian experience
