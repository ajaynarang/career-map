## Why India for Science & Research?

India's science education has a hidden gem problem — most parents haven't heard of IISERs, NISER, or CMI, but these are world-class institutions. While everyone fights for IIT seats, science-focused students can enter equally prestigious institutes with smaller batches (40-60 students per branch), more personal faculty attention, and a direct pathway to PhD programs globally.

**The numbers:** IISER and IISc graduates have a 60-70% rate of going to top global PhD programs (MIT, Stanford, Caltech, Max Planck). Those who enter industry earn ₹12-25 LPA in data science, analytics, and quantitative finance roles. The 5-year BS-MS at IISERs costs under ₹15L total — compare that to ₹2-3 Crore for a US undergraduate degree.

## The Path

1. **Class 11-12:** Prepare for JEE (overlaps heavily with IAT/NEST). Focus on Olympiads (NSEP, NSEC, RMO) — these build the deep thinking that science demands
2. **After 12th boards:** IAT (IISER Aptitude Test, June), NEST (for NISER/CEBS, June), JEE Advanced (for IISc, May-June)
3. **Three channels into IISERs:** Channel 1 (KVPY/INSPIRE), Channel 2 (JEE Advanced), Channel 3 (State/Central board top 1% + IAT)
4. **Counselling:** IISER common counselling (July), IISc admission (July)

## Key Entrance Exams

### IISER IAT (IISER Aptitude Test)
- **What:** Aptitude test covering Physics, Chemistry, Math, and Biology
- **When:** June (after 12th boards)
- **For:** All 7 IISERs (Pune, Kolkata, Mohali, Bhopal, Thiruvananthapuram, Tirupati, Berhampur)
- **Website:** [iiseradmission.in](https://www.iiseradmission.in)

### NEST (National Entrance Screening Test)
- **What:** Tests deep conceptual understanding in Physics, Chemistry, Math, Biology
- **When:** June
- **For:** NISER Bhubaneswar and CEBS Mumbai
- **Website:** [nestexam.in](https://www.nestexam.in)

### JEE Advanced
- **What:** Same exam used for IIT admission
- **For:** IISc Bangalore (BS program), IISERs (Channel 2)
- **Website:** [jeeadv.ac.in](https://jeeadv.ac.in)

## What Parents Should Know

- **IISERs are NOT less than IITs.** They serve a different purpose — research training vs. professional engineering. If your child loves understanding "why" rather than "how to apply," IISERs are the better choice.
- **The BS-MS is 5 years, not 4.** The fifth year includes a full research thesis. This is a feature, not a bug — graduates are treated as Master's-level researchers globally.
- **INSPIRE Scholarship** (₹80,000/year + ₹20,000/year research grant) is available for students in the top 1% of boards or with Olympiad qualifications. Apply at [online-inspire.gov.in](https://online-inspire.gov.in).
- **Placement vs. PhD:** About 60-70% of IISER graduates pursue PhDs (fully funded, usually abroad). The remaining 30-40% enter data science, finance, or tech roles at ₹12-25 LPA. There is no traditional "placement cell" like IITs — science careers work differently.
- **CMI and ISI** (Chennai Mathematical Institute and Indian Statistical Institute) are outstanding for mathematics and statistics — smaller, more specialized, and deeply respected globally.
- **The practical backup:** A Physics or Math degree from IISER + Python/R skills = Data Scientist (₹15-40 LPA). Science graduates are increasingly preferred for quantitative finance (₹20-50 LPA at firms like Goldman Sachs, Tower Research).
