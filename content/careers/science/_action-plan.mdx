## Class 9-10: Building the Foundation

Science research is about curiosity and deep thinking. This is the time to discover which branch of science excites your child most.

### For all paths:
- **Master NCERT PCM completely** — understand concepts, don't just memorize. Science research rewards understanding "why," not just "how."
- **Start reading popular science:** Books like *A Short History of Nearly Everything* (Bryson), *Surely You're Joking, Mr. Feynman*, or *The Gene* (Mukherjee) build genuine interest.
- **Explore Olympiads:** NSEP (Physics), NSEC (Chemistry), RMO (Math) — even if your child doesn't qualify for the international level, the preparation builds deep problem-solving skills.
- **Maintain 85%+ in school** — essential for all international paths and IISER/NISER admissions.
- **Learn basic Python** — data science and computational research require coding. Start at [freeCodeCamp](https://freecodecamp.org).

### If targeting India (IISER/IISc path):
- Understand the three routes into IISERs: **IISER IAT** (aptitude test), **KVPY** (now discontinued, replaced by INSPIRE), **JEE Advanced** (channel 2), and **state board marks** (channel 3).
- Begin Olympiad preparation seriously — NSEP/NSEC/RMO in Class 10 gives a strong foundation.

### If targeting USA:
- Build a **research-oriented profile** — reach out to college professors for summer projects (yes, even in Class 10). Email 20 professors; 1-2 will respond.
- Start SAT prep in summer after Class 10.
- Explore programs like **MIT PRIMES**, **RSI (Research Science Institute)**, and **SSTP (Summer Science Training Program)**.

### If targeting Germany:
- **START GERMAN NOW.** Enroll at [Goethe-Institut](https://www.goethe.de/ins/in/en/index.html). Goal: A2 by end of Class 10.
- Germany has world-class science universities (LMU Munich, Heidelberg, TU Munich) with nearly free tuition.

### If targeting UK:
- Start reading beyond the syllabus in your preferred science — Cambridge and Oxford interview you on depth of understanding, not breadth.

## Class 11: The Critical Year

### For India (IISER/IISc):
- **Register for NEST** (National Entrance Screening Test) conducted by NISER/CEBS — usually in June. Check [nestexam.in](https://www.nestexam.in).
- **Prepare for IISER IAT** — aptitude-based, tests scientific reasoning across PCM and biology.
- If also preparing for JEE Advanced (which gives IISER admission via channel 2), join serious coaching.
- **Apply for INSPIRE Scholarship** (₹80,000/year) through [online-inspire.gov.in](https://online-inspire.gov.in) — for students in top 1% of boards or with Olympiad qualifications.

### For USA:
- **Take the SAT** — aim for 1450+ for top research universities. You can retake.
- **Build your research spike:** Publish a paper, present at a science fair (IRIS National Fair, Google Science Fair), or complete a meaningful research project.
- Take **AP Physics C, AP Chemistry, AP Calculus BC** if available.
- Start your college list: MIT, Caltech, Stanford, University of Chicago, and strong state universities like UC Berkeley, UIUC, UMich.

### For Germany:
- **Reach German B1.** Take Goethe B1 exam.
- Research programs in English — many German universities now offer Bachelor's in English (TU Munich, RWTH Aachen for some programs).

### For UK:
- **Start UCAS research.** UK applications are due October of Class 12.
- If targeting **Cambridge Natural Sciences** or **Oxford Physics/Chemistry**, prepare for interviews and admissions tests (NSAA, PAT, or their replacements).
- Draft your **personal statement** — UK wants academic passion, not extracurriculars.

### For Canada & Australia:
- Focus on **board exam scores** (85%+) — both countries weight grades heavily.
- Take **IELTS** (target 7.0+). Register at [ielts.org](https://www.ielts.org).

## Class 12: Execution

### Month-by-month (India path):
- **July-September:** Complete Class 12 syllabus. Continue Olympiad prep if targeting NSEP/NSEC (usually October-November).
- **October-November:** National Olympiad exams. Apply for KVPY fellowship if still available.
- **January-March:** Board exam preparation. Score 85%+ (needed for IISER channel 3 and INSPIRE).
- **April:** IISER IAT registration opens at [iiseradmission.in](https://www.iiseradmission.in).
- **May-June:** NEST exam, IISER IAT exam.
- **June-July:** JEE Advanced (if also appearing). IISc Bangalore admission through JEE Advanced or KVPY.
- **July:** Counselling and seat allotment.

### For USA applicants:
- **August:** Common Application opens. Start essays. Science-focused supplements are key.
- **October:** Final SAT. Submit Early Action to MIT (November 1), Caltech (November 1).
- **January 1-5:** Regular Decision deadlines for most universities.
- **March-April:** Decisions arrive. Compare financial aid — MIT and Caltech are need-blind for internationals.

### For Germany applicants:
- **Complete German B2** by March.
- **Take TestAS** (April or October) at [testas.de](https://www.testas.de). Good TestAS scores significantly boost your application.
- **Apply through [uni-assist](https://www.uni-assist.de)** — deadline July 15 for winter semester.
- Cost: semester fees only (€150-350/semester). Budget €10,000-12,000/year for living expenses.

### For UK applicants:
- **September-October:** Submit UCAS application. Deadline: October 15 for Oxford/Cambridge, January 31 for others.
- **November-December:** Interviews for Oxbridge (conducted online for international students).
- **January-March:** Offers arrive (conditional on board scores).

## Smart Strategy: What the Best Students Do

1. **Science + JEE is a natural combo.** Preparing for JEE Advanced automatically prepares you for IISER IAT and NEST. The overlap is 80%+. Always apply to both.
2. **IISERs and IISc are India's hidden gems.** World-class research training, fully funded, smaller batch sizes (40-60 students), and excellent faculty. Most parents haven't heard of them — that's an advantage.
3. **The BS-MS dual degree at IISERs** takes 5 years but includes a full research thesis. Graduates are strongly preferred for PhD programs at MIT, Stanford, and European universities.
4. **Germany for science is extraordinary value.** Nearly free tuition, cutting-edge labs (Max Planck Institutes), and a clear path to PhD and permanent residency. But German language is non-negotiable for most programs.
5. **Research experience matters more than grades for PhD admissions.** If your child wants a research career, prioritize getting into a lab (even remotely) over scoring 2 extra marks in a test.
6. **Data Science is the pragmatic pivot.** A pure science degree (Physics/Math) + Python/R skills = Data Scientist (₹15-40 LPA). This is the "practical backup" parents worry about.
7. **Mental health matters.** Science careers are marathons — BS + PhD takes 9-10 years. Make sure your child genuinely loves the subject, not just the idea of it. A burned-out researcher helps nobody.
