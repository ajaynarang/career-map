## Why Bath Architecture?

The University of Bath consistently ranks among the UK's top 5 architecture schools and is renowned for one thing above all: **industry placements.** Bath pioneered the "thick sandwich" course structure, where architecture students spend a full paid year working in a professional practice between their second and third years. This is not an optional extra — it is built into the degree structure and is a defining feature of a Bath architecture education.

**For Indian families:** Bath offers a powerful combination that few UK schools can match: top-tier academic ranking, guaranteed professional experience through the placement year, and a campus set in one of England's most beautiful cities (a UNESCO World Heritage Site). Bath graduates are among the most employable in the UK because they arrive at their final year with real-world professional experience that firms value enormously.

**The placement advantage:** During the placement year, students work full-time at architecture firms across the UK and internationally, earning a salary (typically £18,000-22,000/year). They return to complete their final year with professional maturity, industry contacts, and a much clearer sense of their design direction. Many students receive job offers from their placement firms.

**The engineering connection:** Bath's architecture department sits within the Faculty of Engineering & Design alongside Civil Engineering and one of the world's top structural engineering groups (Buro Happold was founded by a Bath professor). This means architecture students have access to exceptional structural and environmental engineering expertise — producing graduates who understand how buildings actually stand up and perform.

## How to Get In

Bath architecture admits approximately 60-70 students per year:

1. **UCAS application:** Apply by January 31 through UCAS. Personal statement should demonstrate genuine interest in architecture, spatial awareness, and understanding of the built environment.
2. **Academic requirements:** A*AA at A-Level (or equivalent). Indian students should aim for 90%+ in Class 12 boards. Strong performance in Mathematics and/or Physics is valued given Bath's engineering-oriented approach.
3. **Portfolio:** Submit a digital portfolio of 10-20 pages. Include freehand drawings, model photographs, photography, paintings, and any design work. Bath values observation, craft, and evidence of spatial thinking.
4. **Interview:** Shortlisted candidates are invited for a portfolio-based interview (can be conducted online for international applicants). Be prepared to discuss your work, your interests in architecture, and why Bath specifically.

**Key advice for Indian applicants:** Bath values the technical-creative balance. If your child has a strong PCM background AND creative interests, Bath is an excellent fit. Mention any engineering, physics, or mathematics connections to architecture in the personal statement — this resonates with Bath's identity.

**Application timeline:**
- UCAS deadline: January 31
- Portfolio submission: February-March (after UCAS, upon invitation)
- Interviews: March-April
- Decisions: May

## Curriculum & Structure

4-year BSc Architecture with Placement (recommended route):

- **Year 1:** Design fundamentals — drawing, model-making, spatial design, architectural history, structures, and environmental design. Students develop core skills in visual communication and design thinking
- **Year 2:** Intermediate design studios with increasing complexity. Building technology, digital design tools (CAD, Revit, Rhino), sustainability, and advanced history/theory. Students prepare for the placement year with portfolio development and interview skills
- **Year 3 (Placement Year):** Full-time paid employment at an architecture firm. Students apply to practices across the UK and internationally. Bath's placement office provides extensive support and has relationships with 200+ firms. This year counts toward the RIBA Part 1 professional experience requirement
- **Year 4 (Final Year):** Advanced design studio, thesis project, and comprehensive design (a technically detailed project demonstrating construction knowledge). Students return from placement with real-world perspective that elevates their final-year work

**The Bath approach to architecture education:** Bath places greater emphasis on construction technology, structures, and environmental performance than many UK architecture schools. Graduates understand building physics, structural principles, and sustainability in depth — making them highly attractive to employers.

## Campus & City Life

Bath's campus overlooks one of England's most beautiful cities — a UNESCO World Heritage Site:

- **Bath is a living architecture textbook.** Georgian crescents (the Royal Crescent, The Circus), Roman baths, medieval abbey, and contemporary buildings by Grimshaw and Zaha Hadid coexist in a compact, walkable city. Architecture students sketch and study real buildings daily
- **The campus** is set on a hill overlooking the city, with architecture studios in the 4 West building offering views across Bath's rooftops. Fabrication workshops include laser cutters, 3D printers, and model-making facilities
- **Feilden Clegg Bradley Studios** (one of the UK's most respected sustainable architecture practices) and **Buro Happold** (global engineering consultancy) are both headquartered in Bath. Students have direct access to leading professionals
- **Compact, safe city** — Bath has approximately 90,000 residents and is one of the safest cities in the UK. It is an excellent environment for students living away from home for the first time
- **Indian community:** Bath has a growing Indian student population, with cultural societies at the university and Indian restaurants in the city center
- **Bristol is 15 minutes away** by train — a larger city with vibrant nightlife, cultural venues, and additional architecture firms
- **London is 90 minutes** by direct train — accessible for exhibitions, lectures, and firm visits
