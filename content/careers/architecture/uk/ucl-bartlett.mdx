## Why the Bartlett?

The Bartlett School of Architecture at UCL is the UK's #1 architecture school and consistently ranks in the global top 3. It is famous for its experimental, research-driven approach to architecture. The Bartlett doesn't just teach students to design buildings — it trains them to reimagine the built environment through technology, social engagement, and critical theory. Graduates include some of the world's most innovative architects and designers.

What makes the Bartlett unique: **its emphasis on design research and experimentation.** While many architecture schools teach conventional design (site analysis, building codes, standard construction), the Bartlett pushes students to question fundamental assumptions about what architecture can be. Students explore robotic fabrication, interactive environments, bio-materials, virtual reality, and social design — alongside traditional studio projects.

**For Indian families:** The Bartlett's global reputation means its degree is recognized and respected worldwide. Whether your child returns to India, stays in the UK, or works elsewhere, the Bartlett name opens doors.

## How to Get In

The Bartlett admits approximately 250 BSc Architecture students per year from over 2,500 applications (~10-12% acceptance rate):

1. **UCAS application:** Apply by January 31. Personal statement should demonstrate genuine passion for architecture, spatial thinking, and intellectual curiosity
2. **Portfolio shortlisting:** After UCAS submission, shortlisted candidates are invited to submit a portfolio and attend an interview
3. **Portfolio requirements:** 15-30 pages demonstrating creative thinking, observation, experimentation, and spatial awareness. Include drawings, paintings, photography, model photos, and any design/craft work. The Bartlett values ideas and process over technical polish
4. **Interview:** A 15-20 minute conversation about your portfolio, your interests in architecture, and what excites you about design. Be genuine — the Bartlett looks for curious, passionate individuals

**Key advice for Indian applicants:** The Bartlett is not looking for students who can draw realistic buildings. They want students who are curious about the world — who notice how light changes a space, who question why cities are designed a certain way, who experiment with materials and forms. If your child has these instincts, help them express that in the portfolio.

## Curriculum & Structure

3-year BSc Architecture (RIBA Part 1):

- **Year 1 (Foundation):** Introduction to architectural design through studio projects, architectural history, and technical studies. Students learn to draw, model, and think spatially
- **Year 2 (Development):** Students join one of the Bartlett's famous "Design Units" — small groups led by a practicing architect or researcher, each with a specific design theme or approach. This unit system is the Bartlett's signature — it means your child's education is shaped by active practitioners, not just academics
- **Year 3 (Thesis):** Advanced studio project and a written dissertation. Students develop a comprehensive design proposal that demonstrates their design philosophy
- **Architectural technology and environmental design** courses run alongside studios, covering structures, materials, sustainability, and environmental performance
- **History and Theory of Architecture** — extensive study of architectural ideas from classical to contemporary
- **The Bartlett's "B-Made" workshop** provides access to laser cutters, 3D printers, CNC machines, robotic arms, and a full materials lab for physical model-making and prototyping

## Campus Life

UCL is located in Bloomsbury, central London — one of the most culturally rich locations for any architecture student:

- **London is the architecture student's ultimate classroom.** The Soane Museum, RIBA headquarters, the Serpentine Pavilion, the Tate Modern (a converted power station), and countless contemporary projects are all within reach
- **The Bartlett building on Gordon Street** houses studios, workshops, and exhibition spaces. It's a compact but intense environment where students from all years interact
- **London's architecture offices are accessible.** Many students work part-time at London firms during their studies and use these connections for their Year Out (the practice year between Part 1 and Part 2)
- **Indian community at UCL** is large and active, with dedicated societies and regular cultural events
- **London living costs are high.** Budget ₹15-18L/year for accommodation, food, and transport. UCL halls of residence for first-year students cost £8,000-13,000/year; private accommodation thereafter is more expensive
- **The Bartlett Summer Show** is one of London's most anticipated architecture exhibitions — graduates display their best work, and top firms attend to scout talent
