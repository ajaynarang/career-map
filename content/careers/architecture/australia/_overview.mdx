## Why Australia for Architecture?

Australia is an increasingly attractive destination for architecture students, offering a unique combination of quality education, pleasant climate, and clear immigration pathways. Australian architecture itself is globally recognized for its innovative response to extreme environments — bushfire-resistant design, passive cooling, and sustainable building are deeply integrated into the curriculum. Firms like Cox Architecture, BVN, and Hassell are internationally respected.

**The financial picture:** A 3-year Bachelor of Design (Architecture) costs ₹95L-1.2 Crore total. The full path to becoming a registered architect requires a 2-year Master of Architecture afterward (total 5 years, similar to India's B.Arch). Australian wages are high — even part-time work during studies (48 hours per fortnight at ~AUD 24/hour minimum wage) provides meaningful income.

## The Path

1. **Class 11-12:** Focus on board exams (aim for 80%+). Prepare IELTS (target 6.5+). Build a portfolio if possible (some schools require it, others don't)
2. **Research universities and apply directly** through each university's online portal. Most accept applications year-round for February or July intake
3. **Receive conditional offers** based on Class 12 predicted/actual results. Accept and pay deposit
4. **Apply for Student Visa (subclass 500)** with CoE, IELTS, financial proof, and OSHC (health insurance)
5. **February or July:** Start university. Bachelor is 3 years, followed by 2-year Master

## What Parents Should Know

- **February is the main intake.** Unlike the US/UK/Canada (September start), Australian universities primarily begin in February. July intake is also available at most schools.
- **3+2 model:** Australian architecture education follows a 3-year Bachelor + 2-year Master structure. The Bachelor (e.g., Bachelor of Design in Architecture) provides foundational design skills. The Master of Architecture is required for professional registration. Plan for 5 years total.
- **Portfolio requirements vary.** University of Melbourne does not require a portfolio for undergraduate admission; UNSW recommends one. Check each school carefully.
- **Sustainability is core.** Australian architecture schools integrate sustainable design, climate-responsive building, and environmental performance into the curriculum from day one. This is a real advantage — green architecture skills are in demand globally.
- **Work rights are generous.** Students can work 48 hours per fortnight during semester and unlimited hours during breaks. At Australian minimum wage (~AUD 24/hour), this provides significant income to offset costs.
- **Post-study visa + skilled migration.** After completing a degree, graduates get a 2-4 year post-study work visa. Architecture is on Australia's Skills Priority List, providing a pathway to Permanent Residency for those who gain work experience.
- **Climate is a major plus.** Melbourne, Sydney, and Brisbane have mild to warm climates year-round. This is a significant quality-of-life advantage over Canada, the UK, or Germany.
- **Indian community is large.** Australia has one of the biggest Indian diaspora populations globally. Melbourne and Sydney have huge Indian communities with temples, restaurants, and cultural festivals.
