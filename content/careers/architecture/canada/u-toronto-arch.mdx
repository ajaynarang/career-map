## Why U of T Daniels?

The Daniels Faculty at the University of Toronto is Canada's top-ranked architecture school and one of the most comprehensive design faculties in North America. Located in downtown Toronto — Canada's largest, most diverse, and most architecturally active city — the Daniels Faculty offers students immersion in a rapidly growing urban environment. Toronto is experiencing one of the biggest construction booms in North America, with more high-rise projects under construction than any city except for Chinese megacities.

What makes U of T Daniels attractive for Indian families: **no portfolio required for undergraduate admission.** Unlike most architecture schools worldwide, U of T admits to the BA in Architectural Studies based on academic merit and a supplemental application (short essays). This is ideal for students who know they're interested in architecture but haven't had the opportunity to build a formal portfolio.

## How to Get In

U of T uses the Ontario Universities' Application Centre (OUAC) for applications:

1. **Apply through OUAC** (105F application for international students) by January 15 for September entry
2. **Academic requirements:** Class 12 with strong marks, particularly in Math. Mid-80s to low-90s percentage is typically competitive
3. **Supplemental application:** Short essay responses about your interest in architecture, design thinking, and creative experiences. No portfolio required
4. **IELTS:** 6.5 overall with minimum 6.0 in each component
5. **Conditional offer:** U of T sends offers conditional on final Class 12 results

**Strategic advantage:** Because no portfolio is required, students who are strong academically but haven't had formal art/design training can still enter architecture. Once enrolled, the program builds design skills from the ground up.

## Curriculum & Structure

4-year Honours BA in Architectural Studies (pre-professional), followed by a 3.5-year M.Arch:

- **Year 1:** Introduction to architecture through design studios, visual studies, history, and technical drawing. Students learn to observe, represent, and think about space
- **Year 2:** Intermediate design studios with increasing complexity. Building technology and structural systems courses begin
- **Year 3:** Advanced studios exploring different building types and scales. Electives allow specialization in areas like landscape, urbanism, or digital design
- **Year 4:** Thesis research and advanced studio. Students develop independent design positions
- **Daniels Building (One Spadina Crescent):** The faculty is housed in a beautifully renovated heritage building with state-of-the-art studios, fabrication labs (laser cutters, 3D printers, CNC routers), and exhibition spaces
- **The BA is pre-professional.** To become a licensed architect in Canada, your child must continue to the M.Arch (3.5 additional years at U of T or elsewhere). The BA alone qualifies for roles in design firms, real estate development, urban planning, and related fields — but not independent architectural practice
- **M.Arch admission is not guaranteed.** Daniels M.Arch is competitive, but BA graduates have a strong advantage. Many students also apply to M.Arch programs at Harvard, MIT, or Columbia after their U of T BA

## Campus Life

U of T's campus is in downtown Toronto, steps from the city's most dynamic neighborhoods:

- **Toronto is Canada's architecture hub.** Students are surrounded by contemporary projects (the ROM Crystal by Daniel Libeskind, the AGO expansion by Frank Gehry, the TIFF Bell Lightbox) alongside heritage buildings and a booming condo skyline
- **Daniels Faculty** has a strong community culture — small studio groups, collaborative projects, and active student societies. The Daniels Student Union organizes lectures, exhibitions, and social events
- **Diversity is a strength.** U of T is one of the most diverse universities in the world, and the Daniels Faculty reflects this. Indian students are well-represented
- **Toronto is expensive.** Living costs are CAD 15,000-20,000/year for shared accommodation and basic living. Student housing is available for first-year students but limited
- **Co-op and internships:** While U of T doesn't have a formal co-op program like Waterloo, students can take Professional Experience Year (PEY) internships at architecture firms in Toronto
- **Cultural life** is rich — Toronto has world-class museums, galleries, theatre, and one of the best food scenes in North America. The Indian community in the Greater Toronto Area is one of the largest outside India
