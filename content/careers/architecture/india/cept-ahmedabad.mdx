## Why CEPT Ahmedabad?

CEPT (Centre for Environmental Planning and Technology) is widely regarded as India's finest architecture school — the equivalent of what IIT Bombay is for engineering. Founded in 1962 by visionary architect Balkrishna Doshi (who later won the Pritzker Prize, architecture's Nobel equivalent), CEPT has a legacy that no other Indian architecture school can match. Its alumni dominate India's architectural landscape, and its design pedagogy is respected globally.

What makes CEPT extraordinary: **design culture.** While IIT KGP and SPA Delhi are excellent, CEPT is an architecture-first institution. Every student, professor, and building on campus breathes design. The campus itself — designed by Doshi — is an architectural masterpiece and a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Students don't just study architecture; they live it.

## How to Get In

CEPT's admission process is unique and evaluates design aptitude, not just exam scores:

1. **NATA score** is the primary academic requirement — score well in NATA (aim for 120+/200)
2. **CEPT SDA (Situational Design Aptitude test)** — a unique test designed by CEPT that evaluates spatial thinking, observation, and design problem-solving. This is not a conventional exam; it tests how you think, not what you memorize
3. **Portfolio review** — submit a portfolio of sketches, photographs, models, and any creative work
4. **Interview** — a conversation about your design interests, observations, and motivation

**Key insight for parents:** CEPT values original thinking over coached answers. A student who has spent time observing buildings, sketching spaces, and thinking about design — even without formal coaching — can outperform a heavily coached student. Encourage genuine curiosity, not rote preparation.

**Seats:** Approximately 120 seats for B.Arch. The process is competitive but less cutthroat than JEE.

## Curriculum & Structure

5-year B.Arch with CEPT's signature design-centered pedagogy:

- **Design Studio is everything.** At CEPT, studio runs continuously — it's not just one subject, it's the organizing principle of the curriculum. Students design, build models, get critiqued, redesign, and present — over and over
- **Foundation year** focuses on basic design, visual arts, and understanding materials. Students work with wood, metal, clay, and textiles before touching AutoCAD
- **Strong emphasis on craft and making** — students build full-scale installations, not just paper drawings
- **Ahmedabad's built environment is the classroom** — students study Le Corbusier's buildings (the city has his only works in India), Doshi's architecture, and the historic old city's remarkable urban fabric
- **Exchange programs** with top international schools — AA London, ETH Zurich, Bartlett UCL
- **Thesis project** in year 5 involves original research and a comprehensive design proposal

## Campus Life

CEPT's campus in Navrangpura, Ahmedabad is itself a design education:

- **The campus is a UNESCO World Heritage Site** — designed by BV Doshi, it demonstrates principles of open architecture, natural ventilation, and flexible spaces. Students literally study inside a masterpiece
- **Small, focused community** — CEPT is a specialized university (only architecture, planning, design, and construction technology). Everyone shares the same passion
- **Ahmedabad is India's architecture capital** — Le Corbusier, Louis Kahn, Doshi, and Charles Correa all built here. No other Indian city has this concentration of world-class architecture
- **Strong studio culture** — expect to work long hours, but surrounded by passionate peers. All-night charrettes before jury days are common
- **Affordable city** — Ahmedabad is significantly cheaper than Mumbai or Delhi for food and accommodation
- **Annual events:** CEPT hosts exhibitions, lectures by visiting architects, and design workshops throughout the year
