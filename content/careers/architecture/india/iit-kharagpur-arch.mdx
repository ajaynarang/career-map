## Why IIT Kharagpur for Architecture?

IIT Kharagpur is one of only two IITs offering a B.Arch program (the other being IIT Roorkee), and it's the premier choice for students who want an architecture degree backed by the IIT brand. The Department of Architecture & Regional Planning here combines rigorous design studio culture with the technical depth that IITs are known for — structural analysis, building science, and computational design are taught alongside architectural theory and history.

What sets IIT KGP apart: **the IIT ecosystem.** Architecture students share the campus with engineering, science, and management students. They access the same world-class labs, computing facilities, and interdisciplinary culture. This cross-pollination produces architects who think like engineers and designers simultaneously — a rare and valuable combination in the industry.

## How to Get In

The only path is **JEE Advanced** — making this one of the hardest architecture seats to secure in India.

1. Clear JEE Main (be in the top 2.5 lakh qualifiers)
2. Appear for JEE Advanced
3. Choose B.Arch or Dual Degree (B.Arch + M.Arch) during JoSAA counselling

**Reality check:** There are approximately 50-60 B.Arch seats at IIT Kharagpur. Closing ranks typically fall in the 3000-6000 range for General category. This is competitive, but significantly more accessible than IIT Bombay CS. Many students with JEE Advanced ranks of 4000-5000 who might not get their preferred engineering branch at a top IIT choose IIT KGP Architecture — and thrive.

**Important:** Unlike NATA-based colleges, IIT KGP does not have a drawing test in the admission process. Selection is purely based on JEE Advanced rank. However, students are expected to develop drawing and design skills rapidly once they join.

## Curriculum & Structure

5-year B.Arch with a strong emphasis on design studios from the first year:

- **Design Studio** runs every semester — this is the heart of architectural education. Students design buildings, urban spaces, and communities with increasing complexity each year
- **Technical subjects:** Structural systems, building construction, environmental science, building services (HVAC, plumbing, electrical)
- **Humanities and theory:** History of architecture, architectural theory, urban planning
- **Dual Degree option:** 6-year B.Arch + M.Arch with specialization in Urban Design, Landscape Architecture, or Architectural Conservation
- **Thesis project** in the final year — a comprehensive design project that many students use as a portfolio centerpiece
- **Internship** is mandatory — typically 4-6 weeks at an architecture firm during summer breaks

## Campus Life

IIT Kharagpur has the largest campus among all IITs — 2,100 acres of self-contained campus life:

- **Architecture students are known for pulling all-nighters** in the design studio (called "charrettes") — it's a rite of passage
- **Spring Fest** and **Kshitij** — major cultural and tech festivals
- **Strong alumni network** in architecture and urban planning across India
- **Campus location is remote** — Kharagpur is a small town in West Bengal. This means fewer distractions and strong campus bonding, but limited city life
- **Hostel life** is integral — students from all departments live together
