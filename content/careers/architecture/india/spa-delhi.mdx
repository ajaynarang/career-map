## Why SPA Delhi?

SPA Delhi is India's #1-ranked architecture institution — consistently topping NIRF rankings. Established in 1941, it is one of only three Schools of Planning and Architecture in India (the others being in Bhopal and Vijayawada), and the Delhi campus is undisputedly the most prestigious. SPA Delhi has shaped India's architectural landscape for over 80 years — its alumni include some of the country's most celebrated architects and urban planners.

What makes SPA Delhi special: **location and network.** Being in New Delhi, students have direct exposure to India's most significant architectural heritage (Lutyens' Delhi, Mughal architecture, modern government buildings) and access to the country's top architecture firms, many of which are headquartered in Delhi-NCR. The alumni network is unmatched in Indian architecture.

## How to Get In

Entry is through **JEE Main Paper 2 (B.Arch):**

1. Register for JEE Main and select Paper 2 (B.Arch) — this is separate from Paper 1 (B.Tech)
2. Paper 2 tests: Mathematics (Part I), Aptitude Test (Part II), and Drawing Test (Part III)
3. Based on your JEE Main Paper 2 rank, choose SPA Delhi during JoSAA counselling

**Cutoffs:** SPA Delhi is extremely competitive. General category closing ranks are typically in the top 200-500 of JEE Main Paper 2. OBC/SC/ST cutoffs are more accessible. There are approximately 80-90 B.Arch seats.

**Key difference from engineering JEE:** Paper 2 has a Drawing Test component worth 100 marks. Students must produce freehand drawings of scenes, objects, and 3D compositions within a time limit. This cannot be cracked by coaching alone — genuine drawing practice over months is essential.

## Curriculum & Structure

5-year B.Arch following the Council of Architecture's prescribed curriculum, with SPA Delhi's own rigorous additions:

- **Design Studio** is the core — every semester, students tackle increasingly complex design problems, from residential buildings to institutional campuses to urban neighborhoods
- **Architectural drawing and visual arts** in the first two years build foundational skills
- **Building construction and structures** provide the technical backbone
- **History of architecture** covers Indian, Islamic, Western, and contemporary architecture
- **Elective specializations** in the later years: Urban Design, Landscape Architecture, Building Engineering, Architectural Conservation
- **Mandatory internship** with a practicing architect — typically after 3rd or 4th year
- **Thesis project** in 5th year — many students use this to define their career direction

## Campus Life

SPA Delhi's campus is compact but centrally located in Indraprastha Estate, near ITO in central Delhi:

- **Small, intimate batches** — 80-90 students per year means everyone knows everyone. Professors give personal attention
- **Studio culture is intense** — expect late nights, group critiques (called "juries"), and constant iteration. Architecture students work harder than most engineering students, but the work is creative and fulfilling
- **Delhi's architecture scene is at your doorstep** — students regularly visit construction sites, heritage buildings, and architecture firms as part of coursework
- **Zonasa** — SPA Delhi's annual festival combines architecture exhibitions, competitions, and cultural events
- **Housing:** Limited hostel seats (SPA Delhi's campus is small). Many students rent PGs or flats in nearby areas. Budget ₹8,000-15,000/month for shared accommodation in Delhi
