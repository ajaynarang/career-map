## Why India for Architecture?

India is experiencing an unprecedented construction and urbanization boom. Over the next two decades, Indian cities will add infrastructure equivalent to what the US built over 100 years. Smart cities, green buildings, metro systems, and affordable housing are creating massive demand for trained architects. If your child loves design and has the patience for a 5-year degree, architecture in India offers a meaningful and increasingly well-paid career.

**The numbers:** Starting salaries are modest (₹3-6 LPA), but architects with 8-10 years of experience earn ₹12-25 LPA. Those who start their own practice or specialize in green/sustainable architecture can earn significantly more. The real draw is independence — architects can practice on their own, unlike most engineers who need employers.

## The Path

1. **Class 11-12:** PCM with strong focus on Math. Start drawing practice and NATA coaching
2. **After 12th boards:** NATA (April-June, up to 3 attempts) and/or JEE Main Paper 2 (January + April)
3. **JEE Advanced:** For IIT Kharagpur and IIT Roorkee architecture seats only
4. **Counselling:** JoSAA (IITs, NITs, SPAs) + separate counselling for CEPT and state colleges
5. **B.Arch:** 5-year degree including design studios, structural engineering, history of architecture, and a thesis project

## What Parents Should Know

- **B.Arch is 5 years, not 4.** Your child will graduate at 23, compared to 22 for engineering. This extra year includes intensive design studio work and a thesis project. The Council of Architecture mandates this duration — there are no shortcuts.
- **Two entry exams to understand:** NATA tests drawing, aesthetic sensitivity, and math aptitude. JEE Main Paper 2 tests math, aptitude, and drawing. Your child can appear for both. NATA is better for dedicated architecture aspirants; JEE Main Paper 2 is for those also keeping JEE Advanced options open.
- **Drawing skill is non-negotiable.** Unlike engineering, architecture entrance exams test freehand drawing and visual composition. Coaching helps, but your child needs genuine interest in sketching. A student who hates drawing will struggle in 5 years of design studios.
- **Portfolio matters for top colleges.** CEPT Ahmedabad and some other top colleges evaluate portfolios and conduct interviews. Start building a portfolio of sketches, model photos, and design observations from Class 9-10.
- **Licensure is straightforward.** After completing B.Arch from a Council of Architecture-recognized college, graduates register with CoA and can practice independently. No additional licensing exam like the US.
- **The profession is changing.** BIM (Building Information Modeling), parametric design, and sustainable architecture are reshaping the field. Students who combine design skills with software proficiency (Revit, Rhino, Grasshopper) earn significantly more.
