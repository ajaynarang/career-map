## Class 9-10: Building the Foundation

Architecture is where art meets engineering. Your child needs both creative skills and strong math/physics. This is the time to start developing the creative side.

### For all paths:
- **Start sketching daily** — freehand drawing is tested in NATA and most architecture entrance exams. Even 20 minutes a day builds the skill.
- **Learn to observe spaces** — visit buildings, notice how light enters rooms, how spaces make you feel. Architecture schools value students who think about the built environment.
- **Master Math and Physics** — structures, materials, and spatial calculations require strong fundamentals.
- **Build models** — use cardboard, clay, or 3D modeling software (SketchUp is free). This builds spatial thinking.
- **Maintain 85%+ in school** — mandatory for international admissions and helpful for NATA/JEE.
- **Start a portfolio** — collect your sketches, model photos, and design observations. You'll need this for international applications.

### If targeting India:
- Understand the two main exams: **NATA** (conducted by Council of Architecture) and **JEE Main Paper 2** (for IITs and NITs).
- Join a **NATA coaching class** in Class 10 summer — drawing and aptitude skills need structured practice.

### If targeting USA:
- Build an **art/design portfolio** — US architecture schools (Cornell, Rice, MIT, Cooper Union) require a portfolio of 15-20 pieces.
- Start SAT prep in summer after Class 10.

### If targeting Germany:
- **START GERMAN NOW.** Most architecture programs are in German. Reach A2 by end of Class 10 at [Goethe-Institut](https://www.goethe.de/ins/in/en/index.html).
- German architecture schools (TU Munich, TU Berlin, Bauhaus University Weimar) charge nearly zero tuition.

## Class 11: The Critical Year

### For India (NATA/JEE):
- **Join NATA coaching seriously.** NATA tests: drawing, aesthetic sensitivity, mathematical aptitude, and general awareness.
- **Practice drawing 1 hour daily** — perspective drawing, 3D composition, and architectural sketching.
- If appearing for **JEE Main Paper 2** (B.Arch): This covers Math (Part I), Aptitude (Part II), and Drawing (Part III).
- Research colleges: **IIT Kharagpur, IIT Roorkee** (through JEE Advanced), **SPA Delhi, SPA Bhopal** (through JEE Main Paper 2), **CEPT Ahmedabad** (through NATA).

### For USA:
- **Take the SAT** — aim for 1400+.
- **Build your portfolio** — include drawings, paintings, photography, 3D models, and any design projects. Quality over quantity.
- Research: Cornell AAP, Rice Architecture, Cooper Union (free tuition!), MIT, USC, Pratt Institute.
- Some schools (MIT, UC Berkeley) don't require a portfolio for undergrad — research carefully.

### For Germany:
- **Reach German B1.** Take Goethe B1 exam.
- Research portfolio requirements — German architecture schools often require a Mappe (portfolio) with 15-25 works.

### For UK:
- Research **UCAS process.** Architecture is a 3-year BA + 2-year Master's in the UK (Part 1 + Part 2 system).
- Top schools: **Bartlett (UCL), AA School, Cambridge, Bath, Sheffield.**
- Prepare your **portfolio** — UK schools emphasize conceptual thinking and process, not just finished drawings.
- UCAS deadline: January 31 for most architecture programs.

### For Canada & Australia:
- Focus on **grades** and **IELTS** (target 7.0+).
- Build a **portfolio** — University of Toronto, UBC (Canada), University of Melbourne, UNSW (Australia) all require or strongly recommend portfolios.

## Class 12: Execution

### Month-by-month (India path):
- **July-September:** Intensify NATA drawing practice. Complete JEE Main Paper 2 math syllabus.
- **October-December:** Mock NATA tests. Practice timed drawings (you get limited time per drawing in the actual exam).
- **January:** JEE Main Paper 2 (Session 1). NATA registration opens at [nata.in](https://www.nata.in).
- **February-March:** Board exams. **Score 50%+ in PCM** (minimum eligibility for B.Arch in India).
- **April:** NATA exam (first attempt). JEE Main Paper 2 (Session 2).
- **May-June:** NATA second/third attempts if needed. JEE Advanced for IIT architecture.
- **June-July:** JoSAA counselling (IITs, NITs, SPAs). Separate counselling for CEPT and state-level colleges.

### For USA applicants:
- **August:** Common Application opens. Portfolio submission deadlines vary — check each school.
- **November 1:** Early Decision deadlines (Cornell ED is strategic).
- **January 1-5:** Regular Decision deadlines. Portfolio due simultaneously.
- **March-April:** Decisions arrive. Cooper Union is free but extremely selective (~5% acceptance).

### For Germany:
- **Complete German B2** by March.
- **Prepare your Mappe (portfolio)** — 15-25 works showing creativity, spatial thinking, and technical skill.
- **Apply through [uni-assist](https://www.uni-assist.de)** — deadline July 15 for winter semester.
- Some schools require a **Mappengespräch** (portfolio interview) — prepare to explain your work in German or English.

### For UK:
- **January 31:** UCAS deadline for architecture programs.
- **February-April:** Portfolio reviews and interviews.
- **Cost: £20,000-30,000/year tuition** plus £12,000-15,000/year living expenses.

## Smart Strategy: What the Best Students Do

1. **B.Arch is a 5-year degree in India.** Plan accordingly. Your child will be 23 when they graduate, compared to 22 for a B.Tech. But architects can practice independently — engineers usually need an employer.
2. **CEPT Ahmedabad is the IIT of architecture.** Smaller class sizes, excellent faculty, strong industry connections. Fees are very reasonable (under ₹3 lakh/year).
3. **SPA Delhi has the best placement record** among government architecture colleges. Entry through JEE Main Paper 2.
4. **Cooper Union in New York offers FREE TUITION** for all admitted students. It's extremely competitive but worth applying — your child pays only living costs.
5. **Germany is the best value abroad.** Bauhaus University Weimar (where modern architecture was born!) charges under €500/semester. Total cost for 3 years: under ₹25 lakh including living expenses.
6. **Architecture + Tech is the future.** Parametric design, BIM (Building Information Modeling), and computational design are reshaping the field. Students who can code + design earn significantly more.
7. **Honest reality check:** Architecture starting salaries in India are modest (₹3-6 LPA). It takes 8-10 years to earn well. Students who love design and are patient thrive. Those seeking quick returns may be disappointed. Discuss this openly with your child.
8. **Freelancing is common.** Unlike most careers, architects can start independent practice early. Many successful architects run their own firms by age 30-35.
