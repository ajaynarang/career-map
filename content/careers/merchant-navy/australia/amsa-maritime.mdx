## Why AMC?

The Australian Maritime College (AMC), established in 1978, is Australia's national center for maritime education, training, and research. As part of the University of Tasmania, AMC operates at the intersection of academic rigor and practical maritime training. It is one of the most advanced maritime training facilities in the Southern Hemisphere, with ship bridge simulators, engine room simulators, a hydrodynamics test facility, and a fire-fighting training ground.

**For Indian families considering a global maritime career:** AMC offers something that Indian and UK maritime colleges don't — a pathway to Australian Permanent Residency. Tasmania is classified as a regional area, which gives graduates additional points in Australia's skilled migration program and extended post-study work rights (3-4 years instead of 2). For families who want their child to have the option of settling abroad permanently, AMC is worth the higher cost.

**The salary reality in Australia:** Australian maritime wages are among the highest in the world. A newly qualified officer earns AUD 70,000-100,000/year. Experienced officers on offshore vessels (oil and gas) earn AUD 120,000-200,000+. These are genuinely life-changing numbers.

## How to Get In

AMC admission is academic-record-based (no entrance exam):

1. **Online Application:** Apply through the University of Tasmania international student portal at [utas.edu.au/international/apply](https://www.utas.edu.au/international/apply).
2. **Academic requirements:** Indian Class 12 boards with 65%+ in PCM. Mathematics and Physics marks are weighted.
3. **English requirement:** IELTS 6.0 overall (no band below 5.5) or TOEFL 72.
4. **Maritime medical:** Complete an AMSA-approved maritime medical examination. This can be done in India before departing (check for AMSA-recognized medical examiners in India) or upon arrival in Australia. Requirements: eyesight correctable to 6/6 (for deck), color vision, hearing, physical fitness.
5. **Visa:** Apply for Student Visa (subclass 500) once you receive your CoE (Confirmation of Enrolment).

**Application timeline:**
- Semester 1 (February start) — main intake: Apply by October-November
- Semester 2 (July start) — limited availability: Apply by April-May

**Pathway option:** If your child doesn't meet direct entry requirements, AMC offers a Foundation Studies program that leads into the first year of the Bachelor's degree.

## Curriculum & Structure

**Bachelor of Applied Science — Maritime Technology, Nautical Science stream (3 years):**
- Navigation (traditional and electronic), chartwork, passage planning
- Ship handling and manoeuvring
- Cargo handling operations (containers, bulk, tankers, LNG)
- Maritime law, maritime safety, environmental regulations
- Meteorology and oceanography
- Sea time: mandatory supervised sea time on operational vessels (typically 12+ months across the degree)

**Bachelor of Applied Science — Maritime Technology, Marine Engineering stream (4 years):**
- Marine diesel engines, gas turbines, steam systems
- Electrical systems, automation, control systems
- Naval architecture, ship stability, marine surveying
- Workshop practice, maintenance planning
- Sea time: mandatory supervised engine room training on operational vessels

**World-class facilities:**
- **Integrated Marine Simulator Centre** — one of the most advanced ship simulation facilities in the world. Bridge, engine room, and VTS (vessel traffic service) simulators that replicate real-world conditions
- **Survival Centre** — sea survival training, fire-fighting, helicopter underwater escape training (HUET)
- **Hydrodynamics test facility** — a 100-meter towing tank for ship model testing (used by naval architects and researchers)
- **Workshop and engine lab** — real marine engines, electrical equipment, and control systems for hands-on training

## Life at AMC / Launceston

AMC is located in Launceston (also known by its Aboriginal name Kanamaluka) — Tasmania's second-largest city, population ~68,000. It's a small, safe, and affordable city.

- **Affordable living** — Launceston is one of the most affordable places to live in Australia. Shared apartment: AUD 150-200/week. Groceries and daily expenses are cheaper than Melbourne or Sydney
- **Beautiful natural environment** — Tasmania is famous for its wilderness, national parks, and clean air. Weekend trips to Cradle Mountain, Freycinet National Park, and Bay of Fires are popular student activities
- **Small but supportive community** — AMC is a niche institution with small cohorts. Students know each other and faculty well. The maritime community is tight-knit
- **Work opportunities** — while Launceston is small, part-time work is available in hospitality, retail, and seasonal agriculture (fruit picking pays well in Tasmania). The 48 hrs/fortnight work permission helps offset costs
- **Indian community is growing** — University of Tasmania has an increasing number of Indian students. There are Indian grocery stores and restaurants in Launceston, and the university's international student services are supportive
- **Regional benefits for immigration** — studying in Tasmania gives significant advantages for Australian PR applications: additional points under the skilled migration program and access to state-nominated visa pathways
