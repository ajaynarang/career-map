## Why IMU Mumbai?

Indian Maritime University (IMU) is India's only central university dedicated to maritime education, established by an Act of Parliament in 2008. The Mumbai campus — one of IMU's six campuses — benefits from being in India's shipping capital. Mumbai houses the Directorate General of Shipping, most major shipping companies' India offices, and the country's busiest port complex.

**For parents weighing options:** IMU is the safest choice for merchant navy education. It's government-run, affordable (₹10-14L total for the full course), and has near-100% placement. Every major shipping company in the world recruits from IMU. The alternative — private maritime academies — costs 2-3x more with no better placement outcomes.

**The career trajectory is compelling:** Your child starts earning ₹6-10 LPA immediately after completing the course and sea time. Within 5-7 years, this grows to ₹20-30 LPA. By age 35-40, a Captain or Chief Engineer earns ₹60 LPA-1.2 Crore — all tax-free if they spend 182+ days at sea.

## How to Get In

The only route to IMU is through **IMU CET**.

1. **Registration (October-November):** Apply online at [imu.edu.in](https://www.imu.edu.in). Registration fee: approximately ₹1,000-1,500.
2. **IMU CET Exam (May-June):** Computer-based test covering Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, English, and General Aptitude. The exam is similar in difficulty to JEE Main (but with a broader syllabus including English and GK).
3. **Board exam results (June):** Minimum 60% in PCM required. IMU CET score + board marks determine final counselling rank.
4. **Counselling (June-July):** Choose campus (Mumbai, Chennai, Kolkata, Visakhapatnam, Cochin, Navi Mumbai) and program (B.Tech Marine Engineering or B.Sc Nautical Science).
5. **Medical fitness test:** Strict DGMS medical before joining. Includes: eyesight (6/6 without glasses for Nautical Science), color vision, hearing, physical fitness, blood tests, chest X-ray.

**Reality check:** IMU CET is competitive. About 30,000-40,000 students take the exam for roughly 3,000-4,000 seats across all campuses. Mumbai campus is the most competitive.

**Important medical requirements:**
- **Deck (Nautical Science):** 6/6 vision in each eye without glasses/contacts. Normal color vision. No squint.
- **Engine (Marine Engineering):** Vision correctable to 6/12 acceptable. Color vision must be normal.
- **General:** BMI within normal range. No chronic conditions. Swimming ability mandatory.

## Curriculum & Structure

**B.Tech Marine Engineering (4 years):**
- Covers marine diesel engines, steam turbines, refrigeration, electrical systems, automation, naval architecture
- Includes 6 months of onboard training (cadetship) — your child serves as a trainee on a real commercial ship
- After completion: 6 months of sea time → competency exam → 5th Engineer license from DGMS

**B.Sc Nautical Science (3 years):**
- Covers navigation, cargo handling, maritime law, meteorology, ship stability, seamanship
- Includes 18 months of onboard training (cadetship) — longer sea time is mandatory for deck officers
- After completion: competency exam → 3rd Officer (2nd Mate) license from DGMS

**Both programs include:**
- **Practical training** — fire fighting, sea survival, first aid, lifeboat handling (STCW certification)
- **English communication** — essential because English is the official language at sea worldwide
- **Physical training** — regular PT sessions, swimming, and drills

## Life at IMU Mumbai

IMU Mumbai operates as a residential institution — all students live on campus in hostel-style accommodation.

- **Disciplined environment** — IMU follows a semi-regimented schedule. Regular timings, physical training, and professional conduct are expected. This prepares students for the structured life on ships.
- **Uniform is mandatory** — students wear maritime uniforms on campus. This is standard in maritime education globally.
- **Mumbai advantage** — proximity to Directorate General of Shipping, shipping companies, and the port gives students exposure to the industry early.
- **Alumni network** — IMU alumni serve as officers across the global merchant fleet. The network is strong and supportive.
- **Placement cell** — shipping companies visit campus for recruitment. Major recruiters include Anglo-Eastern, Fleet Management, Synergy, MSC, and Maersk.
