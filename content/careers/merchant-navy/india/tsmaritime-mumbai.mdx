## Why TS Chanakya / MERI Mumbai?

TS Chanakya (Training Ship Chanakya) is the most prestigious maritime training institution in India — the "IIT of the Merchant Navy." Run directly by the Directorate General of Shipping (Government of India), it has been training merchant navy officers since 1927. The institution is now part of the MERI (Marine Engineering and Research Institute) network and works in conjunction with IMU.

**For parents:** If your child can get into TS Chanakya, there is no better place to start a merchant navy career. The institution's reputation is unmatched — every shipping company in the world knows and respects TS Chanakya graduates. The cost is minimal (under ₹10L total), placement is virtually guaranteed, and the training quality is world-class.

**The prestige factor:** TS Chanakya alumni hold senior positions across the global maritime industry — as Captains, Chief Engineers, and shore-based executives at major shipping companies. The alumni network is powerful and protective. A TS Chanakya graduate is given preference by many shipping companies.

## How to Get In

TS Chanakya admission is through **IMU CET** or a separate entrance process (check the current year's process at DG Shipping website).

1. **IMU CET** — top rankers in the exam have first preference for TS Chanakya seats during counselling.
2. **Medical fitness** — strict DGMS medical examination. The same eyesight and fitness requirements as other maritime institutions.
3. **Physical fitness test** — swimming (50 meters), running, and general physical assessment.
4. **Interview** — some years include a personal interview as part of the selection process.

**How competitive is it?** TS Chanakya has approximately 60-80 seats for B.Sc Nautical Science. With 30,000+ IMU CET takers, only the top 1-2% secure TS Chanakya seats. It's extremely competitive — but unlike JEE, the exam is more straightforward and the preparation is shorter.

**Important:** Verify the latest admission process at [dgshipping.gov.in](https://www.dgshipping.gov.in), as the process has been modified in recent years with the integration of TS Chanakya into the IMU framework.

## Curriculum & Structure

TS Chanakya is known for its rigorous, discipline-focused training:

**B.Sc Nautical Science (3 years):**
- Navigation (celestial navigation, electronic navigation, ECDIS, radar)
- Meteorology and oceanography
- Ship stability and construction
- Cargo handling (container, bulk, tanker operations)
- Maritime law and regulations
- Seamanship and ship handling

**Training approach:**
- **Regimented schedule** — wake-up at 0530, physical training, classes, practical sessions, study hours, lights out. This mirrors the disciplined schedule on merchant ships.
- **Mandatory sea training** — 18 months of cadetship on operational commercial ships. This is the most valuable part of the training — real-world experience at sea.
- **STCW certification** — fire fighting, sea survival, personal survival techniques, first aid, lifeboat handling. Internationally recognized certifications.
- **Uniform and discipline** — full uniform at all times on campus. Military-style discipline builds the professional conduct expected at sea.

**After graduation:**
- Complete 18 months of sea time as a Deck Cadet
- Pass DGMS competency examination
- Receive Certificate of Competency as 2nd Mate (3rd Officer)
- Begin career as a 3rd Officer on a commercial vessel

## Life at TS Chanakya

TS Chanakya is a fully residential institution with a culture unlike any other college in India.

- **Military-style discipline** — regimented daily schedule, uniform requirement, physical training every morning. This isn't a typical college experience — it's preparation for a professional life at sea.
- **Small batches** — with only 60-80 cadets per batch, the training is intimate and intensive. Faculty know every student personally.
- **Physical fitness emphasis** — daily PT, swimming, boat drills, and outdoor training. Your child will be physically fit by the time they graduate.
- **Strong camaraderie** — the shared intensity of TS Chanakya training creates lifelong bonds. Alumni meet at ports worldwide and look out for each other.
- **Navi Mumbai campus** — modern facilities including a ship simulator, navigation lab, engine room simulator, and swimming pool.

**Honest lifestyle note for parents:** TS Chanakya is demanding. The discipline is strict, the schedule is packed, and personal freedom is limited compared to a regular college. Your child should be prepared for this — it's intentional preparation for the structured, disciplined life at sea.
