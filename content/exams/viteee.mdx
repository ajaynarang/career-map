## What is VITEEE?

VITEEE is the entrance exam for VIT University — one of India's largest private engineering colleges. VIT Vellore's CSE program has excellent placements (average ₹8-12 LPA) and is a strong backup to IITs/NITs.

## How to Prepare

JEE preparation more than covers VITEEE. Practice VIT-specific papers 2 weeks before the exam. No negative marking means attempt everything.
