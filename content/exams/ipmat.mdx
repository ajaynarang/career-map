## What is IPMAT?

IPMAT is the entrance exam for IIM Indore's **5-year Integrated Programme in Management (IPM)** — one of the most prestigious undergraduate business programmes in India. IIM Indore started this in 2011, and IIM Rohtak, IIM Ranchi, IIM Bodh Gaya, and IIM Jammu now offer similar programmes.

**Why it matters for PCM students:** You can enter an IIM directly after Class 12 instead of waiting to crack CAT after graduation. The IPM gives you a BBA (3 years) + MBA (2 years) from an IIM in one shot. IIM Indore's IPM alumni earn ₹18-30 LPA in their first job.

Think of it as a shortcut to an IIM tag — no CAT required, no uncertainty of MBA admissions later.

## Scoring & What It Means

| Score Range | What it gets you |
|------------|-----------------|
| 240+ | IIM Indore IPM (top choice) |
| 200-240 | IIM Indore waitlist, IIM Rohtak confirmed |
| 160-200 | IIM Ranchi / IIM Bodh Gaya / IIM Jammu |
| Below 160 | Unlikely to convert any IIM IPM seat |

**Note:** After IPMAT, there's a Written Ability Test (WAT) and Personal Interview (PI) round. The final selection uses a composite score of IPMAT + WAT + PI + Class 10 + Class 12 marks.

## How to Prepare

**When to start:** Class 11 or early Class 12. The math in IPMAT is easier than JEE but trickier than board exams — it's aptitude-based.

**Strategy:**
- **Quantitative Ability (QA)** is 2/3 of the paper. PCM students have a massive advantage here. Focus on:
  - Number theory (HCF, LCM, divisibility)
  - Algebra (equations, inequalities, progressions)
  - Geometry and mensuration
  - Combinatorics and probability
- **Verbal Ability (VA)** is where most PCM students struggle. Start reading early:
  - Read 1 editorial daily (The Hindu, Indian Express)
  - Practice reading comprehension passages
  - Learn 10 new vocabulary words daily
- **Speed matters** — 60 questions in 120 minutes = 2 minutes per question. Practice timed mock tests.

**Resources:**
- Arun Sharma's "Quantitative Aptitude for CAT" (same question style)
- Word Power Made Easy by Norman Lewis (vocabulary)
- IIM Indore's official sample papers
- TIME/Career Launcher IPMAT test series
