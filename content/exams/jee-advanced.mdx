## What is JEE Advanced?

JEE Advanced is the **final gateway to the IITs** — the 23 Indian Institutes of Technology. It's considered one of the hardest undergraduate entrance exams in the world.

Only the top 2.5 lakh students who clear JEE Main are eligible to sit for JEE Advanced. Of those 2.5 lakh, about 50,000 will be offered seats across all IITs. Getting into IIT Bombay or IIT Delhi CS requires being in the top 100-200.

## Scoring & What It Means

| Rank Range | What it gets you |
|-----------|-----------------|
| Top 100 | IIT Bombay / IIT Delhi CS |
| Top 500 | CS at any IIT |
| Top 2,000 | Good branches (EE, ECE) at top 5 IITs |
| Top 5,000 | Any branch at top 5 IITs, CS at mid-tier IITs |
| Top 10,000 | Most branches at most IITs |

## How to Prepare

If your child is preparing for JEE Main seriously, they're already 80% prepared for Advanced. The key differences:

- **Depth over breadth:** Advanced questions test deeper understanding. Rotational mechanics, thermodynamics, electrochemistry get very tricky.
- **Multi-concept problems:** A single question might combine 2-3 topics.
- **Time pressure:** Two 3-hour papers in one day is exhausting. Practice full-length mock tests.

**Last 3 months strategy:**
1. Solve every JEE Advanced PYQ from 2010-2025
2. Take one full-length mock test every weekend
3. Focus on accuracy, not speed — negative marking is brutal
4. For Physics: Irodov problems. For Maths: Cengage series
