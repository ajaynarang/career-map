## What is TestDaF?

TestDaF is the German language proficiency test for international students who want to study at German universities in **German-taught programmes**. It's the German equivalent of TOEFL/IELTS but for the German language.

**Important context for Indian families:**
- Many German universities offer bachelor's programmes **only in German** (especially in engineering, natural sciences, and architecture)
- Some universities offer English-taught master's programmes, but bachelor's programmes in English are rare
- If your child wants to study engineering at TU Munich, RWTH Aachen, or KIT at the bachelor's level, they will likely need TestDaF
- **Exception:** A few universities accept DSH (Deutsche Sprachprüfung für den Hochschulzugang) instead — this is taken at the university itself after arriving in Germany

**Do you always need TestDaF?** No. If your child applies to an English-taught programme (becoming more common at master's level), IELTS/TOEFL is sufficient. TestDaF is only for German-taught programmes.

## Scoring & What It Means

TestDaF scores each section individually as TDN 3, 4, or 5:

| Level | What it means |
|-------|--------------|
| TDN 5 in all 4 sections | Full admission to any German university — no restrictions |
| TDN 4 in all 4 sections | Accepted by most universities — the standard requirement |
| TDN 3 in 1-2 sections | Some universities accept this; others require TDN 4 minimum in all sections |
| Below TDN 3 in any section | Not accepted — must retake |

**Most universities require TDN 4 in all 4 sections** (called "TDN 4x4"). This corresponds to roughly B2-C1 level German.

## How to Prepare

**When to start learning German:** At least 18-24 months before the test. Going from zero German to TestDaF level is a serious commitment.

**Timeline for a typical Indian student:**
| Stage | Duration | Level reached |
|-------|----------|--------------|
| A1 (Beginner) | 2-3 months | Basic greetings, simple sentences |
| A2 (Elementary) | 2-3 months | Daily conversations, simple texts |
| B1 (Intermediate) | 3-4 months | Can handle most situations, write emails |
| B2 (Upper Intermediate) | 3-4 months | Can understand complex texts, argue a point |
| TestDaF Prep | 2-3 months | Exam-specific strategies and practice |

**Total: 12-18 months of dedicated study (1-2 hours daily)**

**Where to learn German in India:**
- **Goethe-Institut** (Delhi, Mumbai, Bangalore, Chennai, Pune, Kolkata) — gold standard, ₹25,000-35,000 per level
- **Max Mueller Bhavan** — same as Goethe-Institut, different name
- **DAAD partnerships** with Indian universities — sometimes subsidized
- **Online options:** DeutschAkademie (free), Deutsche Welle (free), italki (paid tutors)

**TestDaF-specific preparation:**
- Take official TestDaF practice tests (available on testdaf.de)
- The **Writing section** is hardest for Indian students — practice writing structured German arguments
- The **Speaking section** is recorded (you speak into a microphone, not to a person) — practice speaking on academic topics alone
- Read German newspapers: Süddeutsche Zeitung, Die Zeit, Spiegel
