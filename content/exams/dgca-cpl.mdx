## What is the DGCA CPL?

The DGCA CPL (Commercial Pilot License) is not a single exam — it's a **series of 5 ground subject exams** that every aspiring commercial pilot in India must pass, along with accumulating **200 hours of flight time**, to earn a Commercial Pilot License from India's Directorate General of Civil Aviation.

**The journey to becoming a commercial pilot in India:**
1. **Class 12 with Physics + Maths** — your eligibility
2. **DGCA Class 2 Medical** — medical fitness certificate
3. **Student Pilot License (SPL)** — issued by DGCA after enrolling at a Flying Training Organization
4. **Flight Training** — 200 hours of flying at a DGCA-approved FTO (takes 18-24 months)
5. **5 DGCA Ground Subject Exams** — pass all 5 with 70%+ each
6. **CPL Skill Test** — flight test with a DGCA examiner
7. **CPL Issued** — you can now fly commercial aircraft

**Total cost:** ₹40-80 lakh depending on the flight school. This is an expensive career path, but airline captains earn ₹70 LPA-1 Cr+ in India.

## Scoring & What It Means

| Paper | What it covers |
|-------|---------------|
| Air Navigation | Flight planning, charts, dead reckoning, GPS navigation |
| Aviation Meteorology | Weather patterns, cloud types, turbulence, reading weather reports |
| Air Regulations | DGCA/ICAO rules, airspace classifications, pilot responsibilities |
| Technical General | How aircraft systems work — engines, hydraulics, instruments |
| Technical Specific | Specific to single-engine or multi-engine aircraft |

**Pass mark:** 70% in each paper. You can retake failed papers individually. No aggregate score — each paper must be cleared separately.

**Validity:** Once you pass a paper, the result is valid for 5 years. Complete all 5 papers + flight hours within this window.

## How to Prepare

**When to start:** After Class 12, enrol at a DGCA-approved Flying Training Organization (FTO). Ground classes are part of the FTO curriculum.

**Flight Training Options in India:**
| Institute | Location | Approximate Cost |
|-----------|----------|-----------------|
| Indira Gandhi Rashtriya Uran Akademi (IGRUA) | Amethi, UP | ₹40-45 lakh (government subsidized) |
| Rajiv Gandhi Academy for Aviation Technology | Thiruvananthapuram | ₹35-40 lakh |
| Bombay Flying Club | Mumbai | ₹50-60 lakh |
| National Flying Training Institute (NFTI) | Gondia | ₹45-55 lakh |
| Private FTOs (EPI, CAE, etc.) | Various | ₹55-80 lakh |

**Training abroad (cheaper option):** Many Indian students train in the USA, Philippines, or South Africa where flight time is cheaper. USA CPL costs $40-50K (₹35-42 lakh) but you must convert to an Indian CPL by passing DGCA exams after returning.

**Ground Exam Strategy:**
- Study DGCA-prescribed textbooks (Oxford Aviation Academy series is the standard)
- Use question banks from previous DGCA exams (available through FTOs)
- Air Navigation and Meteorology are the hardest papers — start these early
- Air Regulations is the easiest — mostly memorization of rules
