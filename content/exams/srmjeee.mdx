## What is SRMJEEE?

SRMJEEE (SRM Joint Engineering Entrance Examination) is the entrance exam for **SRM Institute of Science and Technology** — one of South India's largest and most placement-focused private engineering colleges. SRM has campuses in Chennai (Kattankulathur), Amaravati, and NCR, but the main campus in Chennai is where you want to be.

SRMJEEE is one of the easier engineering entrance exams — significantly simpler than JEE Main. For students who want a guaranteed good college with strong CS placements without the extreme pressure of JEE, SRM is a smart backup (or primary) option.

## Scoring & What It Means

| Score Range | What it gets you |
|------------|-----------------|
| 100+ | CS/IT at SRM Kattankulathur (main campus) — scholarship likely |
| 80-100 | CS/IT at main campus, good branch options |
| 60-80 | ECE, EEE at main campus, or CS at other campuses |
| 40-60 | Core branches at main campus, most branches at other campuses |

**Key advantage:** No negative marking. Attempt every question. Even educated guesses can boost your score significantly compared to exams with negative marking.

## How to Prepare

**When to start:** 2-3 months before the exam is sufficient if your child has covered Class 11-12 NCERT.

**Strategy:**
- **NCERT is enough** — SRMJEEE is NCERT-level. No need for advanced coaching material
- **Focus on Maths** — 40 questions, most scoring section
- **English and Aptitude** are free marks — basic grammar and logical reasoning
- **No negative marking** means attempt everything — never leave a question blank
- **Practice with SRM's own sample papers** available on their website

**Coaching:** Not required. If your child is preparing for JEE Main, SRMJEEE requires zero additional preparation. If they're not doing JEE coaching, NCERT + sample papers is sufficient.
