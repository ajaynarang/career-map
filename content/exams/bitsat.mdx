## What is BITSAT?

BITSAT is the entrance exam for BITS Pilani — one of India's top private engineering colleges with campuses in Pilani, Goa, and Hyderabad. BITS is famous for its unique system: no fixed branches until semester 2, and you can change your branch based on CGPA.

## Scoring & What It Means

| Score | What it gets you |
|-------|-----------------|
| 370+ | Pilani campus CS |
| 340+ | Goa/Hyderabad CS |
| 310+ | EEE/ECE at any campus |
| 280+ | Other engineering branches |

**Speed is everything** — you have about 1.4 minutes per question. The exam rewards speed over depth.

## How to Prepare

If you're preparing for JEE, you're 90% ready for BITSAT. Add:
- **English practice** — 2-3 weeks of grammar and vocabulary
- **Logical reasoning** — basic puzzles, 1 week of practice
- **Speed drills** — practice completing mock tests in under 2.5 hours
