## What is CUET?

CUET (Common University Entrance Test) is India's single-window entrance exam for admission to **all central universities** — Delhi University (DU), JNU, BHU, Jamia Millia Islamia, Hyderabad Central University, and 40+ others. Since 2022, Class 12 board marks alone don't get you into these universities — CUET scores are mandatory.

For PCM students interested in **finance, business, economics, or integrated programmes**, CUET is the key exam. It opens doors to top programmes like:
- **BSc Economics (Hons) at Delhi University** — a feeder to top MBA programmes and investment banking
- **BA Economics at JNU** — one of India's best economics programmes
- **BBA/Integrated BBA-MBA programmes** at central universities
- **BSc programmes** at DU, BHU, and other central universities

## Scoring & What It Means

CUET uses percentile scoring (normalized across sessions). Here's what matters:

| Percentile | What it gets you |
|-----------|-----------------|
| 99+ | DU top colleges (SRCC, Hindu, Stephens) — Economics, Commerce |
| 97-99 | Good DU colleges, BHU, JNU top programmes |
| 93-97 | Mid-tier DU colleges, most central university programmes |
| 85-93 | Lower-tier central universities, general programmes |

**Important:** Each university sets its own merit list using CUET scores. DU uses a formula combining domain subjects and language scores. Check individual university admission policies.

## How to Prepare

**When to start:** Class 12, October-November (6 months before exam). CUET is NCERT-based, so students with strong school fundamentals have a natural advantage.

**Strategy:**
- **NCERT is everything** — 95% of CUET questions are directly from NCERT or test NCERT concepts
- **Choose domain subjects wisely** — pick subjects where you can score highest (PCM students should consider Maths, Physics, Economics, Business Studies)
- **General Test** is easy marks — practice basic aptitude, current affairs, and logical reasoning
- **Language section** requires decent reading comprehension — practice passage-based questions

**Resources:**
- NCERT textbooks (Class 12, read every line)
- NTA official CUET mock tests (free on cuet.nta.nic.in)
- Previous year CUET papers (2022-2025)
- Any standard GK/current affairs book for the General Test

**Coaching:** Most students self-prepare. CUET is significantly easier than JEE or NEET. If your child is already preparing for JEE, CUET requires minimal additional effort.
