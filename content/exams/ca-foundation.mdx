## What is CA Foundation?

CA Foundation is the first step in becoming a **Chartered Accountant (CA)** — one of India's most respected and highest-paying professional qualifications. The CA programme is administered by ICAI (Institute of Chartered Accountants of India), the world's second-largest accounting body.

The full CA journey has 3 levels:
1. **CA Foundation** (after Class 12) — 4 papers
2. **CA Intermediate** (after Foundation) — 6 papers in 2 groups
3. **CA Final** (after Intermediate + 2.5 years articleship) — 6 papers in 2 groups

Total journey: 4.5 to 5 years after Class 12.

**Why PCM students should consider CA:** Paper 3 (Quantitative Aptitude) is where PCM students dominate. Your child's math foundation gives them a 20-30 mark advantage over commerce students in this paper. Many PCM students clear CA Foundation in the first attempt while commerce students struggle.

## Scoring & What It Means

| Result | What it means |
|--------|--------------|
| 50%+ aggregate + 40% in each paper | PASS — proceed to CA Intermediate |
| Top 50 All India Rank | Merit list recognition, prestige |
| Below 40% in any paper | FAIL — must re-appear in failed papers |

**Pass rates:** CA Foundation has a 30-40% pass rate. It's not easy, but PCM students typically have higher pass rates due to strong analytical skills.

**Key insight:** CA Foundation is the easiest level. CA Intermediate (25-30% pass rate) and CA Final (10-15% pass rate) are where the real challenge lies. If your child clears Foundation easily, it's a good sign.

## How to Prepare

**When to start:** Register with ICAI right after Class 10 (provisional registration). Start serious study 4-6 months before the exam (January for June session).

**Strategy for PCM students:**
- **Paper 3 (Quant)** — Your strength. Business maths and statistics are easy for PCM students. Target 80+ here.
- **Paper 1 (Accounting)** — This is new territory. Learn double-entry bookkeeping from scratch. Practice journal entries, ledgers, and trial balance. ICAI study material is sufficient.
- **Paper 2 (Business Laws)** — Pure memorization. Read, revise, repeat. Make short notes.
- **Paper 4 (Business Economics)** — Understanding-based. Read ICAI material, understand graphs and concepts.

**Resources:**
- ICAI official study material (mandatory, covers everything)
- ICAI practice papers and RTPs (Revision Test Papers)
- CA coaching: **Unacademy CA** (online), **Aldine CA** (Delhi), **VSI Jaipur** (residential)
