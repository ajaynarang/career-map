## What is the SAT?

The SAT is the standardized test accepted by virtually all US universities and many universities in Canada, UK, Singapore, and other countries. Run by the College Board, it's the most common test Indian students take when applying to American universities.

**Key change (2024 onwards):** The SAT is now fully **digital and adaptive**. It's taken on a laptop/tablet using the College Board's Bluebook app. The test adapts to your performance — if you do well on Module 1, Module 2 is harder (but scores higher). Total time is just 2 hours 14 minutes (down from 3+ hours in the old paper format).

**For Indian parents:** Think of the SAT as the "JEE of the USA" — but much easier, especially the Math section. A PCM student who is preparing for JEE will find SAT Math trivially easy. The challenge is the Reading & Writing section, which tests English at a level most Indian students are not used to.

## Scoring & What It Means

| Score Range | What it gets you |
|------------|-----------------|
| 1550-1600 | MIT, Stanford, Harvard, Caltech (competitive, not guaranteed) |
| 1500-1550 | Top 20 US universities — very strong application |
| 1450-1500 | Top 30-50 universities — competitive |
| 1400-1450 | Top 50-80 universities — solid score |
| 1350-1400 | Good state universities (Purdue, Penn State, etc.) |
| Below 1300 | Consider retaking before applying to competitive schools |

**Important:** US admissions are holistic. SAT is just one factor. Extracurriculars, essays, recommendations, and GPA matter equally. A 1600 SAT does NOT guarantee admission to Harvard. A 1450 with exceptional extracurriculars can get into MIT.

**Superscoring:** Many universities take your best Reading & Writing score and best Math score across all attempts. So if you score 750 RW + 700 Math in attempt 1, and 700 RW + 790 Math in attempt 2, your superscore is 750 + 790 = 1540.

## How to Prepare

**When to start:** Class 11, summer. Take the first attempt by October of Class 12 (for early applications) or December of Class 12 (for regular applications).

**Math Section (PCM students' advantage):**
- SAT Math covers algebra, geometry, and basic trigonometry — all significantly easier than JEE
- A JEE-prepared student should score 750+ with just 2-3 weeks of SAT-specific practice
- **Key tip:** Learn to use the Desmos graphing calculator built into the digital SAT. It can solve many problems instantly.
- Practice: Khan Academy SAT Prep (free, official partnership with College Board)

**Reading & Writing Section (the real challenge):**
- This is where Indian students typically lose 100-200 marks
- **Build reading habit early:** Read The New Yorker, The Atlantic, Scientific American, and classic fiction
- **Vocabulary in context:** SAT no longer tests obscure words — it tests how common words are used in different contexts
- **Grammar rules:** Learn the core 15-20 grammar rules tested on SAT (subject-verb agreement, comma usage, parallel structure, etc.)
- Practice: College Board's official practice tests (8+ available free on Bluebook app)

**Timeline:**
1. **6 months before:** Start Khan Academy SAT prep, read daily
2. **3 months before:** Take first full practice test to benchmark
3. **2 months before:** Daily practice — 30 min Reading, 20 min Math, review mistakes
4. **1 month before:** Take a timed practice test every weekend
5. **1 week before:** Light review, rest, download Bluebook app and familiarize yourself

**Resources:**
- [Khan Academy SAT Prep](https://www.khanacademy.org/sat) — free, official, and the best resource
- College Board Bluebook app — official practice tests
- "The College Panda" books — SAT Math and SAT Writing
