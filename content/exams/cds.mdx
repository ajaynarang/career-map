## What is the CDS Exam?

CDS (Combined Defence Services) is a UPSC-conducted exam for **graduates** who want to join the Indian Armed Forces as commissioned officers. While NDA is for Class 12 students, CDS is for those who have completed (or are completing) a bachelor's degree.

CDS leads to admission in:
- **IMA (Indian Military Academy)** — Dehradun, for Army officers
- **INA (Indian Naval Academy)** — Ezhimala, for Navy officers
- **AFA (Air Force Academy)** — Dundigal (Hyderabad), for Air Force officers
- **OTA (Officers Training Academy)** — Chennai, for Short Service Commission

**Why include CDS here:** Many students plan their defence career early. If your child misses NDA (age limit, didn't clear SSB, etc.), CDS is the backup route — take any graduation degree (BSc/B.Tech/BA) and appear for CDS after graduation.

## Scoring & What It Means

| Stage | Details |
|-------|---------|
| Written Exam | Cutoff typically 100-120 out of 300 (for IMA/INA/AFA) |
| SSB Interview | 5-day assessment, same as NDA SSB |
| Final Merit | Written + SSB combined merit list |

**CDS written exam is easier than NDA** — the math is more basic, and the competition is different. But the SSB interview is equally rigorous.

## How to Prepare

**When to start:** During graduation (final year students are eligible to appear). But awareness from Class 10 helps in planning the right graduation path.

**Written Exam Strategy:**
- **English (100 marks)** — Focus on comprehension passages, sentence improvement, error spotting, vocabulary. Read newspapers daily (The Hindu/Indian Express).
- **General Knowledge (100 marks)** — Current affairs (last 6 months), Indian history, geography, polity, economics, basic science. Read Lucent's GK + monthly current affairs.
- **Mathematics (100 marks)** — Class 10-12 level math. PCM students find this very easy. Focus on accuracy and speed.

**SSB Interview:** Same as NDA — 5 days of psychological testing, group tasks, and personal interviews.
