## What is NEST?

NEST (National Entrance Screening Test) is the entrance exam for two of India's most prestigious science institutions:

1. **NISER Bhubaneswar** (National Institute of Science Education and Research) — a DAE-funded institute known for physics and mathematics
2. **CEBS Mumbai** (Centre for Excellence in Basic Sciences) — a DAE-University of Mumbai joint initiative

Both offer a 5-year Integrated MSc programme (similar to IISERs). Graduates regularly get PhD admits at top global universities.

NEST is less competitive than JEE but the quality of education at NISER and CEBS is exceptional for students passionate about fundamental science.

## Scoring & What It Means

| Score Range | What it gets you |
|------------|-----------------|
| 140+ | NISER — any subject of choice |
| 110-140 | NISER — most subjects, CEBS — any subject |
| 80-110 | CEBS — most subjects |
| 60-80 | Waitlist territory |

**Key advantage:** NEST allows you to choose 3 out of 4 science subjects. PCM students can skip Biology entirely, unlike the IISER IAT where Biology is mandatory.

## How to Prepare

**When to start:** Class 11, alongside JEE or IISER preparation. NEST has significant overlap with JEE and IAT syllabi.

**Strategy:**
- **Section 1 (General)** is unique to NEST — it tests general scientific awareness, history of science, logical reasoning. Read science magazines and stay curious.
- **Choose your 3 strongest subjects** — PCM students typically skip Biology
- **NEST questions are conceptual** but less tricky than JEE Advanced. Strong NCERT understanding + problem-solving practice is sufficient.
- **Previous year papers** are the best resource — patterns repeat

**Resources:**
- NCERT textbooks (foundation)
- Previous year NEST papers (2013-2025, available on nestexam.in)
- HC Verma for Physics, Cengage for Maths
- Science magazines for General section (Resonance, Current Science)
