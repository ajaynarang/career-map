## What is the NDA Exam?

NDA (National Defence Academy) exam is conducted by UPSC to select candidates for the Indian Army, Navy, and Air Force. Clearing the NDA exam gets you into the **National Defence Academy in Khadakwasla, Pune** — a 3-year training programme after which you are commissioned as an officer in the armed forces.

**This is the earliest entry point into the Indian military** — you can appear while still in Class 12. It's the most prestigious route to becoming a defence officer.

After the written exam, shortlisted candidates go through the **SSB Interview** (Services Selection Board) — a 5-day assessment of personality, leadership, and officer-like qualities. Only about 2-3% of applicants finally get selected.

**For PCM students:** You're eligible for all three services — Army, Navy, and Air Force. Arts and commerce students can only apply for the Army wing.

## Scoring & What It Means

| Stage | Details |
|-------|---------|
| Written Exam | Cutoff typically 340-370 out of 900 (varies by year) |
| SSB Interview | 900 marks (Psychological tests, Group Tasks, Personal Interview) |
| Final Merit | Written (900) + SSB (900) = Total 1800. Merit list drawn from top scorers. |

**Key insight:** The written exam cutoff is not very high — about 40%. But the SSB interview eliminates 90%+ of the candidates who clear the written. SSB tests your personality, not academics.

## How to Prepare

**When to start:** Class 11. Apply in January of Class 12 for NDA I (exam in April).

**Written Exam Strategy:**
- **Mathematics (300 marks)** — PCM students' biggest advantage. Focus on:
  - Algebra, Matrices, Determinants (high weightage)
  - Trigonometry and Analytical Geometry
  - Calculus (basics only, not as tough as JEE)
  - Vector Algebra and Statistics
- **General Ability (600 marks):**
  - **English (200 marks)** — Grammar, vocabulary, comprehension. Practice Wren & Martin.
  - **General Knowledge (400 marks)** — Physics, Chemistry, General Science, History, Geography, Current Affairs. Read NCERT (Class 6-12 for History/Geography), and a monthly current affairs magazine.

**SSB Interview Preparation:**
- Start developing personality traits early — leadership, communication, social skills
- Join NCC (National Cadet Corps) if available in your school
- Read "Let's Crack SSB Interview" by Dr. N.K. Natarajan
- Practice group discussions and extempore speaking

**Resources:**
- Pathfinder for NDA/NA by Arihant
- NCERT textbooks (History, Geography, Science)
- Manohar Pandey's General Knowledge
- SSB prep: SSBCrack.com, MKC (Major Kalshi Classes)
