## What is UCEED?

UCEED (Undergraduate Common Entrance Exam for Design) is the entrance exam for India's most prestigious **B.Des (Bachelor of Design)** programmes at:

- **IIT Bombay** (IDC School of Design) — India's #1 design school
- **IIT Delhi** (Department of Design)
- **IIT Guwahati** (Department of Design)
- **IIITDM Jabalpur** (Design programme)

UCEED is to design what JEE Advanced is to engineering — the hardest and most competitive design entrance exam in India.

**Why PCM students should consider design:** Design is no longer just about art. Product design, UX/UI, interaction design, and industrial design require strong analytical and spatial thinking — skills PCM students naturally have. IIT Bombay IDC graduates earn ₹15-30 LPA and work at Apple, Google, Microsoft, and top design consultancies.

## Scoring & What It Means

| Score/Rank | What it gets you |
|-----------|-----------------|
| Top 50 | IIT Bombay B.Des (most coveted) |
| Top 150 | IIT Delhi or IIT Guwahati B.Des |
| Top 300 | IIITDM Jabalpur Design |
| Below top 300 | May not convert any seat through UCEED |

**Total seats are very limited** — IIT Bombay has only ~30 B.Des seats, IIT Delhi about 30, IIT Guwahati about 30. Competition is intense but the applicant pool is much smaller than JEE.

## How to Prepare

**When to start:** At least 6-8 months before the exam (June-July of Class 12).

**Strategy:**
- **Visual & Spatial Ability** — Practice 2D-to-3D conversion, mirror images, paper folding, spatial visualization. This is where PCM students shine.
- **Observation & Design Sensitivity** — Develop an eye for everyday design. Notice fonts, product shapes, packaging, architecture around you.
- **Drawing Section (Part C)** — You need to draw. Practice:
  - Object sketching
  - Storyboarding
  - Creating visual narratives
- **Analytical Reasoning** — Similar to logical reasoning in aptitude tests. Practice pattern recognition and data interpretation.
- **Design Awareness** — Study famous designs, designers (Dieter Rams, Charles Eames), and design movements. Follow design blogs.

**Resources:**
- UCEED official website for past year papers and sample questions
- "Design Drawing" by Francis Ching
- Follow design platforms: Behance, Dribbble, Dezeen
- Coaching: CREATIVEly (Mumbai), Silica (Pune), DesignGuru — UCEED-specific prep
