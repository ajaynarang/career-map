## What is the IISER Aptitude Test?

The IISER Aptitude Test (IAT) is the entrance exam for India's 7 Indian Institutes of Science Education and Research — IISER Pune, Kolkata, Mohali, Bhopal, Thiruvananthapuram, Tirupati, and Berhampur. These are **elite research institutions** funded by the Ministry of Education, often called "the IITs of science."

IISERs offer a 5-year integrated BS-MS programme. Graduates are among the most sought-after for PhD positions at top global universities (MIT, Stanford, Cambridge). If your child loves fundamental science and wants a research career, IISERs are the best option in India.

There are **3 admission channels** to IISERs — KVPY (now discontinued, replaced by INSPIRE), JEE Advanced cutoff, and the **IISER Aptitude Test**. The IAT is the primary route for most students.

## Scoring & What It Means

| Score Range | What it gets you |
|------------|-----------------|
| 180+ | IISER Pune / Kolkata (top 2 IISERs) |
| 150-180 | IISER Mohali / Bhopal |
| 120-150 | IISER Thiruvananthapuram / Tirupati |
| 100-120 | IISER Berhampur |

**Important:** Unlike JEE, the IAT is not about speed — it's about deep conceptual understanding. Many JEE toppers struggle with IAT because the questions test scientific reasoning, not pattern recognition.

## How to Prepare

**When to start:** Serious preparation from Class 11. The IAT tests NCERT concepts deeply, so build a strong foundation.

**Strategy:**
- **Master NCERT thoroughly** — IAT questions are rooted in NCERT but require deeper thinking
- **Focus on conceptual problems** — "why" matters more than "how" for IAT
- **Biology is compulsory** — even PCM students must study Class 11-12 Biology. This is the section where most PCM students lose marks. Start early.
- **Practice previous year IAT papers** (available on the IISER admission portal)

**Resources:**
- NCERT textbooks (read them like a novel, not a formula sheet)
- HC Verma for Physics concepts
- Previous year IAT papers (2018-2025)
- IISER-specific coaching is rare — most students self-prepare alongside JEE coaching
