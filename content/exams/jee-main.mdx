## What is JEE Main?

JEE Main (Joint Entrance Examination Main) is the gateway exam to India's top engineering colleges — NITs (National Institutes of Technology), IIITs (Indian Institutes of Information Technology), and other centrally funded institutions. It's also the **qualifying exam** for JEE Advanced, which is the only way to get into IITs.

Think of it this way: JEE Main opens the door to 31 NITs and 26 IIITs. JEE Advanced (which requires clearing JEE Main first) opens the door to 23 IITs.

## Scoring & What Your Child's Score Means

JEE Main uses percentile scoring (not raw marks). Here's what the percentiles translate to:

| Percentile | What it gets you |
|-----------|-----------------|
| 99.8+ | NIT Trichy / NIT Surathkal CS |
| 99.5+ | Top NITs — CS at any top 5 NIT |
| 99.0+ | Good NITs — CS/IT at top 10 NITs |
| 98.0+ | Mid-tier NITs — decent branches |
| 95.0+ | Lower NITs or good branches at state colleges |

**Key advantage:** Your child gets **two attempts per year** (January + April). Colleges take the **best score**. Strategy: treat January as a serious practice run, go all-in for April.

## How to Prepare

**When to start:** Class 11, day one. This is non-negotiable.

**Daily commitment:** 4-6 hours alongside school (including coaching).

**Coaching options:**
- **Allen** (Kota) — largest, most systematic
- **FIITJEE** — strong in Delhi/NCR
- **Physics Wallah (PW)** — excellent online option, much cheaper (₹5-15K vs ₹1-3L)
- **Unacademy** — good for supplement

**Subject-wise strategy:**
- **Mathematics** (most scoring): Master coordinate geometry, calculus, and algebra. These 3 topics = 60% of math questions.
- **Physics** (most conceptual): Mechanics and electrostatics are heavily tested. HC Verma + Irodov for practice.
- **Chemistry** (most memory): Organic chemistry and periodic table trends. NCERT is the Bible for inorganic.

**Practice is everything:** Solve previous year papers (PYQs) from 2019-2025. Patterns repeat. NTA loves to recycle question types.
