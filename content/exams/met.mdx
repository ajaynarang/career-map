## What is MET?

MET (Manipal Entrance Test) is the entrance exam for **Manipal Institute of Technology (MIT), Manipal** — one of India's oldest and most respected private engineering colleges, established in 1957. MIT Manipal is in Karnataka's coastal town of Manipal, and it has a legacy that predates most private engineering colleges in India.

MET is a moderate-difficulty exam — harder than SRMJEEE but easier than JEE Main. For students looking for a well-rounded college experience with strong industry connections and a beautiful campus, Manipal is an excellent choice.

## Scoring & What It Means

| Score Range | What it gets you |
|------------|-----------------|
| 170+ | CS/IT at MIT Manipal — likely with scholarship |
| 140-170 | CS/IT, Data Science, or ECE at MIT Manipal |
| 110-140 | Good branches at MIT Manipal — Mechanical, EEE, Civil |
| 80-110 | Most branches at MIT Manipal or Manipal Jaipur campus |

**Key advantage:** MET has no negative marking and offers multiple exam slots. Your child can choose a comfortable date and time. The flexibility reduces exam-day pressure significantly.

## How to Prepare

**When to start:** 2-3 months before the exam. If your child is doing JEE prep, MET requires no additional effort.

**Strategy:**
- **Maths has the most questions (70)** — focus here for maximum impact
- **NCERT-level concepts** — MET doesn't test JEE Advanced-level problems
- **No negative marking** — attempt every single question
- **English & General Aptitude section** is easy marks — basic vocabulary, reading comprehension, and logical reasoning
- **Practice with MET sample papers** from Manipal's website

**Resources:**
- NCERT textbooks
- Manipal official sample papers and mock tests
- Any standard Class 11-12 reference books
