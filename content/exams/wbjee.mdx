## What is WBJEE?

WBJEE (West Bengal Joint Entrance Examination) is the state-level engineering entrance exam for West Bengal. It's the gateway to **Jadavpur University** — one of India's most respected public engineering colleges — along with other top Bengal institutions like IIEST Shibpur (formerly BE College), Kalyani Government Engineering College, and numerous private colleges.

For students in Eastern India, WBJEE is as important as JEE Main. Jadavpur University, which admits primarily through WBJEE, is consistently ranked among India's top 10-15 engineering colleges by NIRF and produces alumni who rival IIT graduates in their careers.

## Scoring & What It Means

| Rank Range | What it gets you |
|-----------|-----------------|
| Top 200 | Jadavpur University CS/IT |
| 200-1000 | JU ECE, EE, Mechanical, or IIEST Shibpur CS |
| 1000-3000 | JU Civil/Chemical, IIEST good branches |
| 3000-10000 | Good government colleges across WB |

**Key advantage:** WBJEE is significantly less competitive than JEE Main nationally. About 1 lakh students appear, compared to 10+ lakh for JEE Main. A student who might get a 90 percentile in JEE Main can potentially crack top 500 in WBJEE.

## How to Prepare

**When to start:** Class 11, alongside JEE preparation. WBJEE syllabus is a subset of JEE Main.

**Strategy:**
- **If preparing for JEE Main, you're already prepared** — WBJEE is easier in difficulty
- **Maths carries 50% weightage** — master it. Algebra, calculus, and coordinate geometry dominate
- **Negative marking is mild** — only -0.25 for Category I questions, so strategic guessing can work
- **WBJEE has unique question types** — Category I (1 mark, -0.25), Category II (2 marks, -0.5), Category III (2 marks, no negative) — know the pattern
- **Previous year papers are gold** — WBJEE recycles concepts and question styles

**Resources:**
- NCERT textbooks + any JEE Main material
- Previous year WBJEE papers (2015-2025)
- Arihant or MTG WBJEE-specific books
