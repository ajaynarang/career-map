## What is IMU CET?

IMU CET is the entrance exam for the **Indian Maritime University (IMU)** and its affiliated maritime colleges. It's the gateway to a career in the Merchant Navy — one of the highest-paying careers available to PCM students.

IMU CET gets you into programmes like:
- **B.Tech in Marine Engineering** (4 years) — become a Marine Engineer on ships
- **B.Sc in Nautical Science** (3 years) — become a Deck Officer (and eventually, a Ship Captain)
- **DNS (Diploma in Nautical Science)** (1 year) — fastest route to sea

**Why Merchant Navy:** Officers earn ₹12-40 LPA (tax-free if at sea 182+ days). Captains and Chief Engineers earn ₹60 LPA-1.2 Cr. You travel the world, have zero living expenses on ship, and get 4-6 months of leave per year.

## Scoring & What It Means

| Score Range | What it gets you |
|------------|-----------------|
| 160+ | IMU campuses (Chennai, Mumbai, Kolkata, Vizag) — top choice |
| 130-160 | Good private maritime colleges (HIMT, Tolani, MASSA) |
| 100-130 | Mid-tier maritime colleges |
| Below 100 | May not get a good college — consider retaking |

**Note:** Some premier institutes like T.S. Chanakya (Mumbai) and T.S. Chandragupt (previously MERI) have separate admission processes through government sponsorship. Check the Directorate General of Shipping website.

## How to Prepare

**When to start:** Class 12, January-February (3-4 months before exam). IMU CET is much easier than JEE.

**Strategy:**
- **Mathematics (50Q)** — Highest weightage. Class 11-12 math, focus on calculus, trigonometry, and coordinate geometry. If you're preparing for JEE, this is very easy.
- **Physics & Chemistry (50Q combined)** — NCERT level. Direct application questions.
- **General Aptitude (50Q)** — Logical reasoning, quantitative aptitude, analytical skills. Practice from any standard aptitude book.
- **English (25Q)** — Reading comprehension, grammar, vocabulary. Read newspapers.
- **General Knowledge (25Q)** — Focus on maritime-related GK: oceans, ports, shipping companies, geography.

**Resources:**
- NCERT textbooks for PCM
- Previous year IMU CET papers (available online)
- RS Aggarwal for Aptitude
- Maritime-specific GK: read about India's major ports, shipping routes, types of ships

**Physical Fitness:** Merchant Navy has strict medical standards — 6/6 vision (correctable), no colour blindness, good physical fitness. Ensure your child meets these before committing to this path.
