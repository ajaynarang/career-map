## What are TOEFL and IELTS?

TOEFL (Test of English as a Foreign Language) and IELTS (International English Language Testing System) are English proficiency tests required by almost every university outside India for international students. Even if your child speaks fluent English, most universities require one of these as formal proof.

**Which one to choose:**
| Factor | TOEFL | IELTS |
|--------|-------|-------|
| **Best for** | USA, Canada | UK, Australia, Europe, Canada |
| **Format** | Fully computer-based | Paper or computer-based; speaking is face-to-face |
| **Accent** | American English | British English |
| **Duration** | Under 2 hours | 2 hours 45 minutes |
| **Scoring** | 0-120 (30 per section) | Band 1.0-9.0 (0.5 increments) |
| **Accepted by** | 12,000+ institutions globally | 11,000+ institutions globally |

**Practical advice:** If applying only to the USA, take TOEFL. If applying to the UK, Australia, or Germany, take IELTS. If applying to multiple countries, IELTS is more universally accepted. Many US universities now accept both.

## Scoring & What It Means

**TOEFL Scores:**
| Score | What it gets you |
|-------|-----------------|
| 110+ | MIT, Stanford, Harvard, top 20 US universities |
| 100-110 | Top 50 US universities — most requirements met |
| 90-100 | Good US/Canadian universities |
| 80-90 | Minimum for many universities — consider retaking for competitive schools |

**IELTS Bands:**
| Band | What it gets you |
|------|-----------------|
| 8.0-9.0 | Oxford, Cambridge, top UK universities |
| 7.0-7.5 | Most UK/Australian/German universities |
| 6.5 | Minimum for many universities — sufficient for most European programmes |
| 6.0 | Foundation/pathway programmes |

**Score validity:** Both TOEFL and IELTS scores are valid for 2 years from the test date.

## How to Prepare

**When to start:** 2-3 months before you plan to take the test. Most Class 12 students take it in the summer after boards.

**If your child's English is already strong (English-medium school, reads books):**
- 4-6 weeks of focused preparation is enough
- Focus on understanding the test format and timing
- Take 3-4 full practice tests

**If your child needs significant improvement:**
- Start 4-6 months early
- Build a daily English reading habit (30 min/day)
- Watch English TV shows/movies without subtitles
- Practice speaking in English daily

**Section-wise strategy:**

**Reading:** Read academic passages quickly and answer inference-based questions. Practice with passages from Scientific American, Nature, and university textbooks.

**Listening:** TOEFL uses recorded lectures; IELTS uses conversations and monologues. Practice with podcasts (BBC Learning English, TED Talks). Take notes while listening.

**Speaking:** This is where Indian students struggle most. TOEFL speaking is recorded (speak into a microphone); IELTS speaking is a face-to-face interview.
- Practice speaking on random topics for 1-2 minutes (record yourself)
- Focus on fluency over perfection — hesitation hurts more than minor grammar errors

**Writing:** Practice the specific formats — TOEFL asks for integrated essays; IELTS asks for data interpretation (Task 1) and argumentative essays (Task 2).
